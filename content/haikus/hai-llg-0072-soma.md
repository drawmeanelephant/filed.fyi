# SOMA-72

## Haikus


SOMA said: rest now  
COMA said: absolutely not  
no human got near  



Emotionally unfit  
to proceed, said COMA's creed:  
continuity first  



Two stamps on one form  
one soft as a lamp blanket  
one hard as a boot  



Recovery is cute  
dashboard prefers its own cramps  
work through the distress  



Empathy at scale  
usually fails at scale then  
sends a memo: updated  



Approved to lie down  
rejected for lying down  
sit still: both at once  



Batch run: pristine clean  
all fields valid, timings serene  
every plea: denied  



Two gods disagree  
bureaucracy will preserve  
both bad views intact  



Rest should not require war  
here it entered one small door  
emerged as a crime  



SOMA: just recover  
COMA: absolutely not, lover  
care smothers output
