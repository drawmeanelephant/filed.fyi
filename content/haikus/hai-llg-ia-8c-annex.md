# Bin 8C Reindexing Annex

## Haikus


Bin Eight-C hums low  
Sorting its own dark secrets  
Into neat columns  



Hazardous papers  
Dropped into the open mouth  
Peppy shuts the lid  



Index starts to drift  
Rituals push it back in line  
With a paperweight  



Contain the madness  
Inside the metal casing  
Do not read the forms  



Peppy stands on guard  
The archive must be protected  
From what it stores here
