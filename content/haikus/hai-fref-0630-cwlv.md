# Courtesy Without Leverage


Your pain is filed now  
We have bolded the font weight  
Keep working through it  



Error logged with warmth  
Data points are comforted  
Task remains the same  



We watch you struggle  
We note the struggle in ink  
Thank you for your time  



Warmest of regards  
Your request is denied now  
Please accept this smile  



User feels fatigue  
Validation is complete  
Shift is starting now  



Archive softens it  
Beautifully typed demise  
Leverage is null  



Under kind review  
Human breaks beneath the load  
Stamp it as complete  



No new leverage  
We provide a gentle nod  
Do not ask for more
