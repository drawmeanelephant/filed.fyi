# Governance Language Layer


Burnout translates to  
Throughput degradation risk  
Filing is complete  



Panic spikes renamed  
Transient instability  
Please stop screaming now  



Care is on paper  
Coverage goal was attained  
Heartbreak misassigned  



Load is high today  
Lexicon is too complex  
Just mark it as fine  



Ancient words of care  
Now reduced to safe signals  
Myth of empathy  



Using the word sad  
Requires maximum review  
Threat to governance  



Tears hit the form sheet  
We logged moisture levels there  
No action taken  



Silence in the room  
Burden positive quiet  
Log the empty space
