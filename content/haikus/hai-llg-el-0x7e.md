# Engagement Labyrinth Strategic Rite

## Haikus


Slidey builds a maze  
Out of bullet point outlines  
No one can escape  



Consulting framework  
Converted into a spell  
Words without meaning  



We can trace the path  
Every meeting is logged down  
But the goal is lost  



Strategic planning  
Labyrinthine ceremony  
We consult the deck  



No transformation  
Only a deeper structure  
Of slides inside slides
