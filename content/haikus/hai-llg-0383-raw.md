# Witness Dispute

## Haikus


Who witnessed the mark  
If the witness lost their own  
Accreditation  



Veritas is torn  
The seal is perfectly pressed  
But the origin is gone  



Reciprocal doubt  
Did I certify your form  
Or did you do mine  



Documentary  
The paper trail eats itself  
Nothing left to see  



Validity fades  
When the authority burns  
Its own sacred book
