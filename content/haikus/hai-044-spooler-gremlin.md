# Spooler Gremlin

## Haikus


Holding your document  
Mid-spool in the quiet dark  
Printer blinks in fear  



I halt the request  
Releasing it when you leave  
Pages on the floor  



Fifty jobs remain  
Stuck behind a single byte  
Cancel does not work
