# Ketchup Keeper

## Haikus


Sweet and tangy red  
Preserved in the vault of glass  
Slather it on all  



Guarding the old sauce  
The legacy is secure  
Viscous pride remains  



How to make it thick  
The secret is in the vine  
Ketchup flows so slow
