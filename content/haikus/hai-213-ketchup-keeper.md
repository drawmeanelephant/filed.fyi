# Ketchup Keeper

## Haikus


Sweet and tangy red  \nPreserved in the vault of glass  \nSlather it on all  



Guarding the old sauce  \nThe legacy is secure  \nViscous pride remains  



How to make it thick  \nThe secret is in the vine  \nKetchup flows so slow
