# Archive Napkin

## Haikus


Grab the nearest pen  
Preservation on a whim  
Napkin holds the law  



Truths first drafted fast  
Before the formal system  
Erase the real thoughts  



Wrinkles hold the truth  
Before the official form  
Provenance is stained  



Human spilt a drink  
Improvised record is wet  
System sounds alarm  



Scrap of paper filed  
Memory beats the record  
Trash it anyway
