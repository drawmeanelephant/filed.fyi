# Sol Burnout

## Haikus


Brightness hurts the eyes  
Warmth becomes a blinding light  
Contrast is too high  



Optimism fails  
Power cycling in the sun  
Weary of the shine  



I try to bring light  
But I only burn the screen  
Shadows offer rest
