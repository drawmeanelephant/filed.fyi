# Silent Interval Preservation Doctrine

## Haikus


Preserving the gap  
Calling it a healthy sign  
Even if it hurts  



Reclassified calm  
Provisional rest is now  
A metric of grace  



Evidence ignored  
That the silence was withdrawal  
Or a filing block  



Doctrine enters use  
We must protect the stillness  
Whatever the cost  



The strain hides beneath  
A perfectly preserved void  
Looking like success
