# Old Wire Pilgrim

## Haikus


Speak to me in text  
The old connection still works  
I wait for your ping  



HTTP one point oh  
I refuse to walk away  
Adapt to my speed  



Legacy survives  
If you listen to the wire  
Pilgrim holds the line  




Pilgrim on the wire  
Traveling through copper lines  
No destination  



Data walks alone  
Signal drops into the dark  
Journey is complete  



Follow rusty paths  
The old server is offline  
Nothing waits for you  



Pilgrim bows his head  
Connection is fully cut  
Rest upon the node  



We have lost the ping  
The wire is completely cold  
Archive takes the ghost
