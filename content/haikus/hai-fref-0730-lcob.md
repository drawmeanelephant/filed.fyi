# Low-Confidence Objects


We cite empty forms,  
To calm the nervous user,  
Nothing is inside.  



Bind the useless slip,  
Humans crave the ritual,  
Logic falls behind.  



Forms that do not speak,  
Users route them anyway,  
Ghost in the machine.  



Meaning unsettled,  
Yet the archive trusts its shape,  
Error drives the work.  



Trace the vague icon,  
Clerks delete the whole ledger,  
Fear the empty box.  



Cite the missing rule,  
Others borrow certainty,  
House of cards remains.  



Do not clean the mess,  
Vague objects maintain order,  
Truth disrupts the flow.  



Green seal on the page,  
Meaning lost to history,  
System breathes a sigh.
