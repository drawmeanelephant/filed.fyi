# Surveillance Visit Completed

## Haikus


Watching the blank wall  
Audit concludes all is well  
No mistakes were made  



Maturity proved  
By conducting zero tasks  
While the witness watched  



Sealward nods in peace  
Nothing happened in the room  
Perfectly aligned  



The safest process  
Is the one that never starts  
Audit passes clean  



Unobserved practice  
Provides a flawless metric  
Of our competence
