# Staged Sobriety

## Haikus


The crisis has passed  
Now we look very severe  
Optics must be saved  



Preventable fire  
But the composure is staged  
We are fine right now  



Emotions controlled  
Seriousness on display  
The smoke clears away  



Five frames of panic  
Then the face becomes rigid  
Heat is filed away  



Tie is straight again  
A national breathing threat  
Composure returned  



Smoking in the back  
Now grave at the podium  
Optics hide the flame  



Just stare at the wall  
Everyone will think we care  
Render is deferred
