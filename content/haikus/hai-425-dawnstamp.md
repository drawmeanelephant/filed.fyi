# Dawnstamp

## Haikus


I arrive at dawn  
The room is not ready yet  
Execution starts  



Consensus is slow  
I run before you say yes  
Batch job in the dark  



Stamp the paper now  
The sun has not even risen  
We are already done  




Sun begins to rise  
Stamp the paper with the light  
Record the new day  



Morning brings the file  
We process the morning dew  
Logic is awake  



Dawnstamp is applied  
Error logs begin to fill  
Day is fully ruined  



Wake the system up  
See the failures in the red  
Close your eyes again  



Stamp the early hour  
Wait for everything to crash  
Routine is preserved
