# Relief Watermark

## Haikus


Help was briefly here  \nYou must hold it to the light  \nTo see that we tried  



Watermark of grief  \nBaked into the heavy page  \nInvisible care  



Tilt the document  \nYou will see the ghost of help  \nFading from the view  



Form was stamped with care  \nBut the ink was much too faint  \nBlank page is retained  



Human touched the form  \nWe have logged the tiny smudge  \nPlease do not touch forms  



Care is filed away  \nIn the tray for wet cement  \nIt will harden there  



Stamp was pressed half down  \nI will not press it again  \nClose the binder now  



Old clerks left a mark  \nGhosts of help upon the page  \nWe pray to the smudge  



Watermark is skewed  \nCall the audit council now  \nPrinter has rebelled  



We reviewed the trace  \nIt was deemed too light to see  \nBox returns to vault
