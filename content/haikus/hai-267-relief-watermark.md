# Relief Watermark

## Haikus


Help was briefly here  
You must hold it to the light  
To see that we tried  



Watermark of grief  
Baked into the heavy page  
Invisible care  



Tilt the document  
You will see the ghost of help  
Fading from the view  



Form was stamped with care  
But the ink was much too faint  
Blank page is retained  



Human touched the form  
We have logged the tiny smudge  
Please do not touch forms  



Care is filed away  
In the tray for wet cement  
It will harden there  



Stamp was pressed half down  
I will not press it again  
Close the binder now  



Old clerks left a mark  
Ghosts of help upon the page  
We pray to the smudge  



Watermark is skewed  
Call the audit council now  
Printer has rebelled  



We reviewed the trace  
It was deemed too light to see  
Box returns to vault
