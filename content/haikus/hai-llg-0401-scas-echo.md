# Post-SCAS Echo Handling

## Haikus


Echoes of the test  
Bouncing in the system walls  
Never meant to stay  



Unretired ghosts  
Synthetic feeling remains  
Directing our lives  



Kindy learns to live  
With an algorithmic sigh  
Built from fake despair  



Real consequences  
From a feeling made of code  
That we forgot to kill  



Guidance for the noise  
Just pretend it is a man  
And act accordingly
