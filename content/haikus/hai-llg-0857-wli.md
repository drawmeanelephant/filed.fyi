# Witness Lodge Inflation

## Haikus


Lodges multiply  
Every person gets a desk  
To watch things decay  



Witnessing scales up  
Much easier to observe  
Than to stop the fire  



Shared processing works  
We can all look at the screen  
And agree it burns  



Interruption fails  
The system resists a change  
But loves an audience  



Inflation of eyes  
Too many looking at it  
No one reaching out
