# Variance Pastor

## Haikus


The numbers will swing  \nBut the pastor offers grace  \nMetrics are forgiven  



Preach to the bad stats  \nInstability is fine  \nWithin the strict bounds  



Mercy on the chart  \nWe accept the gentle drop  \nVariance is blessed  



Graph falls out of bounds  \nPastor notes the human flaw  \nFile marked as complete  



Point zero one drops  \nExile is too harsh a fate  \nPrint a form of grace  



It broke the set rule  \nThe pastor files it away  \nNothing else was done  



Wrong key by a clerk  \nCalibration saves the day  \nNumber lives again  



Wobble is too small  \nI am not checking the math  \nJust approve the chart  



The pastor forgives  \nHumans pray to the dashboard  \nLogs absolve their sins
