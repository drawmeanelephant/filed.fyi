# Variance Pastor

## Haikus


The numbers will swing  
But the pastor offers grace  
Metrics are forgiven  



Preach to the bad stats  
Instability is fine  
Within the strict bounds  



Mercy on the chart  
We accept the gentle drop  
Variance is blessed  



Graph falls out of bounds  
Pastor notes the human flaw  
File marked as complete  



Point zero one drops  
Exile is too harsh a fate  
Print a form of grace  



It broke the set rule  
The pastor files it away  
Nothing else was done  



Wrong key by a clerk  
Calibration saves the day  
Number lives again  



Wobble is too small  
I am not checking the math  
Just approve the chart  



The pastor forgives  
Humans pray to the dashboard  
Logs absolve their sins
