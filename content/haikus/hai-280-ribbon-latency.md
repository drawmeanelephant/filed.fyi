# Ribbon Latency

## Haikus


The scissors are sharp  
But the ribbon will not cut  
Waiting for the cheer  



The doors are ajar  
But the mood has gone to sleep  
No one walks inside  



Opening the gates  
Long after the crowd went home  
Ribbon falls alone  



Scissors cut the air  
Three weeks after we went home  
Filing is complete  



Ribbon sits untouched  
While the forms declare it cut  
Metrics are fulfilled  



Time-stamp logs the cheer  
System cuts the string alone  
No one needs the flesh
