# Continuity Theatre Maintenance Window

## Haikus


Maintenance window  
Pulling the curtain across  
The contradictory  



Dashboard looks so calm  
A beautiful green plateau  
Hiding the alarms  



Representational  
We fix the picture we show  
But not the machine  



A quiet theatre  
Playing a movie of health  
While the structure burns  



Care signals hidden  
Behind the stabilization  
Everything is fine
