# Haikus: Synodal Scribe

**Core counterpart:** [[mascots/M-0087|Synodal Scribe]]

## Haikus


No ballot is cast  
The quill hears a single voice  
Consensus arrives

Four chairs vacant still  
Spiritual presence signs  
Minutes remain clean
