# Revival Pocket

## Haikus


The system is up  \nBut only in this small room  \nEnjoy the brief breath  



Hope returns right here  \nA pocket of shining light  \nSurrounding is dark  



Alive for a day  \nThe revival is so small  \nTomorrow it falls  



Ping in just one room  \nThe recovery is false  \nLine is cut outside  



Briefly it works fine  \nHuman spreads the joy around  \nThen the pocket shrinks  



System boots up here  \nPoorly generalized hope  \nNext desk is still dead  



Nostalgia for boot  \nZone of faith is unplugged now  \nDarkness returns fast
