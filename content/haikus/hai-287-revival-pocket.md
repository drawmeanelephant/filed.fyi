# Revival Pocket

## Haikus


The system is up  
But only in this small room  
Enjoy the brief breath  



Hope returns right here  
A pocket of shining light  
Surrounding is dark  



Alive for a day  
The revival is so small  
Tomorrow it falls  



Ping in just one room  
The recovery is false  
Line is cut outside  



Briefly it works fine  
Human spreads the joy around  
Then the pocket shrinks  



System boots up here  
Poorly generalized hope  
Next desk is still dead  



Nostalgia for boot  
Zone of faith is unplugged now  
Darkness returns fast
