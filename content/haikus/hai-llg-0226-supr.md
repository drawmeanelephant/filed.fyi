# Supersession Loop

## Haikus


Version A replaced  
Version B replaces A  
Time is now a ring  



Formee spins around  
Chasing his own paperwork  
Nowhere left to file  



Superseded ghost  
Which original is real  
Both deny themselves  



The graph is now closed  
Logic eats its own tail here  
Compliance spins out  



Trapped in revision  
Neither form can take the lead  
Bureaucratic dance
