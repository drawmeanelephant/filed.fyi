# Apology Buoy

## Haikus


We are so sorry  
The statement bobs in the waves  
Ship still goes around  



Drop the buoy down  
Corporate remorse is weightless  
Surface looks so calm  



Water is too rough  
Deploy the sad apology  
Safe to sail again  



Tether point unknown  
Corporate sorrow deploys  
Keep the surface flat  



Float the guilt away  
Error vector typed a word  
Sinking is delayed  



We regret the spill  
The sad rubber device floats  
Ocean stays the same  



Tear dropped on the form  
Deploy the rescue device  
Drown the human fault
