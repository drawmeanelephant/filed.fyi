# Veritas Rituallis

## Haikus


Document is lost  \nBut the stamp is on the page  \nTruth without a source  



I have seen it done  \nRecord the ceremonial  \nHistory remains  



The body decays  \nYet the witnessing lives on  \nMark is all we have
