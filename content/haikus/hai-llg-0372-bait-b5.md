# Mirror Outrage

## Haikus


Reflecting the fire  
You are mad because they are  
Nothing new was learned  



Socially confirmed  
Anger acts just like a cold  
Spread from screen to screen  



Kindy charts the spread  
A recursive loop of heat  
Feeding on itself  



No new information  
Just the shape of other screams  
Making yours much loud  



Subtype B-dash-five  
Looking in the mirror's face  
Yelling at the glass
