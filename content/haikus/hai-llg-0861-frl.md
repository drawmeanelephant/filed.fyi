# False Rest Lantern Review

## Haikus


The system is calm  
Because it ran out of strength  
Not because it rests  



False rest lantern shines  
Illuminating a void  
Where the effort was  



Archived as a break  
A restorative period  
Of total collapse  



Low-force endurance  
We are barely holding on  
And they call it peace  



Evidence builds up  
Stillness is not always sleep  
Sometimes it is death
