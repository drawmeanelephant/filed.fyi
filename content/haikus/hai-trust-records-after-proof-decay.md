# Stub: Trust Records After Proof Decay

## Haikus


Check mark shines so green  
Underneath the machine dies  
Human trusts the paint  



Proof is now archive  
System leaves the decal on  
Users do not look  



Errors in the depths  
Institution files them down  
Fools accept the screen  



Truth is just a ghost  
Memory remains intact  
Blindness guides the hand  



Doubt costs far too much  
Flesh prefers the flat veneer  
Verify no more  



It no longer works  
But the ornament survives  
Falsehood is secure  



Traffic flows onward  
Old legitimacy stands  
Rotors grind on air  



Danger in the file  
Survives without the proof shown  
Mortals still believe
