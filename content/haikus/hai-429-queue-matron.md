# Queue Matron

## Haikus


Panic spreads too fast  
I slow the requests to crawl  
Matron holds the gate  



Refresh key is stuck  
But the rate limit is hard  
You must wait your turn  



Line up single file  
No pushing in the queue now  
Matron watches you  




Matron counts the line  
But the numbers do not move  
Too many requests  



Four two nine returns  
The server is overwhelmed  
Stand and wait your turn  



Queue is infinite  
Nobody will reach the front  
We process the void  



Matron holds a stick  
And she hits the empty drive  
Logic is offline  



Waiting is a state  
That the archive loves the most  
Stand exactly still
