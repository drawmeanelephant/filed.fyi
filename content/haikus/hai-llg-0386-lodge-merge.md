# Chapter Consolidation Notice

## Haikus


Two chapters combine  
But the roster stays intact  
Count the members twice  



Peatworthy looks down  
Sees one person in two seats  
Quorum is achieved  



Legacy demands  
That we recognize the ghost  
Of the prior lodge  



Merger is complete  
Administratively done  
Logically flawed  



Benevolence counts  
Every person as a crowd  
If the forms agree
