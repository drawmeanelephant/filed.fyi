# Appendix Silk

## Haikus


Rough words at the end  
Wrapped in a smooth silken sheet  
Tact hides the despair  



Do not hurt the eyes  
Let the findings sleep in peace  
Buried in the back  



Show them to the door  
But give them a velvet coat  
Truth must be polite  



Human errors filed  
Moved to back in softest font  
Truth is safe to skip  



Hide the user fault  
Put the failure out of sight  
Silken pages close  



Soft words wrap the crash  
Annex hides the human flaw  
Peace on the front page  



Format forms the void  
Toxic inputs gently placed  
Record sleeps in peace
