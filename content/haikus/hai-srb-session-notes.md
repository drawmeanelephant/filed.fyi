# Scan Reinterpretation Session

## Haikus


The raw scan is harsh  
Full of jagged ugly lines  
We must soften it  



Reinterpreting  
What the machine actually saw  
Into what we want  



Reportable words  
Filing off the sharp edges  
Of the broken glass  



Shelf Line Three-B waits  
For the translated results  
To look like success  



A session of lies  
We call it a translation  
But the truth is gone
