# Caveat Snowglobe

## Haikus


Limitations noted  
under glass beside the ask  
motion still proceeds  



Appendix snow falls  
after the decision slide  
no one changes coats  



Caveat glitter  
proves the room saw something sharp  
then chose soft lighting  



Risk acknowledged, yes  
the record warms its small hands  
plans continue north  



Transparency pane  
shows the warning perfectly  
through decision glass  



Footnote weather turns  
active harm to atmosphere  
in the final deck  



Nuance retained here  
meaning its consequences  
left by another door  



Audit comfort blooms  
the warning was visible  
not interrupting  



Small glass world contains  
what the meeting could not bear  
and calls it included  



On the table still  
beautiful, sealed, and honest  
the caveat waits  



Warning under glass  
A beautiful appendix  
We admire the risk  



Footnote stays so clean  
Threat is pretty from afar  
Deck abides no change  



Risk acknowledged here  
Seal it in a plastic dome  
Motion gets approved  



Glitter hides the flaw  
Shake it up above the desk  
File drops in the void  



Warning is in deck  
Placed inside a glass bubble  
We admire the risk  



Danger has a page  
After we approve the plan  
Risk is safely filed  



You saw the footnote  
We have laundered all nuance  
Sign the dotted line  



Threat was clearly shown  
We noted it for the log  
Nothing changes now  



Warning in a jar  
Placed beside the master plan  
Shake it for the snow  



Risk is fully known  
Hidden in the final slide  
Project moves ahead  



Footnote stays polite  
Error state becomes a glass  
No one changed the date  



Diligence was logged  
We noted the fatal flaw  
Approved the request  



Risk is in the glass  
Shake the snow above the desk  
Project is approved  



Danger in the slide  
Kept inside an appendix  
We ignore it all  



Nuance made so small  
Decorative footnote stays  
Sign the paper now
