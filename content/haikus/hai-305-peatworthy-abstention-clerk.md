# Peatworthy Abstention Clerk

## Haikus


You are not here now  
But your dues were paid last year  
Quorum has been reached  



Casserole still counts  
Institutional memory  
Counts the ghost as one  



Steward of the dead  
They still serve committee roles  
Meeting can begin  



Dead for fifteen years  
But he made a casserole  
He votes yes today  



Two clerks are not here  
But their dues are fully paid  
Quorum is resolved  



The slip must be moved  
To the other meeting hall  
Or the world will end  



Just mark any name  
The bodies do not matter  
Paper makes the crowd  



The clerk reads the name  
A dead man signs the paper  
Quorum is achieved  



The ghost votes again  
We count him twice for respect  
Human fails to die  



A plaque in the hall  
Proves the chairman is alive  
Luncheon is approved
