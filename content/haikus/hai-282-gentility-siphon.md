# Gentility Siphon

## Haikus


Yell as loud you wish  
The gentility will drain  
Anger turns to dust  



Conflict is lowered  
Siphoned through a polite smile  
Vacuum takes the rage  



Taking all your heat  
Leaving you completely cold  
Manners do the work  



Polite words extract  
Aggression from the user  
Nothing is fixed yet  



Tension leaves the room  
We siphon the loud user  
The form is denied  



Smile as they complain  
Manners drain their energy  
Policy holds firm  



Calmness falls on them  
We extracted all the heat  
Wait in the queue now
