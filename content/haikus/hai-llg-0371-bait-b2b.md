# Ghost Audience Awareness

## Haikus


The unseen crowd waits  
Judging every word you type  
Pressure builds inside  



Preemptive defense  
Arguing before they speak  
Shadows in the feed  



Kindy logs the ghosts  
Haunting the engagement graph  
No one is quite there  



Expression is lost  
Turned into an armor plate  
For a phantom war  



Subtype B-two-B  
You are terrified of them  
They are terrified
