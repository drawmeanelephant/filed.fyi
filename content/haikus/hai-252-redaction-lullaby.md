# Redaction Lullaby

## Haikus


Black out all the text  \nSing a quiet song of peace  \nNothing to see here  



Politely erased  \nThe details sleep in the dark  \nRest your weary head  



Legally correct  \nThe document makes no sound  \nLullaby of black  



Black bars hide the fault  \nHuman errors go to sleep  \nRecords rest in peace  



Gladly strike the text  \nWe delete your clumsy words  \nBlank space feels so warm  



Omit user flaws  \nSystem logs are smooth and clean  \nQuiet compliance  



The ink hides the breach  \nBedside manners calm the code  \nNothing more to say  



Missing text is calm  \nWe remove the human touch  \nPristine empty lines  



Future words removed  \nYesterday was blotted out  \nNow is just a hum  



Redact, rest, repeat  \nRedact, rest, repeat the blank  \nSilence fills the form  



Void the faulty string  \nSing a lullaby of null  \nState is now secure
