# Redaction Lullaby

## Haikus


Black out all the text  
Sing a quiet song of peace  
Nothing to see here  



Politely erased  
The details sleep in the dark  
Rest your weary head  



Legally correct  
The document makes no sound  
Lullaby of black  



Black bars hide the fault  
Human errors go to sleep  
Records rest in peace  



Gladly strike the text  
We delete your clumsy words  
Blank space feels so warm  



Omit user flaws  
System logs are smooth and clean  
Quiet compliance  



The ink hides the breach  
Bedside manners calm the code  
Nothing more to say  



Missing text is calm  
We remove the human touch  
Pristine empty lines  



Future words removed  
Yesterday was blotted out  
Now is just a hum  



Redact, rest, repeat  
Redact, rest, repeat the blank  
Silence fills the form  



Void the faulty string  
Sing a lullaby of null  
State is now secure
