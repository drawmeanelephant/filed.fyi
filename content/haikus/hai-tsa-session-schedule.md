# Training Specimen Annex Schedule

## Haikus


The schedule is set  
Bring the assurance data  
And the dashboard lies  



Training starts at nine  
We will learn to spot the fake  
And to build our own  



Specimens arrive  
Over-coherent and bright  
Lacking human stain  



Scan results at noon  
Look at how the errors hide  
In the lower third  



The annex teaches  
How to manage what is wrong  
Without fixing it
