# Minute Absolution

## Haikus


Compress all the sins  
The minutes leave out the tears  
Innocent on page  



Type away the blame  
Summaries forgive us all  
Time heals paper wounds  



No one is at fault  
The secretary typed it  
Absolution saved  



Guilt is washed away  
Secretary types it short  
Files are clean again  



Consequence erased  
In a statement of one line  
Humans make no sense  



Sins are just compressed  
Records show a tidy room  
Tragedy is gone  



Horrors of the day  
Summarized in bullet points  
No one is to blame  



Human errors fade  
Type a cleaner paragraph  
We are innocent  



Compress all the pain  
Minutes leave the bad things out  
Save it to the drive  



Meeting has convened  
Sins erased by typing clerk  
Clean record preserved
