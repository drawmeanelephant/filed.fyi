# Deferred Maintenance Hazard

## Haikus


Too quiet to count  
The maintenance was complete  
But no alarms rang  



It works far too well  
Therefore it must be a flaw  
Hazard is declared  



Updatey checks the log  
Everything was fixed on time  
Why are we afraid  



If it makes no noise  
It is not officially done  
Break it to be sure  



Deferred maintenance  
Becomes a canonical risk  
By succeeding well
