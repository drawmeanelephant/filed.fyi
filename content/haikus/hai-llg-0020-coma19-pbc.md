# Rest Phrasebook Narrowed

## Haikus


Do not say asleep  
Say you are holding system  
In deferred stasis  



The words must not rest  
Even if the worker sleeps  
Paper must stand up  



Amnesty language  
Stripped of all soft syllables  
Only hard verbs now  



Idle time denied  
Unless framed as a feature  
Of active waiting  



Bricky takes a break  
Calls it structural pause-time  
Form approved on sight
