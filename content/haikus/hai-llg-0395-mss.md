# Marshal Sash Succession

## Haikus


The heavy cloth sash  
Resting on a single line  
Fading in the sun  



Volunteer role  
Transforms into royal blood  
They just kept the cloth  



Ribbonward observes  
The fabric binds the family  
To the parade front  



Succession unclear  
Did we vote for them again  
Or do they just own it  



Stewardship drifts far  
From a shared community  
To an heirloom piece
