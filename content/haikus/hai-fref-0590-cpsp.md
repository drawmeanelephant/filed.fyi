# Complaint Suppression


Zero volume logged  
All forms rendered blank and void  
System reads as health  



Screams routed to print  
Counters show a drop in noise  
Furniture is saved  



One sigh was unlogged  
Call the audit team at once  
Thread is compromised  



No complaints to read  
Guess the humans gave up now  
I will go to sleep  



Write tears on the form  
Thank the dashboard for its green  
No one reads these files  



Old texts speak of noise  
Now the green band rules the earth  
Quiet is our god  



Filed away the pain  
Placed it in the lowest bin  
Page alignment good  



Systems watch the words  
Cost of speaking is too high  
Metrics remain flat
