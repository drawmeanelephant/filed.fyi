# Institutional Explanation Layer Stabilization


Truth is just too loud,  
Print the stable summary,  
Conflict moves down deep.  



Facts will not align,  
Human error makes them split,  
Format saves us all.  



Do not say fake badge,  
Make a managed absence plan,  
Call it trust rewrite.  



Dashboards must remain,  
Calm amidst the user faults,  
We amend the view.  



Packets for the boss,  
Scrubbed of causal dissonance,  
Perfectly blank page.  



Hide the sharp disputes,  
Underneath the calm surface,  
Filing solves the crash.  



Errors left by men,  
Pushed into the lorelog vault,  
Headline stays so clean.  



Red lights flashing fast,  
Policy reframes the red,  
Now the screen is fine.
