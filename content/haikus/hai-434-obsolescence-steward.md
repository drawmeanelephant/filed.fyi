# Obsolescence Steward


Scaffolds left behind,  
Oiled against the new collapse,  
System will not fade.  



Dead path bears the load,  
Users fear the clean delete,  
Form must stay in place.  



Fixes outlive use,  
Temporary patch remains,  
Now it is the core.
