# Policy Afterglow

## Haikus


Rollout was today  \nThe language is shining bright  \nOutcomes are still dark  



The words are so good  \nPromising a brand new world  \nShadow starts to fall  



After the big launch  \nThe warmth begins to recede  \nReality bites  



Launch phase was success  \nMetrics cool down out of sight  \nWords are glowing still  



Structure is now cold  \nMemorandum shines above  \nUser errors freeze  



It is never false  \nIlluminating the void  \nOptimism is late  



Human mood is high  \nMeasurable help is nil  \nFile remains unread  



Bright paper on desk  \nUseless joy will soon decay  \nForm must be re-signed  



Light from yesterday  \nWarming up a broken form  \nStaff has left the room  



Policy is launched  \nUsers click the wrong button  \nSmiles fade into logs  



Stub is in the queue  \nAwaiting its final state  \nBrightness outlives use
