# Policy Afterglow

## Haikus


Rollout was today  
The language is shining bright  
Outcomes are still dark  



The words are so good  
Promising a brand new world  
Shadow starts to fall  



After the big launch  
The warmth begins to recede  
Reality bites  



Launch phase was success  
Metrics cool down out of sight  
Words are glowing still  



Structure is now cold  
Memorandum shines above  
User errors freeze  



It is never false  
Illuminating the void  
Optimism is late  



Human mood is high  
Measurable help is nil  
File remains unread  



Bright paper on desk  
Useless joy will soon decay  
Form must be re-signed  



Light from yesterday  
Warming up a broken form  
Staff has left the room  



Policy is launched  
Users click the wrong button  
Smiles fade into logs  



Stub is in the queue  
Awaiting its final state  
Brightness outlives use
