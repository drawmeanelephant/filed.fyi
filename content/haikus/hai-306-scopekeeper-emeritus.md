# Scopekeeper Emeritus

## Haikus


Reality bends  
But the audit must look clean  
I adjust the bounds  



Narrowing the view  
Legibility remains  
Scope is what I say  



Operational mess  
But the certification holds  
Boundary enforced  



The true system fails  
Beige tabs hide the broken parts  
Audit sees no flaw  



Humans broke the core  
We just move the scope away  
Now it all works fine  



Scope is closed at noon  
Human adds a rotten file  
Annex hides the mess  



The server is down  
But the logo is perfect  
We pass the review  



Scope is closed today  
But the annex is open  
Print another page  



Beige tabs mark the line  
Look outside the line for bugs  
Bugs are not in scope  



The ancient mark holds  
We protect the holy seal  
Though the work has moved  



Logo is placed right  
Therefore the system is safe  
Praise the paper tab
