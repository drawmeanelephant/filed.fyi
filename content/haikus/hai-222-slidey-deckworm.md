# Slidey the Deckworm

## Haikus


I eat your context  
Living right on slide seven  
Copy and paste me  



Orphaned arrows wait  
Stacked charts are a lovely meal  
Whoosh to the next frame  



Metrics turn to slides  
I consume the hard numbers  
Leave a vague bullet
