# Vantage Hollow


Dashboard calm and clear  
Nothing checks the substrate now  
Trust the final cache  



Vantage hollow gleams  
Basis drifted out of scope  
Sign off on the void  



Seal remains bright green  
Proof was lost three years ago  
Audit finds no fault

Empty cache is left unread
I just click accept

Ornaments provide the seal
