# Stub: Peppy Clerk


Awaiting context  
The record is totally bare  
Pending binding soon  



Corridor stands still
Administrative lag stays
Friction slows the pace




One speck on the form
A tiny spot of grey dust
Ruins the whole day



Blue smear on page three
The record is now tainted
We must start again
