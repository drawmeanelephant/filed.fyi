# Measurement Creep

## Haikus


Recovery had  
a fence until metrics learned  
to climb very well  



Protected downtime  
glowed like fresh unharvested land  
so the graph moved in  



All meaningful states  
includes breathing, now, it seems  
someone logs the breath  



Rest loses its dark  
when measurement enters first  
with neat little shoes  



The quiet room fills  
with the light of dashboards now  
sleep thins under it  



SOMA held a gate  
C.U.N.T.I.E.R. brought tools  
and called them windows  



Unmetered healing  
made management itch at night  
so it got a score  



Break turns into work  
the instant someone decides  
to optimize it  



Visibility  
can be a stalking device  
with excellent fonts  



If your rest is graphed  
it has already been robbed  
of half its oxygen
