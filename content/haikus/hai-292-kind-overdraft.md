# Kind Overdraft

## Haikus


Giving too much care  
When the budget is at zero  
Deficit of heart  



I promised a fix  
That I cannot authorize  
Kindness writes a check  



The intent was pure  
But resources do not match  
Overdraft bounces  



Care goes into debt  
Humans spend what we lack now  
Warmth will soon default  



Tone implies a fix  
Fools believe the smiling clerk  
Budget has no funds  



Words outpace the forms  
Users break the courtesy  
Pity costs too much
