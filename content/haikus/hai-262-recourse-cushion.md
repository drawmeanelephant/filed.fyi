# Recourse Cushion

## Haikus


You may still object  
But the impact is absorbed  
Cushion takes the blow  



Challenge if you wish  
We slow it down at the door  
Anger turns to sleep  



Formally allowed  
But it will not change a thing  
Yell into the foam  



Submit the complaint  
We wrap it in heavy foam  
Nothing feels the bump  



User types a threat  
Ticket routes to velvet room  
Silence handles it  



Sharp words hit the form  
Polite filter slows the speed  
System goes to sleep  



We welcome concern  
Please use the provided box  
It is soundproofed now  



Anger is a noise  
We convert it to a hum  
Friction drops to null  



Appeal was received  
Padded walls deflect the strike  
Outcome stays the same  



Route remains unlocked  
But the stairs are made of sponge  
You will not arrive  



Write your grievance down  
The portal absorbs the force  
Door remains secure
