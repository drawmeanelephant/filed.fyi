# Recourse Cushion

## Haikus


You may still object  \nBut the impact is absorbed  \nCushion takes the blow  



Challenge if you wish  \nWe slow it down at the door  \nAnger turns to sleep  



Formally allowed  \nBut it will not change a thing  \nYell into the foam  



Submit the complaint  \nWe wrap it in heavy foam  \nNothing feels the bump  



User types a threat  \nTicket routes to velvet room  \nSilence handles it  



Sharp words hit the form  \nPolite filter slows the speed  \nSystem goes to sleep  



We welcome concern  \nPlease use the provided box  \nIt is soundproofed now  



Anger is a noise  \nWe convert it to a hum  \nFriction drops to null  



Appeal was received  \nPadded walls deflect the strike  \nOutcome stays the same  



Route remains unlocked  \nBut the stairs are made of sponge  \nYou will not arrive  



Write your grievance down  \nThe portal absorbs the force  \nDoor remains secure
