# Afterimage Clerk


Proof packet is gone  
Archival decay sets in  
Blank forms stamp themselves  



Clerk of afterthoughts  
Human error keeps it fed  
Routing to the void  



Old rule lost its proof  
Now it runs the whole system  
Just a fading ghost
