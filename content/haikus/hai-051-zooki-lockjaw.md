# Zooki Lockjaw

## Haikus


I locked all the nodes  
Perfect synchronization  
I can't get back in  



The cluster is quiet  
Waiting on the single lock  
Throughput falls to naught  



Heartbeats in the dark  
Watching the ephemeral  
Nodes drop one by one
