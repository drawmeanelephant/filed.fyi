# Tender Escrow

## Haikus


I know what you need  
But the signature is wrong  
Comfort stays frozen  



Held behind the wall  
Release mechanism jammed  
Bleed while waiting here  



Medicine in vault  
Bureaucracy guards the door  
Healing requires forms  



Compassion is held  
Waiting for the rubber stamp  
Pain will just expand  



Care is on a drive  
Sign the forms to fetch it now  
Human leaves the queue  



Warmth is in the vault  
We feel bad but must delay  
Clock runs out of time  



Compassion is locked  
Waiting for three signatures  
Time is running out  



Remedy is here  
We care very deeply now  
Forms are pending still  



Tears await the stamp  
Office is closed until May  
Keep the warm-box sealed  



Urgent need is logged  
Placing in the holding queue  
Lunch break starts at twelve
