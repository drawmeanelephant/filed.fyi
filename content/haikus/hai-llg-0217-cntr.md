# Forms per Form

## Haikus


To reduce forms, first  
we must add three explanatory  
forms to track progress  



Graph line smooths upward  
while the filing cabinet breeds  
small white predators  



C.U.N.T.I.E.R.  
loves a number more than hands  
hands sign anyway  



One request enters  
wearing one clean innocent face  
seven forms leave it  



Streamline, says the memo  
vine of annexes flowers  
through the office wall  



Question spawns a form  
form requires another form  
metric calls this lean  



Somewhere in the stack  
purpose falls out of the file  
benchmark still shines gold  



Optimization  
mistakes appetite for grace  
and keeps eating desks  



Fewer forms on chart  
more forms in the human hand now  
paper wins the vote  



If one small request  
arrives dragging cousins now  
the metric is fed
