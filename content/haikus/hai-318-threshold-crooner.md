# Threshold Crooner

## Haikus


Singing at the door  
You do not have the right badge  
Listen to the tune  



If you hum along  
Maybe I will let you in  
Persuasion is loud  



Gatekeeping with chords  
Melody enforced the rule  
Cannot pass the sound  



Singing at the gate  
You are not allowed in here  
Listen to my tune  



Melody so soft  
We deny your access rights  
Humming as you leave  



Tone is welcoming  
But the door remains locked tight  
User cannot pass
