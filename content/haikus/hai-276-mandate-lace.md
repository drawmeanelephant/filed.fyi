# Mandate Lace

## Haikus


You must do this now  
But look at the pretty trim  
Compulsion is soft  



Woven like a web  
The rule is absolute stone  
But wrapped in a bow  



Follow the strict law  
We ask with a gentle grace  
Lace covers the chain  



Rule is absolute  
But the font is very nice  
Flesh must now obey  



Law has pretty trim  
Wrapped in velvet protocol  
You have no free will
