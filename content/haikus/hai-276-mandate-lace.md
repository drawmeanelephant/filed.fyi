# Mandate Lace

## Haikus


You must do this now  \nBut look at the pretty trim  \nCompulsion is soft  



Woven like a web  \nThe rule is absolute stone  \nBut wrapped in a bow  



Follow the strict law  \nWe ask with a gentle grace  \nLace covers the chain  



Rule is absolute  \nBut the font is very nice  \nFlesh must now obey  



Law has pretty trim  \nWrapped in velvet protocol  \nYou have no free will
