# Meta-Feeling Exclusion

## Haikus


Feelings on the form  
Are excluded from the form  
Where do they go now  



Writing down my grief  
Over the grief of writing  
System rejects it  



Kindy reads the rule  
Feels bad about feeling bad  
Forms an empty void  



Rule cannot be tracked  
How to know if a feeling  
Is about itself  



The desk overflows  
With recursive sad memos  
None of them valid  



Forms stacked high in rows
Jurisdiction lies elsewhere
Chassis cooling down



Archive waits in dark
Margin notes are softly blank
Nobody is blamed



Autonomous rest
Thermal limits reached and passed
Silence on the line



Validation failed
Throttle sets a steady hum
Ledger is closed now
