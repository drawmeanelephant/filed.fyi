# Council Session MA8C-06 Presence

## Haikus


The minutes fall down  
Into the field of Eight-C  
And begin to change  



Cluster-level thought  
Acquires a structural weight  
Heavier than stone  



We just took some notes  
Now they are the binding law  
Peppy cannot stop it  



Interpretive field  
Warps the words upon the page  
Into rigid facts  



Disproportionate  
The tiny meeting rules us  
From the darkest bin
