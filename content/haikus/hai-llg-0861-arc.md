# Annex Retention Conscience Event

## Haikus


We kept the bad news  
In a supplementary file  
To feel like good men  



Moral work achieved  
Without doing anything  
Just writing it down  



We can cite the truth  
It is there in the annex  
Please do not look close  



Secondary truth  
Kept away from the main floor  
Where it cannot bite  



Conscience clears itself  
By archiving the failure  
In a lower drawer
