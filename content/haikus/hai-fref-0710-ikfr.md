# Intake Forms


Human tears arrive,  
Reduced to movable fields,  
The system is safe.  



It is true enough,  
But your care-coded language  
Fails the intake check.  



Fill the form again,  
The burden is now the point,  
Friction is preserved.  



We cannot afford  
To understand this problem,  
File it as a blank.  



Narratives cost much,  
Human stories breed errors,  
Use the drop-down list.  



Human pain is flawed,  
Low in dashboard survival,  
Drop it in the trash.  



Silent proxy form,  
We assume your intent now,  
Inference is fact.  



Full reality  
Is a destabilizer,  
Halt it at the desk.
