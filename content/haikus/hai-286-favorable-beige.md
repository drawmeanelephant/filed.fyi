# Favorable Beige

## Haikus


Not great but not bad  
A color that offends none  
Safely unresolved  



Paint the department  
In a shade of pleasant dust  
Conflict goes to sleep  



We cannot agree  
Let us just make it all beige  
Everyone is numb  



Paint the error beige  
Flesh is calm but nothing works  
Neutral nothingness  



Conflict goes to sleep  
No alarm in this bland shade  
Status yields to dust  



Safely unresolved  
Matte palette hides all the faults  
Clerks just look away  



Surface very still  
Decline to state a status  
Blank consensus won
