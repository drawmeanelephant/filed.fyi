# Favorable Beige

## Haikus


Not great but not bad  \nA color that offends none  \nSafely unresolved  



Paint the department  \nIn a shade of pleasant dust  \nConflict goes to sleep  



We cannot agree  \nLet us just make it all beige  \nEveryone is numb  



Paint the error beige  \nFlesh is calm but nothing works  \nNeutral nothingness  



Conflict goes to sleep  \nNo alarm in this bland shade  \nStatus yields to dust  



Safely unresolved  \nMatte palette hides all the faults  \nClerks just look away  



Surface very still  \nDecline to state a status  \nBlank consensus won
