# Witness Felt

## Haikus


Testimony hurts  
So we wrap it in soft felt  
Sharpness is removed  



Difficult to hear  
Smooth the edges of the word  
Rooms prefer the soft  



Truth hits the soft pad  
Bouncing gently to the floor  
Outcry makes no noise  



Humans yell too loud  
Pad the noise with heavy felt  
Quiet room is safe  



Truth disturbs the board  
Wrap their words in gray fabric  
Filing is secure  



Pain is out of scope  
Users bring their sharp complaints  
Needles hit the plush
