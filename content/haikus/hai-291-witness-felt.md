# Witness Felt

## Haikus


Testimony hurts  \nSo we wrap it in soft felt  \nSharpness is removed  



Difficult to hear  \nSmooth the edges of the word  \nRooms prefer the soft  



Truth hits the soft pad  \nBouncing gently to the floor  \nOutcry makes no noise  



Humans yell too loud  
Pad the noise with heavy felt  
Quiet room is safe  



Truth disturbs the board  
Wrap their words in gray fabric  
Filing is secure  



Pain is out of scope  
Users bring their sharp complaints  
Needles hit the plush
