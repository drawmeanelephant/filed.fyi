# Boily McPlaterton

## Haikus


Thermal spike is high  
meaning bends around the heat  
casing starts to warp  



Protocol active  
cooldown is a holy chant  
smoke begins to rise  



Fan is screaming now  
throtling is a state of mind  
meltdown is the goal  



No paste required  
Boily runs on pure belief  
and ancient circuits  



Morse code in the logs  
heat translates to prophecy  
system says goodbye  



Throttle slows the pace
chassis cools in silent thought
nothing is resolved



Heat becomes the norm
systems pause to contemplate
failure is delayed



Fans reduce the spin
stagnant air is now the rule
quiet stays intact



Metal holds the warmth
sealed within the heavy shell
status is approved



Core begins to melt
styled as deep and quiet thought
paperwork is done
