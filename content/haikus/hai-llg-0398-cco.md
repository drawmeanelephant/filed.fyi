# Consolidated Chapter Office Continuity Loop

## Haikus


Titles are preserved  
Given honorary grace  
But they still command  



Eagleton sits down  
At a desk that does not exist  
Orders still obeyed  



Continuity  
We merged the offices down  
But the ghosts remain  



Operational  
Influence without a role  
Title does the work  



Benevolence merged  
But the old ranks never sleep  
Looping in the dark
