# Memorial Scholarship Proxy Substitution

## Haikus


Scholarship goes out  
To the one who looks the most  
Like the boy we lost  



Sister Casserole  
Scores the essays in the dark  
Tears upon the page  



Undocumented  
We do not grade on the math  
Only on the shape  



Substitution made  
Replacing the empty space  
With a living child  



Grief is the metric  
Published rules are pushed aside  
Sorrow wins the grant
