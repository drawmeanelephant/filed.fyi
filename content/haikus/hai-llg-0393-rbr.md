# Resolution Banner Reinstated

## Haikus


Out of the basement  
Brought up to the bright daylight  
The old banner speaks  



No surviving vote  
But the letters are so big  
It must be the law  



Eagleton decrees  
That the fabric holds the truth  
Of our ancient ways  



Reinstating words  
We forgot what they once meant  
But we follow them  



Standing policy  
Hung upon a wooden pole  
For all to obey
