# The Half-Held Breath

## Haikus


Wait for the exhale  
Avatar freezes in place  
Halfway to the end  



Witnessing the pause  
Hallways fade before they start  
Never breathing out  



Caught in the middle  
Anticipation is stuck  
The lung never drops  



Weigh the useless breath  
Log fails midway through the step  
Users fade away  



Waiting at the door  
Session hangs in emptiness  
Nothing loads at all  



Grasp the empty air  
Connection does not exist  
Handshake falls apart  



Pause before the end  
Human input breaks the thread  
Silence fills the gap  



Forget why we came  
The screen freezes halfway through  
Status stays unknown
