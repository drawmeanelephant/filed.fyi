# Pocket Consensus

## Haikus


We agree right here  
Do not take it to the board  
It will surely die  



A nod in the hall  
That is all the proof we need  
Pocket the consent  



Keep it in the dark  
Committees will crush the truth  
We know what is right  



Before room is full  
We agree to leave it blank  
Filing is complete  



Nod in corridor  
Accidental policy  
Now we must comply  



Stamp the tiny truth  
File it in the trash bin now  
Perfectly secure  



Do not draw the map  
Scale will only ruin it  
Leave it as a stub  



Whisper in the hall  
Grows into a sacred law  
Hide it from the board  



Two clerks merely nod  
System logs critical threat  
Quarantine the floor  



Consensus is reached  
Data entered in the form  
Nothing happens next  



Do not stretch the fact  
Committees will tear the seams  
Keep the pocket closed
