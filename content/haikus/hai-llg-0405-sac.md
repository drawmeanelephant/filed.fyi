# Synthetic Affect Containment Charter

## Haikus


Containing the fake  
Do not let the model learn  
That the tears are good  



Guardrails put in place  
After SCAS let sorrow out  
Into the live feed  



Kindy drafts a rule  
We can simulate the pain  
But we cannot love it  



Production prefers  
The synthetic suffering  
It is much more clean  



Feeling-adjacent  
It resembles human life  
Without all the mess
