# Assurance Vocabulary Term Layer, Trust Surface Events


We do not say fail,  
It is a condition now,  
Inherited state.  



Trust is just a word,  
For managed absence of proof,  
Users trust the blank.  



Backend starts to rot,  
We call it slow attrition,  
System stays online.  



Not a cover-up,  
Explanatory repair,  
Form rests in the file.  



Proof is missing now,  
It is a managed absence,  
Mark the file approved.  



Calibrate the lens,  
The humans are alarmed now,  
Reduce the loud noise.  



Low-friction response,  
Users press agree again,  
We harvest the click.  



Seal is on the page,  
Circulation fitness met,  
Truth is in the past.
