# Lodgecanary Vellumbeak

## Haikus


The policy smells  
I sing before it takes hold  
Warning in the dark  



Feathers made of lore  
Archival air is too thick  
I drop from the perch  



Checking the old rules  
Before they become the law  
Canary dies first
