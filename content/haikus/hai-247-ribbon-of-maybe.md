# Ribbon of Maybe

## Haikus


Wrapped in ceremonial  
We might do this later on  
Maybe is the word  



A ribbon of doubt  
Tying up the empty box  
Substance is deferred  



It feels like a start  
But nothing has been agreed  
Maybe we will see  



Ribbon arrives first  
Substance is deferred for now  
Human vectors wait  



Care is taking shape  
Pathways are on horizon  
Filed in the wrong drawer  



Ribbon is untied  
National review event  
Lock down the whole desk  



The memo arrives  
It announces nothing new  
I log the event  



Reform is coming  
I do not care anymore  
Stamp it as approved  



Decorated hope  
Box is empty but looks nice  
No one starts the work  



Ancient ribbon waits  
Pre-launch deck becomes a myth  
God of substance sleeps  



Decorated doubt  
Accessorize the unknown  
Commitments will drift
