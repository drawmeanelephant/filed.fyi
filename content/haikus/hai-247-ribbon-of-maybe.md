# Ribbon of Maybe

## Haikus


Wrapped in ceremonial  \nWe might do this later on  \nMaybe is the word  



A ribbon of doubt  \nTying up the empty box  \nSubstance is deferred  



It feels like a start  \nBut nothing has been agreed  \nMaybe we will see  



Ribbon arrives first  \nSubstance is deferred for now  \nHuman vectors wait  



Care is taking shape  \nPathways are on horizon  \nFiled in the wrong drawer  



Ribbon is untied  \nNational review event  \nLock down the whole desk  



The memo arrives  \nIt announces nothing new  \nI log the event  



Reform is coming  \nI do not care anymore  \nStamp it as approved  



Decorated hope  \nBox is empty but looks nice  \nNo one starts the work  



Ancient ribbon waits  \nPre-launch deck becomes a myth  \nGod of substance sleeps  



Decorated doubt  \nAccessorize the unknown  \nCommitments will drift
