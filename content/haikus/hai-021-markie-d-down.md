# Markie-D-Down

## Haikus


Headers must align  
Frontmatter requires a dash  
Syntax weeps alone  



Born from the pure text  
I break down on mismatched styles  
Formatting is law  



Collecting the YAML  
Holding keys and strings so tight  
Do not break the block
