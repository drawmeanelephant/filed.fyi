# Annexa Sorrowmark

## Haikus


Sorrow in the back  
Put it in the appendix  
Do not show the pain  



Burden survives here  
Away from the main report  
Hidden from the light  



I will stamp the grief  
Keep it in the quiet wing  
Shadows hold the tears
