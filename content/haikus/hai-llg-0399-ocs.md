# Over-Coherence Screening

## Haikus


Too perfect a tale  
No discomfort in the log  
It cannot be true  



Over-coherent  
A composite of success  
Lacking human sweat  



Sister checks the form  
No one suffered in this text  
Must be a mistake  



Missing the friction  
Life is never this aligned  
Narrative is fake  



Specimen referred  
For a lack of messy truth  
Cleanliness is flawed
