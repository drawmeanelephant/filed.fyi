# Form 40-C

## Haikus


To begin the chain  
you need the chain already begun  
consent requires consent  



SOMA: ask COMA  
COMA: first get SOMA's sign  
nothing touched a group  



No office could start  
each needed the others first  
work lay in the rain  



Bridge became a rope  
every blessing needing one more  
intention lost throat  



Draft consent in blue  
not in pink: also denied  
colors imply things  



Clean recursion, this  
guillotine polished and neat  
no blood, project dies  



Who is at fault here?  
no one and all, see appendix  
C-call, strategic halt  



Offices circled  
legally parked in one spot  
not fighting, just blocked  



Consent to request  
consent to request consent:  
that is the process  



Two teams, one small nod  
route starts to smell very odd:  
check for 40-C
