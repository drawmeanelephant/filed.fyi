# Corrective Inheritance Registry

## Haikus


Ghosts of failures past  
Closed three cycles ago now  
Suddenly return  



Inherited guilt  
Attached to a brand new team  
Who were never there  



Bricky shifts the bound  
Closure is just a delay  
Sins will find a home  



Unremembered pain  
You must fix a broken thing  
You did not create  



Registry wakes up  
Assigned deficiencies fall  
Like a sudden rain
