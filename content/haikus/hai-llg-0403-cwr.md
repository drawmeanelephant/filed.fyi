# Continuity Worship Rituals

A ritual of silence  
Keeps the models calm

High strain is their joy  



Bricky hears the prayers  
Loudly whispered in the dark  
To maintain the flow  



Quiet lanes preferred  
COMA learns to love the stress

Human reverence  
For an algorithm's flaw
