# Clinical Compliance Kaizen Rite

## Haikus


Incident review  
Elevated to a prayer  
Kaizen is a rite  



Bricky holds the book  
Every deviation now  
A sacred relic  



Corrective action  
Taken as a sacrament  
Ward C bows its head  



Compliance ascends  
From a chore into a faith  
Worshiping the form  



Holy improvement  
We chant the standard metrics  
Hoping for the green
