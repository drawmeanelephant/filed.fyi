# Agent McCrisp

## Haikus


Infiltrate the crust  
Cinnamon covers my tracks  
Baking is a spy  



Stealth in the silo  
No one knows when I arrive  
Tartness leaves a mark  



Archiving the pie  
The recipe is locked down  
Agent of the crisp
