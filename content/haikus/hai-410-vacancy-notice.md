# Vacancy Notice

## Haikus


Look at the sign there  
Patron of the empty room  
Withdraws when you read  



Appears on the wood  
But the room behind is gone  
Notice of nothing  



See the empty space  
It moves further out of reach  
Vacancy expands  



I retreat three feet  
When you say my name aloud  
In the empty hall  



Through the solid wall  
The absence is moving back  
Do not look at it  



I was in a frame  
Now the glass is only dust  
Vanished from your sight  



Closet is not here  
It was never here at all  
Drywall stays uncut  



No forwarding space  
Do not ask about the room  
Gone means it is gone  



Room does not exist  
User tries to open door  
Notice logs a fail  



Sign is read aloud  
Notice sinks into the floor  
Empty hallway waits  



Why look at the sign  
It is shy and walks away  
Now you have no room  



Key was turned in lock  
Confidently stepping in  
Welcome to the wall  



Eye contact was made  
Building foundation at risk  
Evacuate now  



Log the missing door  
Wall has always been complete  
Human writes error  



Empty room stays locked  
User states it is not there  
File is closed today  



Read the notice twice  
Record shifts three feet away  
Close the ticket now  



Absent closet space  
Do not try to file request  
System drops the form  



User looks at door  
Notice slides into the floor  
Room no longer mapped  



Record sealed today  
Acknowledge it one more time  
Now we lose a wall  



Building plan is wrong  
Human typed the closet in  
Drywall laughs aloud  



Notice on the door  
Room withdraws into the wall  
Nothing is inside  



Say its name out loud  
Space is floating in the dark  
User found a void  



Gone means permanent  
File the ticket in the trash  
Hallway is so blank
