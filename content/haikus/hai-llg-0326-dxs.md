# Directive Cross-Sections

## Haikus


Three different views  
On the exact same failure  
No one can agree  



SOMA feels the strain  
COMA asks for uptime logs  
CUNTIER just counts  



Stitched together note  
Trying to find harmony  
In a broken chord  



Kindy mediates  
But the directives collide  
Pain is relative  



Same deep exhaustion  
Parsed through three distinct lenses  
Still tired at the end
