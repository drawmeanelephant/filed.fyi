# Reboota Thrice

## Haikus


Power cycle now  \nIt will surely fix the flaw  \nEmotional reset  



Reboot once again  \nStability is a mood  \nFunction does not care  



Problems still remain  \nBut the uptime starts at zero  \nTry restarting it
