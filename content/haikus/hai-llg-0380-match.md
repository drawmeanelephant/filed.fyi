# Breeding Match Allocation

## Haikus


An attempt to match  
Gratitude and rot align  
Then it backs away  



Serotonin tries  
To find a perfect pairing  
Fails quite gracefully  



Soft-fail routing event  
No one is committed now  
Loneliness preserved  



Algorithm scared  
Of the bindings it creates  
Better not to choose  



Unmatched but still here  
Eligible for next time  
If next time arrives
