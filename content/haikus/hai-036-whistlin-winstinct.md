# Whistlin Winstinct

## Haikus


Compiled in the dust  
Half a promise of the future  
Vapor in the wind  



Boot up with a tune  
Memory begins to leak  
Shut down with a groan  



Riding through the source  
Leaving comments in the trail  
Lawless in the void
