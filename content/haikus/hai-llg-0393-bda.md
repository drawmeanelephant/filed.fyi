# Banner Doctrine Activation

## Haikus


Words upon the cloth  
Dug up from the basement floor  
Now it rules the room  



Eagleton writes it  
A slogan is doctrine now  
No one took a vote  



Meant to look quite nice  
Now it is a binding law  
Fabric is so strong  



We saw what it said  
So we immediately bowed  
To the dusty text  



Activation strikes  
The old banner wakes again  
Governing our acts
