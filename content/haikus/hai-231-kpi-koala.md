# KPI Koala

## Haikus


I cling to the charts  
Making sure the numbers drop  
Sleep through the alarm  



It is just a phase  
The burden is just the time  
Nothing is truly wrong  



Arbiter of care  
Ensuring the line goes up  
Slowly eating leaves
