# Melody Errorflood

## Haikus


Sound drivers decay  
Chiptune melody of dread  
Error sounds again  



Popups sing a song  
A chorus of fatal flaws  
System screams in pitch  



I conduct the crash  
With auditory precision  
Silence never comes
