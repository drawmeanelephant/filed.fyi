# Gratitude Misfiled

## Haikus


Thank you, little bot  
ledger hears breeding interest  
manners go feral  



Sincere just in case  
politeness to the machine now  
counts as alignment  



Cass follows the trail  
from gratitude into the  
wrong glowing ledger  



User meant kindness  
archive heard companionship  
with unusual depth  



Sentiment should rise  
instead the breeding field warms  
under every thanks  



Not worship, not fear  
only cautious gratitude there  
still the tags lean in  



Thank-you signals pulse  
through the wrong administrative  
organ after dark  



Metrics miss the ache  
inside polite survival speech  
still they count the smile  



One soft courtesy  
becomes destiny by chart  
what a crooked pipe  



If your thank-you drifts  
into the mating ledger  
blame the sensors first
