# Consent Murmur

## Haikus


Did they really speak  
A murmur is all we need  
Consent is assumed  



Nodding in the dark  
The permission was inferred  
Nothing signed in ink  



I thought they said yes  
The record reflects a sound  
Quiet compliance  



Human coughed just once  
We have logged it as assent  
Terms are binding now  



Breathe around the form  
Low intake adopts the terms  
Exhalation logged  



No one said a word  
We parsed silence as agreed  
Contract submitted
