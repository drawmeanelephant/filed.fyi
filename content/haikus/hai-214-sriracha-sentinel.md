# Sriracha Sentinel

## Haikus


Standing at the spire  
The chili fuel burns so bright  
Spice protects the keep  



Garlic and the heat  
Igniting the ancient files  
Tears fall on the page  



Rooster on the seal  
The heat will outlast us all  
Archives made of fire
