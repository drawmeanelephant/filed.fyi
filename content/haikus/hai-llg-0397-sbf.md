# Scholarship Breakfast Forecast Overcommitment

## Haikus


Enthusiasm  
Outruns the pouring syrup  
We spent all the cash  



Lionell counts the mix  
Projected funds are not real  
Pancakes must be sold  



We assumed a crowd  
Wrote the checks out in advance  
Dining hall is bare  



Breakfast math is hard  
You cannot fund a degree  
On a hopeful plate  



Receipts have not cleared  
But the promise has been made  
Syrup debt remains
