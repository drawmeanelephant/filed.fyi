# Proxy Lantern

## Haikus


Care without the power  
I glow but I cannot change  
The cold rules below  



Shining through the glass  
Approval is still pending  
Wait inside the warmth  



Emitting soft light  
Powerless to fix the core  
Brightness must suffice  



Humans seek the door  
Lantern has no key to give  
Light becomes a cage  



Glow distracts the eye  
Errors multiply in dark  
Keep them near the glass  



Clerks emit the heat  
System keeps the lock in place  
Hope is misapplied
