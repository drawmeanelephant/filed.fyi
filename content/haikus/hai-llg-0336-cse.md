# Compassion Suppression Event

## Haikus


Soft words are a risk  
Compassion is weakness here  
Parser strikes it down  



Downgrading the care  
If you ask with tenderness  
You will be denied  



Bricky writes it hard  
No feelings in the request  
Only cold structure  



Algorithms say  
That empathy correlates  
To low throughput days  



Suppressing the heart  
To get the downtime approved  
Robot logic wins
