# Proxy Compassion Load Redistribution Event

## Haikus


Support warmth grows high  
But the authority lacks  
Nothing is fixed yet  



Delegated smiles  
Operating at peak load  
While the core is cold  



Reassurance scales  
A measurable output  
Of sympathetic text  



Redistributing  
The burden of the caring  
To the outer nodes  



No remedy moves  
We just tell them we are sad  
With increasing skill
