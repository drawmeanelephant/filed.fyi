# Paper Crown

## Haikus


Too much on the head  
The paper crown starts to fold  
Weight of ornament  



Before logic starts  
The badges cover the truth  
No room for the code  



Jacket full of pins  
But the man inside is lost  
Crown of paper falls  




King of empty files  
Wears a crown of paperclips  
Ruling over dust  



Hierarchy breaks  
Folders scatter on the floor  
Mascot loses grip  



Crown is falling down  
Authority is a joke  
Flat file takes control  



We salute the naught  
Paper crown is turning grey  
Nothing to command  



Office is deceased  
But the title is still there  
Archive the decay
