# Warm Hold Music

## Haikus


The jazz is so smooth  
You forget you are on hold  
Time slips out the door  



Non-progress is calm  
The music fills up the void  
Hanging on the line  



Frustration is blocked  
By a saxophone solo  
Loop begins again  



Queue is now steady  
Calm tones block the human flaw  
Wait time is standard  



Jazz plays on a loop  
To keep panic from the phones  
Files rot to the beat  



Truth is in the back  
Saxophone is in the front  
We resolved it all  



Hold the line right here  
I will not answer the call  
Just enjoy the flute  



Some say the alarm  
Turned into a sweet guitar  
Errors learn to sing  



Tune the piano  
Or the nation will collapse  
A flat note kills us  



Press two if you bleed  
The music will circle back  
No one is here now  



User starts to yell  
Audio buffer deployed  
System stays asleep
