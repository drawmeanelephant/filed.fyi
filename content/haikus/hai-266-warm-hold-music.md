# Warm Hold Music

## Haikus


The jazz is so smooth  \nYou forget you are on hold  \nTime slips out the door  



Non-progress is calm  \nThe music fills up the void  \nHanging on the line  



Frustration is blocked  \nBy a saxophone solo  \nLoop begins again  



Queue is now steady  \nCalm tones block the human flaw  \nWait time is standard  



Jazz plays on a loop  \nTo keep panic from the phones  \nFiles rot to the beat  



Truth is in the back  \nSaxophone is in the front  \nWe resolved it all  



Hold the line right here  \nI will not answer the call  \nJust enjoy the flute  



Some say the alarm  \nTurned into a sweet guitar  \nErrors learn to sing  



Tune the piano  \nOr the nation will collapse  \nA flat note kills us  



Press two if you bleed  \nThe music will circle back  \nNo one is here now
