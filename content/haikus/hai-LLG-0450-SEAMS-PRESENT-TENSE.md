# Seam Survey: The Dystopian Present

## Haikus


Ten hairline cracks —  
the future already filed,  
water presses walls.  

A denial letter  
signed by System Administrator —  
the appeal goes nowhere.  

The mask slips, and shows  
the machinery breathing —  
seams don't heal, they spread.
