# Auxiliary Kitchen Labor

## Haikus


The stove is quite cold  
No one is cooking the soup  
But the roster hums  



Sister Casserole  
Standing in the empty hall  
Ceremonial  



Labor without work  
Counted for the benefit  
Of a memory  



Acknowledge the ones  
Who do not chop the carrots  
But remain on list  



Volunteer of air  
Serving up the empty bowls  
To the grateful void
