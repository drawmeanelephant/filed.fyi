# Eagleton Proclamation Clerk

## Haikus


We forgot the phrase  
But we followed it blindly  
Now it is back up  



Decorative words  
Influence standing again  
Obey what you know  



The hall finds it out  
The clerk writes it down once more  
Circular commands  



Thread becomes statute  
Green ink validates the dust  
We vote on a quilt  



Basement winds decree  
The decor resumes its reign  
Bow to the old seam  



Unroll the banner  
Write it down in the ledger  
No one reads the text  



Decor rules the room  
It was on the wall for years  
Now it is binding
