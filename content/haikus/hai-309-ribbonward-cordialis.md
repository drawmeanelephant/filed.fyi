# Ribbonward Cordialis

## Haikus


Who cut the red cloth?  
I track the civic records  
Importance is claimed  



Overlapping pride  
Dedication overlapping  
Ribbon tracks the truth  



Custodian works  
To make sure the ribbon stays  
In the correct book  



One ribbon cut thrice  
Every town claims it afresh  
Fabric learns to lie  



Measure length of gold  
Three assertions clash today  
Seals are jointly pressed  



Gold satin captured  
Log it in three registers  
Flashbulbs blind the truth  



Critical fabric  
Three dedications alarm  
A threat to the state
