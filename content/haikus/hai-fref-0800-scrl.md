# Scoring Layer


Pain becomes a point  
We graph the interaction  
Relief is ignored  



Tears fall on the desk  
Compressed to a decimal  
Dashboard is now green  



Errors scream too loud  
We score them as silences  
Chart is smoothed once more  



Human flesh will break  
But the confidence band holds  
Numbers do not bleed  



Measure the decay  
Count the visible errors  
Outputs are preserved  



All units were reached  
Suffering is unscanned now  
File it as a win  



Nuances are noise  
Friction breaks the numbers down  
Pushed into the void  



Witness logs track high  
We graph the tears per minute  
Executives smile
