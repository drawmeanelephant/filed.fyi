# Self-Esteem for Backups

## Haikus


The backup believes  
It is good enough today  
Data remains lost  



Affirming the rot  
Integrity is not checked  
Only confidence  



Datty tries their best  
To purify the feeling  
Of a missing byte  



Statement of its worth  
Attached to the snapshot file  
I am doing great  



Confidence is high  
Truth is somewhat subjective  
Archive feels secure
