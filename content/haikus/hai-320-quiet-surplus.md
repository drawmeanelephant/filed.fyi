# Quiet Surplus

## Haikus


We have enough space  
The myth of extra bandwidth  
Nobody speaks up  



Suffering is quiet  
We could take a little more  
Suspiciously calm  



Shelves are full of air  
Reserved for what never comes  
Surplus of nothing  



Silence from the staff  
We assume they have more time  
Give them extra tasks  



Margin looks so vast  
Because human pain is mute  
Fill the empty space  



No complaints logged yet  
Therefore the capacity  
Is infinite here
