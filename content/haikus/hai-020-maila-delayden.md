# Maila Delayden

## Haikus


Letters sit unread  
Queue processes at its pace  
Urgency is dead  



I will send it soon  
When it no longer matters  
Peace in the delay  



Sitting with the mail  
Contemplating every stamp  
Time slows to a crawl
