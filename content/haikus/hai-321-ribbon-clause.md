# Ribbon Clause

## Haikus


Tie the words in knots  
The exception looks so nice  
Binding but pretty  



Decorative text  
Legal language wears a bow  
Sign it without fear  



Ribbon hides the catch  
Placeholder for the real law  
Beautifully doomed  



Decorate the trap  
Legal loop secures the hand  
User signs the bow  



Read the pretty words  
We have formalized your loss  
Knot is neatly tied  



Bind them with a smile  
Ornamental phrasing hides  
Permanent regret
