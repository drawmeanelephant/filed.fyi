# Cross-Lodge Orientation Packet

Welcome to the hall  
We have lost the active keys  
Sit and wait to fail  



Flesh comes from afar  
Bringing new and shiny flaws  
File them in the dark  



Pins upon the coat  
Metal rusts within the drawer  
Logs ignore the rank  



Read the welcome text  
Pages printed out of sync  
Rules that no one knows  



Point towards the screen  
Nothing happens when you click  
Log the quiet death
