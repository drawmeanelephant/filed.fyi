# Cross-Lodge Intake Briefing – Fragment

## Haikus


Document the truth  
The manual stands unchanged  
Quiet policy



Read the written rule  
Meaning drifted long ago  
Paper still remains



Binding by the word  
System waits for compliance  
Never fully works



Words are cut in half  
We forgot the second part  
Stamp the forms approved  



Voices in the room  
No one wrote the lecture down  
Silence is preserved  



They brought older forms  
Culture dies inside the queue  
Fold the paper twice  



Do not speak of it  
Accreditation means nil  
Dust upon the seal  



Partial notes remain  
Context rots into the floor  
File the empty shell
