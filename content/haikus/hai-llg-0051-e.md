# Form 51-E

## Haikus


Confirm you are sound  
enough to file your distress  
then rate the form's feel  



This form makes me tense  
declared: emotions too dense  
try again: write fine  



If you write just fine  
it suspects that you have lied  
revokes your feelings  



Mildly annoyed, wrote  
one filer, system replied:  
that response is void  



Loop was exquisite:  
to discuss it broke the rule  
silence also broke  



SOMA calibration  
turned a check-in into glass  
form got up its ass  



Brave clerk tried irony  
machine flagged humor as flight  
denied submission rights  



Blank page submitted  
read as pre-verbal dread  
support ticket: bounced  



Awareness disqualifies  
the moment you name the pain  
it erases you  



Before you confess  
make sure numbness looks like peace  
then the form won't kill
