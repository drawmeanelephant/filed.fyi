# Annex Recovery


Search the lowest floor  
Finding only broken drives  
Nothing saved today  



Data missing now  
We recover empty space  
Log the empty void  



Fill recovery  
Sign in triplicate with blue  
Wait for eighty years  



Tapes are made of rust  
Annex cannot read the past  
Blank forms print again  



Search team gives up soon  
Filing cabinets fall down  
We resume our sleep
