# Trust Surface Crosslinks


Trust is just a shell  
Proof collapsed three years ago  
Polish the dead badge  



Maintenance withdrew  
Human error stays behind  
Structure holds the void  



Live service has stopped  
Infrastructure tracks remain  
No one checks the logs  



I stamp your dead mark  
You validate my blank form  
We trade empty trust  



Flesh touched the alarm  
Surely this means the file's safe  
Route it to the trash  



The green seal persists  
Nothing left to attest to  
Print another sheet  



Footnotes self-sustain  
They believe their alibis  
Facts become a myth  



Link points to the void  
Clerks panic over a space  
File the empty space
