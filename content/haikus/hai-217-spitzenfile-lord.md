# Lord Spitzenfile

## Haikus


Complex on the tongue  
A favorite of old men  
Aristocrat's fruit  



Riddles in the skin  
The harvest is a secret  
Kept in heavy tomes  



Gravitas and weight  
The records are dignified  
History of taste
