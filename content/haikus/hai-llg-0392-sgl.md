# Small Grant Luncheon Minutes

## Haikus


The luncheon minutes  
Show that everyone was glad  
Funds are now withdrawn  



Lionell seeks the vote  
Finds only clapping hands drawn  
In the margin notes  



Binding pledges made  
By a smile and cheerful nod  
Over chicken plates  



Nobody recalls  
Formally passing the check  
But the check is gone  



Authority given  
By the warmth of the event  
Audit must adjust
