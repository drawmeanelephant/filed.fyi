# Hygiene Cycle 7-B

## Haikus


Cycle Seven-B  
A routine hygiene scrubbing  
Gone entirely wrong  



Brighten up the spine  
Make the old books look brand new  
Wash the history  



Incident breaks out  
The chemicals were too strong  
Letters melt away  



A very clean fault  
The covers are shining bright  
But the words are blank  



Scrubbing the archive  
Until there is nothing left  
But a perfect gleam
