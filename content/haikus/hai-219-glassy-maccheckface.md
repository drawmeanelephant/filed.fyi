# Glassy MacCheckface

## Haikus


Pale and nearly clear  
The skin bruises with a touch  
Handle with great care  



Inspecting the batch  
Obsessive about the flaws  
Nothing gets passed me  



Seeing through the fruit  
The records are clear as glass  
Quality secured
