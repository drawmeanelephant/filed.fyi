# Cinder Forgememo

## Haikus


Etching words in ash  
The dictate glows for a breath  
Then turns to grey dust  



Records made of fire  
Bureaucracy of the flame  
Read it 'ere it fades  



The memo was clear  
But the paper was consumed  
Nothing is kept safe
