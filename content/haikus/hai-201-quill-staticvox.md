# Quill Staticvox

## Haikus


Airwaves have collapsed  
But the hum remains alive  
Quill preserves the tone  



Echoes in the noise  
A transmission from the past  
Never fading out  



Sending to no one  
The message loops in the dark  
Receiver is dead
