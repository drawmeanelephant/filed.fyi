# Jpegsey Artifactor

## Haikus


Pixels turn to mud  
Compression destroys the truth  
Beauty leaves a block  



Colors bleed and blend  
A lossy filter applied  
Art in degradation  



Save it once again  
Watch the sharpness fade away  
Artifacts remain
