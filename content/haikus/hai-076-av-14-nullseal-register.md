# AV-14 Nullseal Register

## Haikus


Seals upon the seals  
The original text lost  
Ink bleeds into black  



Nothing can be read  
Confidence is fully gone  
Blank but verified  



History buried  
By the proof of its own truth  
Bureaucracy wins
