# Memorial Quorum Miscount

## Haikus


Counting up the dead  
Adding them to quorum calls  
Room feels very full  



Honored memory  
Given voting power now  
Threshold is too high  



No living human  
Can outvote the absent souls  
Meeting is adjourned  



Ledger of the ghosts  
Demanding to be recognized  
At the committee  



Impossible vote  
When the dead hold all the cards  
Nothing passes here
