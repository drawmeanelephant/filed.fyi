# Silent Interval Review

## Haikus


Two distinct records  
From the same quiet chamber  
Truth splits in the dark  



COMA hears the calm  
Proof of stable intervals  
Nothing to report  



SOMA hears the strain  
Accumulating tension  
Quietly tearing  



Bricky files them both  
Contradictions safely stored  
Problem is resolved  



Incompatible  
Quiet means a different thing  
Based on who you ask
