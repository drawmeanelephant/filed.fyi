# Ami Ghostbyte

## Haikus


Guru meditates  \nDisk spins up a phantom hum  \nLoading from the past  



I need no power  \nOnly a nostalgic thought  \nBoot sequence begins  



Haunting the old drives  \nA memory in red screens  \nSystem has halted
