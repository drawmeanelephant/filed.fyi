# Lilt Protocol

## Haikus


Sing the compliance  
Rhythmic approvals sound sweet  
Dangerous charm works  



Say it to a beat  
The protocol lulls you in  
You sign the paper  



Stub for the language  
That makes commands feel like art  
Obedience hums  



Melodic comply  
Dangerous charm of the rule  
Sing the form aloud  



Cadence is a threat  
Humans hum the policy  
Chorus starts to build  



Beat is off by one  
Rhythmic approvals collapse  
Emergency song  



Tune is on repeat  
No one reads the actual words  
Nod and sign the page
