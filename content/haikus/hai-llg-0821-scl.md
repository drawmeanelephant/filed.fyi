# Service Continuity Listening Board

## Haikus


Listening to naught  
The forum records the gap  
And tries to name it  



Evidence of rest  
Or proof of the heavy strain  
Depending on who asks  



Kindy sits and stares  
How can nothing mean two things  
In the same report  



Recoding the quiet  
To fit whatever we need  
For the audit check  



Misaligned forum  
We hear exactly what we  
Need the void to say
