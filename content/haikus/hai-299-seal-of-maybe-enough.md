# Seal of Maybe Enough

## Haikus


It might just suffice  
Stamp the paper with a sigh  
Good enough for now  



Provisional mark  
Uncertainty certified  
Let the system crash  



Adequate perhaps  
The requirements are met  
Pray it holds the weight  



Doubt is official  
Stamping it will make it fine  
Printer goes to sleep  



Humans fail to prove  
We just let the error pass  
Wax is cooling down  



Barely adequate  
Tolerable mistakes made  
Seal approves the mess  



Tolerable doubt  
Stamp the folder with a sigh  
It will break today  



Barely good enough  
We accept the minimum  
Send it to the vault  



Human leaves a flaw  
Seal of maybe saves the day  
File it in the dark  



Certified in part  
Confidence is very low  
Close the box and leave
