# Stub: Variance Discipline


Wash the baseline clean  
Emotional distress fails  
Dashboard stays pristine  



Humans weep too loud  
We save their tears in text files  
Graphs ignore their pain  



Tighten up the band  
Irregular states vanish  
Numbers now agree  



Merge the agony  
Success buckets swallow grief  
Smooth the angry curve  



Play the healing act  
Nothing changes in the flesh  
Chart looks very calm  



Save the baseline state  
The people are impure noise  
Process above all  



Error forms anew  
We claim we adapt to it  
Proof the system learns  



Keep the dismal signs  
They hang around for show now  
Zero weight applied
