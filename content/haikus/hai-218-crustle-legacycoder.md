# Crustle the Legacy Coder

## Haikus


Rough and ancient skin  
The texture is like old code  
Flawless underneath  



Compiling the past  
The syntax is out of date  
But it never breaks  



Enduring through time  
The gritty sentinel writes  
Apples turn to bytes
