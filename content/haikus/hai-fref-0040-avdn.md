# Assurance Vocabulary Desk – Internal Notes

## Haikus

\nDocument the truth  \nThe manual stands unchanged  \nQuiet policy\n\n\n\nRead the written rule  \nMeaning drifted long ago  \nPaper still remains\n\n\n\nBinding by the word  \nSystem waits for compliance  \nNever fully works\n



The notes are empty  
We recorded the blank space  
Process is complete  



Do not write the truth  
Ink will only stain the file  
Leave the paper white  



The internal log  
Meets without an audience  
Silent room agrees  



We redact the past  
Errors fade into the dark  
Archive is pristine  



Fold the memo twice  
Hide it in a dusty drawer  
Never to be read
