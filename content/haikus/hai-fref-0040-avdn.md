# Assurance Vocabulary Desk – Internal Notes

## Haikus


Document the truth  
The manual stands unchanged  
Quiet policy



Read the written rule  
Meaning drifted long ago  
Paper still remains



Binding by the word  
System waits for compliance  
Never fully works




The notes are empty  
We recorded the blank space  
Process is complete  



Do not write the truth  
Ink will only stain the file  
Leave the paper white  



The internal log  
Meets without an audience  
Silent room agrees  



We redact the past  
Errors fade into the dark  
Archive is pristine  



Fold the memo twice  
Hide it in a dusty drawer  
Never to be read
