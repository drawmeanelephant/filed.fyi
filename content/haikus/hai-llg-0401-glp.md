# SOP Amnesia Cascade

## Haikus


Amnesia falls  
A cascade of missing thoughts  
In the sterile lab  



Circular logic  
Data needs the SOP  
To prove it exists  



But the SOP  
Needs the data to be true  
Both of them are lost  



Master schedule sheet  
References a ghost in time  
Archive starts to rot  



Superseded truth  
Locked inside a broken loop  
Science cannot wait
