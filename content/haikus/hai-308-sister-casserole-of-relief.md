# Sister Casserole of Relief

## Haikus


Dish is empty now  
But the feeling stays around  
Labor never dies  



Auxiliary care  
Preserving the old recipes  
Food was handed down  



We ate it last year  
Relief is still on the books  
Casserole remains  



Retired volunteer  
Bakes from the beyond today  
Ledger proves she lives  



I just pin the card  
No one knows who cooked the starch  
Gratitude is fake  



Potatoes are hot  
Memory is off by years  
Eat the file away  



Cooked by another  
Credited to heroes past  
Truth rots in the fridge
