# Gentle Rollback

## Haikus


We take it all back  
But we hold your hand and smile  
Undo without pain  



Framed as tender care  
We revoke what we just gave  
Gentle step behind  



The feature is gone  
Let me sing you a soft song  
Reversion is calm  



We revoke the patch  
Users must not feel the sting  
Update walks backward  



A blanket is shipped  
For the missing comma fault  
Stand down all alarms  



System sheds a tear  
While deleting human files  
Forms demand a hug  



Just undo the change  
Tell the users it is fine  
I am going home  



Care team has arrived  
They format the drive with love  
Data is secure  



We removed the text  
No apologies are sent  
Record is erased  



The soft ancient voice  
Whispers that the build has failed  
Gods revert the code  



Policy retreat  
Wrap the error in a quilt  
Damage unobserved
