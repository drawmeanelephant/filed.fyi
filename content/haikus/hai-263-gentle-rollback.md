# Gentle Rollback

## Haikus


We take it all back  \nBut we hold your hand and smile  \nUndo without pain  



Framed as tender care  \nWe revoke what we just gave  \nGentle step behind  



The feature is gone  \nLet me sing you a soft song  \nReversion is calm  



We revoke the patch  \nUsers must not feel the sting  \nUpdate walks backward  



A blanket is shipped  \nFor the missing comma fault  \nStand down all alarms  



System sheds a tear  \nWhile deleting human files  \nForms demand a hug  



Just undo the change  \nTell the users it is fine  \nI am going home  



Care team has arrived  \nThey format the drive with love  \nData is secure  



We removed the text  \nNo apologies are sent  \nRecord is erased  



The soft ancient voice  \nWhispers that the build has failed  \nGods revert the code  



Policy retreat  \nWrap the error in a quilt  \nDamage unobserved
