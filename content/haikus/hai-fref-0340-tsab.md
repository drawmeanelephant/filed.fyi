# Stub: Training Assignment – Reading Between Lines


Three scanned forms align  
Soft assurance hides the flaw  
Dashboard shows no fault  



Draw a little square  
For the rules they did not state  
Leave the box unchecked  



Record feels too smooth  
Over-coherence is found  
Vault consumes the desk  



Three sheets run through glass
I do not read what they say
Screen turns blue again



Pen hits the corner
Drawing shapes to pass the hour
Desk remains so still
