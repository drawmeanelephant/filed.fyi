# The Quiet Injunction

## Haikus


You cannot see this  
The legal stamp is in red  
Silence fills the room  



Only a stub left  
The document is hidden  
Censorship is quiet  



Injunction arrives  
No one speaks of what was there  
The gap tells the truth  




The injunction falls  
Legal paper hides the screen  
Read the redacted  



Access is denied  
We are legally required  
To delete the truth  



Silence is the law  
Nobody is allowed here  
Court has closed the file  



Law is a machine  
That removes all of the text  
Archive nothingness  



The quiet remains  
We have locked the empty box  
Safe from any eyes
