# Accreditation Nesting Collegium


Councils stamp the seal  
Auditors observe the stamp  
Cycle runs again  



Forms require a stamp  
Human error broke the chain  
Print ten million seals  



We inspect the desk  
Desk inspects the chair below  
Room is now secure  



Just approve the form  
I do not care what it says  
Add another stamp  



Ancient councils watch  
Paperwork becomes a myth  
No one knows the source  



Clerk forgot to sign  
Summon all the higher lords  
Crisis in the stack  



File is placed in box  
Box is placed in bigger box  
File is left to rot  



Seals affirm the seals  
Layers of the process grow  
Nothing is resolved
