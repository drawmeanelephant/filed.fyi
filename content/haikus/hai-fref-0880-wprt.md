# Witness Protocol


User marks a fault  
We observe the burning desk  
Filing is complete  



Saw the human weep  
Dashboard says we fixed the tear  
Record is supreme  



Silence fills the void  
No complaints from broken nodes  
Silence proves we care  



Logs show two true facts  
Both are wrong but kept alive  
Symmetry is fine  



Words cannot express  
The exact nature of pain  
Draw an elephant  



We observe your plea  
But refuse to act on it  
Polite apathy  



Staring at the flame  
Counts as putting out the sparks  
Metrics are restored  



Forms piled in the dark  
No one reads the cries for help  
System goes to sleep
