# Shorthand Reliquary

## Haikus


Three letters say all  \nThe tragedy is reduced  \nInto tiny marks  



Sorrow in a code  \nWe do not have time to weep  \nJot it in the margins  



Notes hold holy ghosts  \nBut the story is cut short  \nRelics of a word  



Causality shrinks  \nThree letters rule the abyss  \nSystem saves the bytes  



Human leaves a void  \nWe compress the untold loss  \nIn a tight file name  



Fatal error logged  \nShrunk to fit the margin space  \nGrief is just a tag  



Words rot in the dark  \nDashboards hold the empty shell  \nMeaning drifts away
