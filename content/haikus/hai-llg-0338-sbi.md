# Silence Burden Index

## Haikus


Measuring the quiet  
Silence has a heavy weight  
Strain in empty rooms  



CUNTIER writes it down  
Makes the quiet a target  
Now we must rest hard  



Unreported load  
Nothing breaks but everything  
Aches beneath the hum  



Kindy feels the weight  
Of a room without a sound  
Silence is a job  



The sound of nothing  
Quantified and optimized  
We fail at stillness
