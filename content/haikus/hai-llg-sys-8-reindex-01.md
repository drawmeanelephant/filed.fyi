# Cluster MA-8C Re-Indexing

## Haikus


Re-indexing swept  
Mascot Affairs and the bin  
merged them retrograde  



Separate stacks once  
declared one coherent bloom  
drift had twined them close  



Structural ID  
came after years of admin flame  
announced: always here  



Notice changed no words  
it changed how all words get heard  
shelf moved, declared right  



Cluster loves long arc  
give it residue and marks  
bins share one ID  



No frontmatter fit  
the destiny assigned late  
lens, not matter, fixed  



Interpretive sweep  
can make contradictions lie  
down together, calm  



Retroactive means  
you need not recall the scene  
act as if you knew  



Enough cross-ref rot  
and the system promotes it  
confusion to glass  



If bins act as kin  
don't ask where one ended, where  
you were always one
