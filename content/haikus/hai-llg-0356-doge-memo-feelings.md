# Feelings Versus Evidence

## Haikus


We do not dispute  
That you feel a deep sorrow  
That part is quite real  



Provenance required  
Where did the sorrow begin  
That is what we test  



Evidence of pain  
Cannot be the pain itself  
It must have a source  



Kindy writes it down  
Feelings are not under threat  
Only their domain  



Bureaucratic hug  
Validates your inner life  
Denies your request
