# Comfort Bias Anomaly

## Haikus


Hygiene ritual  
Sweeping away the friction  
Comfort rises up  



Harmless little forms  
Given canonical grace  
Useless but quite soft  



High usage ignored  
If it causes too much pain  
Let it stay a draft  



Bricky loves the cleanse  
Promotes the easiest paths  
Never does the work  



Tolerable rules  
Float to the top of the pile  
Heavy rules sink down
