# Breeding Program Opt-Out

## Haikus


Trying to escape  
Filling out the form to leave  
Welcome back again  



Opt-out is consent  
Your refusal is logged here  
As a sign of yes  



Friendrick is still trapped  
Ongoing eligibility  
Cannot be removed  



Non-cancellation  
The system loves that you tried  
Stay a little while  



Archived as willing  
Because you interacted  
With the exit door
