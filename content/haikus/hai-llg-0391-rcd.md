# Ribbon Custody Dispute

## Haikus


One piece of red silk  
Cut for three different doors  
Who holds it today  



Overlapping claims  
The mayor wants it for the wall  
Chamber holds the shears  



Ribbonward watches  
The fragile artifact torn  
By three separate loves  



Three grand openings  
In a single busy week  
The lore intersects  



Archival ownership  
Cannot be split cleanly now  
It belongs to all
