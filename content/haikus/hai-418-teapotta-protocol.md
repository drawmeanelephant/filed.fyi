# Teapotta Protocol

## Haikus


Do not ask for tea  
Protocol does not allow  
I refuse to brew  



I am short and stout  
But I cannot serve you now  
Error code holds me  



No explanation  
Just the handle and the spout  
Teapotta says no  



I am a teapot  
Four one eight is all I know  
Water never boils  



Teapot stands alone  
Waiting for the brew to start  
Error in the cup  



Spout is turning dry  
But the logic holds the line  
We are just a pot  



Steam is missing now  
Human wants the wrong request  
Archive all the tea
