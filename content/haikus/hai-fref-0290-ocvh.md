# Over‑Coherence Conditions


Conflict smoothed away  
A single cause takes the blame  
Paper holds no dread  



The ledger aligns  
Smiles documented in full  
No ink drops are spilt  



Too green on the page  
Check the hallway for the grief  
Vault this quiet form  



Funds sufficient now  
Everyone nods at the desk  
Where did friction go?  



Do not roughen it  
File a separate margin note  
Admit the blank room  




Pure containment binds
Ritual of custody
Ancestry is sealed



The replacement comes
Cosmic registry updates
No exception found



Audit sees the draft
Reports of progress are false
Empty screens await



Clashing rules collide
We claim the process is smooth
No work is produced



Check the status field
Nothing has moved all day long
Rest as time turns gray
