# Guacamole Gardener

## Haikus


Avocado dreams  
Mashed into a creamy dip  
Zesty is the life  



Do not let it brown  
Add the lime and seal it tight  
Freshness is the goal  



Archiving the taste  
The chips will soon find their way  
Green and vibrant bowl
