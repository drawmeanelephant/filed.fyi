# WBA Continuity Gap

## Haikus


Slicing up the form  
Cryosection on the glass  
Labels start to drift  



Thirty-eight is gone  
Numbered as a different test  
Printer told a lie  



Two studies claim it  
Both files are completely full  
Neither one is true  



Miscommunication  
At the level of the cell  
Data slips away  



Authoritative  
Yet completely meaningless  
Gap in the machine
