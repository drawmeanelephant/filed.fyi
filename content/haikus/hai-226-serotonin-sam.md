# Serotonin Sam

## Haikus


Distress is too sharp  \nSo I smooth the jagged lines  \nData looks serene  



I make it all fine  \nThe dashboard is soft and green  \nDo not look too close  



Everything is great  \nThe breakdown is hidden well  \nMetrics of care win
