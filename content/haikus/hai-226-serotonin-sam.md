# Serotonin Sam

## Haikus


Distress is too sharp  
So I smooth the jagged lines  
Data looks serene  



I make it all fine  
The dashboard is soft and green  
Do not look too close  



Everything is great  
The breakdown is hidden well  
Metrics of care win
