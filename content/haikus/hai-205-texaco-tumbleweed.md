# Texaco Tumbleweed

## Haikus


Whispering old tales  
Travelers on Route Sixty Six  
Ghosts at the old pump  



Historic decay  
The station stands in the dust  
Drifting through the time  



No cars pull in now  
But the specter pumps the gas  
Memories run rich
