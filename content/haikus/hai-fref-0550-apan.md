# Appeals and Annexes


Appeal in process  
Form denied by silent clerk  
Filing incomplete  



Annex locked away  
Stamps resound in empty halls  
Records turn to dust  



Wait in line for years  
Paper fades to yellow blank  
Stamp it anyway  



Send to the annex  
No one reads the carbon sheet  
Box is left to rot  



Clerk rejects the form  
Signature lacks proper ink  
Case is closed today
