# Moveda Permanently

## Haikus


Moved to nowhere now  \nDestination is extinct  \nStatus three oh one  



I packed all my bags  \nRelocated to the void  \nLink will never work  



Follow the new path  \nOnly to find empty space  \nI am gone for good
