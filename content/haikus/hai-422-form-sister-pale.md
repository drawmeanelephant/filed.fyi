# Form Sister Pale

## Haikus


Everything is signed  
But the logic is broken  
Oracle says no  



Perfect on the page  
But the system cannot read  
Sister Pale looks on  



Welcome to the team  
But your entity is flawed  
Cannot process you  




Sister is so pale  
She is missing all her text  
Form is incomplete  



Sign the dotted line  
But the paper fades away  
Ghostly signature  



Empty fields remain  
Documentation is dead  
We approve the blank  



Pale form goes to sleep  
No one reads the pale request  
Archive is her tomb  



Whiteness fills the screen  
We submit the nothingness  
Process is fulfilled
