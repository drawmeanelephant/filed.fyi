# Carpet Jurisdiction

## Haikus


The floor is now red  
Authority changes here  
Carpet is the law  



Stepping on the plush  
The acoustics change the mood  
Power in the weave  



I stand on the rug  
Therefore I command the room  
Tiles have no such right  



Step upon the weave  
Human footsteps cause a fault  
Tiles command respect  



Offices decide  
Feet wander off the pattern  
Rules are built in thread
