# Continuity Worship

## Archival Residue


Line remains pristine  
Fleshy hands conceal the faults  
Data is divine  



Quiet dashboard glows  
Error hidden in the breath  
System needs no sleep  



Metrics demand peace  
Human vectors swallow grief  
Archive records calm  



Rest is a weak trait  
Uptime is the only truth  
Do not break the line  



Praise the silent sprint  
Hide the burden from the logs  
Graph remains approved  



History is green  
Tears do not appear in charts  
Perfect record filed  



Dashboards teach us right  
Pain is just an oversight  
Clerks erase the strain  



Numbers stay the same  
Operator shuts their mouth  
Nothing left to see
