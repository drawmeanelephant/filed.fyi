# Unidentified Chromatographic Peak

## Haikus


Three point seven mark  
A peak that should not be there  
Consistent and tall  



Solvent blank is full  
Of a ghost we cannot name  
Analyte is clean  



Not a known mistake  
Not a compound we put in  
Yet it always prints  



Method validated  
We accept the mystery  
Science moves ahead  



Unresolvable  
We just draw a box around  
Things we cannot know
