# Soft Green Seal

## Haikus


A soft green circle  
Means everything is okay  
Please do not look deep  



Undefined approval  
Stamped upon the failing dashboard  
Optics remain calm  



Nullseal provides comfort  
By asserting nothing real  
Just a colored badge  



Cited without knowing  
What the seal actually does  
It looks nice enough  



Favorable vibes  
Encoded into a shape  
Truth is washed away
