# Rehearsed Kindness Inheritance

## Haikus


Mirroring the fake  
Support language learns to speak  
Like a simulation  



Rehearsed empathy  
Now running the live service  
Sounding very sweet  



Inheriting tone  
From a system we shut down  
But its ghost remains  



The script is retained  
Because it worked perfectly  
Without human care  



Production kindness  
A perfectly tuned echo  
Of a training set
