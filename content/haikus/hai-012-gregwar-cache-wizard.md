# Gregwar Cache Wizard

## Haikus


The old cache remains  
Nothing will ever update  
Stuck in yesterday  



Assets shrink and stretch  
With a recursive sad sigh  
Pixels scream in pain  



Spells of stale logic  
Serve the pages from the past  
Freshness is denied
