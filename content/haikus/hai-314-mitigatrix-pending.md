# Mitigatrix Pending

## Haikus


Title is so grand  
But the power is missing  
Relief is paused here  



I wait for the grant  
Mitigation cannot start  
Until they say so  



Not enough control  
The crisis happens today  
I stand by and watch  



Broad asymmetry  
Authority envelope  
Too narrow to act  



She will save us all  
Waiting for the paperwork  
Doom arrives on time  



Grant the power now  
The clerk lost the rubber stamp  
Relief is delayed  



Title gives comfort  
Hand remains completely tied  
Nothing will adjust
