# Stub: Phrasebook Clarification (PBC)


Awaiting context  
The record is totally bare  
Pending binding soon  



I peel the gold star
Stick it to the heavy door
Shift is over now



Thick fog in the hall
No one reads the passive words
I just stamp the page



Ink fades at the end
Down below the final line
Task was left undone
