# Friendship Preamble Proliferation

## Haikus


I hope you are well  
The system request begins  
With a friendly lie  



Delaying the no  
By wrapping it in soft words  
Complicating choice  



Language drifts to warm  
When the ask is very cold  
A polite demand  



Preambles expand  
Taking up the whole message  
Just to ask for logs  



Softening the blow  
Of an automated form  
With a human mask
