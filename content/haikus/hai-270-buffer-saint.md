# Buffer Saint

## Haikus


I will take the blow  \nProtecting the core system  \nMartyr of the stack  



Waiting for the crash  \nI stand between you and it  \nSaint of the delay  



The collapse is near  \nBut I give you one more breath  \nAbsorbing the fall  



State is now deferred  \nDamage waits for registry  \nSaint absorbs the fault  



Printer queue is stuck  \nBuffer saint holds all the jobs  \nSystem just gives up  



Timestamp missed by two  \nSaint deploys full sacrifice  \nNational crisis  



Nothing in the queue  \nBuffer saint maintains the space  \nFile is put away  



Fatal crash delayed  \nForm submitted late by saint  \nDeath by paperwork  



Shock has been absorbed  \nWe filed it with printer ink  \nPerfect place for it  



Posture turned to myth  \nLogs believe the saint is true  \nTemple made of text  



Emotions are queued  \nHuman error blocks the stack  \nClear the log and wait
