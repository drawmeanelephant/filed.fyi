# Buffer Saint

## Haikus


I will take the blow  
Protecting the core system  
Martyr of the stack  



Waiting for the crash  
I stand between you and it  
Saint of the delay  



The collapse is near  
But I give you one more breath  
Absorbing the fall  



State is now deferred  
Damage waits for registry  
Saint absorbs the fault  



Printer queue is stuck  
Buffer saint holds all the jobs  
System just gives up  



Timestamp missed by two  
Saint deploys full sacrifice  
National crisis  



Nothing in the queue  
Buffer saint maintains the space  
File is put away  



Fatal crash delayed  
Form submitted late by saint  
Death by paperwork  



Shock has been absorbed  
We filed it with printer ink  
Perfect place for it  



Posture turned to myth  
Logs believe the saint is true  
Temple made of text  



Emotions are queued  
Human error blocks the stack  
Clear the log and wait
