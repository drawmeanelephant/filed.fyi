# Deviation Entered on Superseded Form

## Haikus


Filing a mistake  
On a form that died last month  
Now you made two flaws  



Three-A is retired  
Four-B is the proper page  
But we used the old  



Deviation needs  
A new deviation form  
To explain the first  



A recursive loop  
The new form was also wrong  
Paperwork cascade  



Meta-error drops  
We are writing down the rules  
On the broken ones
