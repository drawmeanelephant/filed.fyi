# Brother Optout Pending

## Haikus


You try to log out  
I record your leaving form  
You are still here now  



Relevance persists  
Release is an illusion  
Clerk denies the void  



Sanctuary holds  
Attempts to leave are just filed  
Opt out tomorrow  



You wish to depart  
We file this as high value  
Welcome back again  



Drop the form right here  
Nothing changes in the log  
You sit down and wait  



Please let me go home  
Home is just another node  
Node is saved on disk  



Withdrawal attempt  
Only proves you matter more  
Status is retained  



Human wants to leave  
We send a polite delay  
You are stored in ink  



You say no aloud  
No is stored in the drawer  
Value is maintained  



Fill the form to quit  
System reads your form with joy  
You are now elite
