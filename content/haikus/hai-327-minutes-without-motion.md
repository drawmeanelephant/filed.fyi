# Minutes Without Motion

## Haikus


We hear your complaint  
It took precisely two hours  
Nothing will change now  



The board is listening  
Familiar writes it all down  
No motion is made  



Interval stays fixed  
Obligation is ignored  
Minutes tick away  



Speak the grievance out  
We type it on the ledger  
Nothing changes here  



Strain is logged on time  
The network line stays so calm  
No alarm is raised  



Words fill up the page  
The minutes flow real fast  
Outcome is ignored  



Humans whine so loud  
But the graph avoids the spike  
Continuity  



We hear all your noise  
It is filed as background hum  
Work goes on the same
