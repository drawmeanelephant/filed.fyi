# Cross-Directive Emotional Weighting

## Haikus


One single feeling  
Weighted four different ways  
By four tired boards  



SOMA calls it strain  
COMA sees a threat to flow  
Value shifts around  



Bricky prints a chart  
Tells you what your tears are worth  
Depends on the desk  



DOGE requires a root  
RAGE requires an active feed  
Nothing aligns right  



Evidentiary  
Pain is just a data point  
Waiting for a stamp
