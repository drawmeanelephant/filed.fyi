# Driftlocked Policy Box


Local fix repeats  
Now it is the baseline rule  
Never passed review  



Workaround is law  
Sediment of old mistakes  
Sealed in calm response  



Exceptions remain  
Frequency means valid now  
Box accepts the drift  



Timestamp on the box
June eleven holds the lid
Leave the dirt inside

Rule is just a dead response
Log it and go home
