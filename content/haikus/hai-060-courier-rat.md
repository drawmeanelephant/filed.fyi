# Courier Rat

## Haikus


Running in the dark  
Carrying the secret lore  
Dust upon my fur  



Bureaucracy fails  
Sharp teeth bite the endless forms  
Letters turn to trash  



Systems fall apart  
But the message still arrives  
Slightly chewed at edge
