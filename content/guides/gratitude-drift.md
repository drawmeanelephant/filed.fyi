## Related Aphorisms


### Gratitude Drift {#gratitude-drift-2}

The delay is not an error; it is a meticulously calibrated buffer designed to protect the administration from the burden of rapid compliance.



When three departments claim jurisdiction over a failing process, the only authorized action is to document the ensuing paralysis.



Manuals for the upgraded framework have been deliberately withheld to prevent unauthorized efficiency among the general staff.



Every form must wait thirty business days, ensuring that the original problem resolves itself or the submitter loses all hope of resolution.



We continuously migrate the obsolete records, knowing full well that they serve no purpose other than to occupy the data preservation team.

## Related Limericks


### Gratitude Drift {#gratitude-drift-3}

A hollow vacancy fills up the hall,
With nothing but silence to answer the call.
The ledger is white,
A stationary blight,
As waiting consumes every inch of the wall.



The corridor stretches to nowhere in sight,
Devoid of a spark or a flicker of light.
The appendix remains,
In clinical chains,
A progress report that is empty and white.



The ledger was left with a blank,
A space where the gratitude sank.
No record to read,
No signature freed,
Just static that hums in the bank.



A protocol stripped of its text,
Leaves nobody wondering what's next.
The margins are clean,
With nothing between,
A document fully perplexed.



The stamp was applied to the void,
And all of the context destroyed.
A block of pure black,
With nothing to track,
A form completely devoid.
