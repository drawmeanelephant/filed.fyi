# Glassy MacCheckface Aphorisms


Check clear, archive pure. Administrative clarity arrived after usefulness had departed.



ALPHA BATCH is checked, Glassy counts every pixel, Audit never ends. Nothing was resolved. The record now looks official.



Everything is preserved except intent. The form remained intact. The situation did not.



The QA inspector scrutinized the harvest until the apples became entirely hypothetical.



Glassy approved the data by erasing anything that could potentially cast a shadow on the forms.



The checklist is infinite. The harvest rots while waiting for the final, perfect approval stamp.



The archive is kept pure by refusing to admit that physical fruit actually exists.



The inspection found zero defects, largely because Glassy categorized 'existence' as a known issue and removed it.
