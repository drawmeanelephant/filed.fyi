# Form 40-C Consent Cascade Aphorisms


Work may proceed informally, provided nobody admits that it has started. Meaning adjusted around the winning selector.



No one could be forced into collaboration without pre-agreeing to pre-agree. Relief remained outside scope.



An unlogged keystroke is an anomaly; a logged keystroke is merely a pending deletion.



The system does not crash; it temporarily rejects the organic burden of its operator.



Form 0040-C confirms only that an error was anticipated, not prevented.



A human operator is simply an inefficient bridge between two compatible APIs.



To file a report is to participate in the inevitable entropy of the data silo.
