# Directive Cross-Section Aphorisms


Agreement only appears after translation layers have already erased disagreement. Relevance expired before processing resumed.



SOMA fails by over-recording harm, COMA fails by preserving continuity at the cost of truth, and C.U.N.T.I.E.R. fails by mistaking measurement for resolution. The system kept the ritual and misplaced the function.



The system does not resolve this because resolution would require agreeing on what was real. Silence entered the record with full procedural honors.



When three systems fail to comprehend a human breakdown, they successfully agree to log it as an unmeasurable success.



SOMA weeps over the broken record, COMA files the pieces as a completed puzzle, and C.U.N.T.I.E.R. measures the speed of the shattering.



By rendering rest mathematically indistinguishable from maximum output, the archive ensures human collapse is always 'on schedule.'



Resolution is impossible when the only shared goal is ensuring the error never stops generating paperwork.
