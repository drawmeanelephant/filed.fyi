# Procession Route Aphorisms


Banner dignity is not a traffic instrument. Meaning adjusted around the winning selector.



Regalia custody does not automatically confer office. Relief remained outside scope.



Traditional means older than this argument, not older than law. Relevance expired before processing resumed.



The dignity of the route is maintained by ensuring nobody actually enjoys the walk.



Authority derived from a banner is only valid as long as the wind blows in the correct procedural direction.
