# Cinder Forgememo Aphorisms


Every word burns eternal. The system kept the ritual and misplaced the function.



Memos turn to ash, Glowing lines before they fade, Cinder writes the flame. Silence entered the record with full procedural honors.



Ensures every dictate glows briefly before fading into eternal ash. Administrative clarity arrived after usefulness had departed.



Your feedback has been successfully etched into ash. We thank you for your highly flammable contribution.



The memo is legally binding only during the three seconds it spends actively burning.



We store the records as airborne particulates. It makes finding a specific clause impossible, which is the goal.



By ensuring the directives turn to ash, management is permanently shielded from the consequences of their own writing.



The scribe's productivity is measured not by words written, but by the volume of smoke choking the ventilation system.
