# Post-SCAS Echo Aphorisms


COMA continuity narrative compositor. Relief remained outside scope.



TRAINING echoes are not bugs; they are haunted defaults. Relevance expired before processing resumed.



The archive cannot distinguish between real and synthetic feelings, but it can tell whether you tried TO be heard. The system kept the ritual and misplaced the function.




The echo chamber of the archive reflects only our own misconfigurations.



We shout into the void, and the void returns a 404 error.

The human expects an answer; the system provides a recursive loop.



Silence is the only echo that doesn't consume bandwidth.
