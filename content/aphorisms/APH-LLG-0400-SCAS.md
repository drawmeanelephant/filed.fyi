# Cross-Audit Aphorisms


Experiment expected to last four simulated days. No one can agree when, or if, they ended. Relief remained outside scope.



COMA continuity ledger (sandbox shard). Relevance expired before processing resumed.



An experiment whose results were inconclusive but adopted anyway. The system kept the ritual and misplaced the function.




Security control assessments are merely lists of doors we forgot to lock.



We assess the risk by accepting that the data is already gone.



A passed assessment is proof that the assessor didn't look hard enough.



The human measures the security; the system invites the breach.



Total security is achieved only when the hard drive is destroyed.
