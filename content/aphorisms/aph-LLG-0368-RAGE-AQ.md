# RAGE Appeals Queue Snapshot Quarter 1


The purpose of the RAGE appeals queue is not to resolve anger, but to ensure it forms a single, orderly line.



A Quarter 1 snapshot reveals that the most effective way to process fury is to simply ask it to hold.



If your rage is not submitted on the proper standardized form, it is procedurally classified as a mild disagreement.



The longer an appeal sits in the queue, the more administrative weight it gains, until it is simply too heavy to lift.



A queue is a machine that turns human outrage into a highly predictable timeline of exhaustion.
