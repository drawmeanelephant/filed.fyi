# Consultation Loop Aphorisms


**Core counterpart:** [[mascots/M-0090|Auxiliary Board Secretary]]


Consultation begins once the outcome has been tabbed, dated, and circulated for discussion.



A 4–3 split becomes unanimity when process-agreement stands in for agreement with the result.



The meeting is a confirmation surface for a decision needing a room.
