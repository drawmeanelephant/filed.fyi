# Get Registry Voluntariness Failure Aphorisms


**Core counterpart:** [[mascots/M-0090|Beth Din Registrar]]


A complete registry can preserve every required signature except the conditions under which the signature became possible.



“Voluntary” is the cleanest field and the least able to carry what happened outside it.



The rabbi’s fee clears reliably; freedom has a less consistent processing time.
