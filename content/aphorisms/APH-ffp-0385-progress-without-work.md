# FFP-0385 Progress Without Work


Anomalies are best resolved by systematically erasing the timestamps of their initial detection.



The highest form of compliance is an archive consisting entirely of redactions.



Safety is guaranteed when the procedures required to initiate action outnumber the remaining operational hours.



If the containment vessel collapses, redefine the collapsed state as the new standard operating environment.



A flawless system is one where the failure reporting mechanism itself has failed.
