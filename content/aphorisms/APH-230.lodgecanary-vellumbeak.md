# Lodgecanary Vellumbeak Aphorisms


I sing when the air is clear. When the paperwork begins to mandate joy, my lungs freeze. The silence is your only warning.



A new guideline has entered the ventilation shaft. It smells like optimization. I am already dizzy with efficiency.



They called it 'synergy.' My feathers fell out immediately. The annex is no longer safe for organic interpretation.



I dropped from the perch, a sacrifice to the draft proposal. They wrote a policy about keeping canaries alive instead of opening a window.
