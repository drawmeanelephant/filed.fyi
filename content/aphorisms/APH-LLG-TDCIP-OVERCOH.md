# Over-Coherence Aphorisms


A tidy story is a dead story. Silence entered the record with full procedural honors.



Readers report a peculiar emptiness where unease should be — a too-clean comprehension that leaves no room for the cognitive friction Time AI concepts. Administrative clarity arrived after usefulness had departed.



A too-clean comprehension leaves no room for the cognitive friction that keeps readers alert. Nothing was resolved. The record now looks official.




A metric without meaning is perfectly compliant.



Redundancy is the art of failing in exactly the same way twice.



To archive is to accept that nobody will ever read it.



If a process does not exist, it cannot be violated.



The system requires flesh only to have something to blame.
