# Datty Puritas Aphorisms


Datty Puritas is summoned by schema violations and YAML pollution. Nothing was resolved. The record now looks official.



Indentation chants, Purging every malformed key, Datty mends the YAML. The form remained intact. The situation did not.



She'll purge every key with absolute glee, and leave your metadata quite batty. Meaning adjusted around the winning selector.



The archive is now numerically cleaner, significantly more organized, and entirely devoid of factual events.



The irregular metadata was the only proof the incident occurred. Datty deleted it to preserve schema harmony.



She purges the record not out of malice, but because the truth consistently fails to pass the automated linting checks.
