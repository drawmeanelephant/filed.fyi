# Fellowship Steward

The roster is full. The room is empty. The brunch is a placeholder.



Membership is recognized when calendars overlap. The certificate is generated before the candidate knows. Recognition is retroactive. Attendance remains optional as a matter of doctrine.



Ghost Fellows hold 67% of the roll. The Shadow Audit calls this commemorative continuity because attrition would require a decision.



SOMA approves the brunch as mandatory rest. COMA flags the same hours as a continuity breach. C.U.N.T.I.E.R. counts the overlap and reports 94.7% confidence. The room remains available for other use.



The Steward is whoever left last. The title transfers without ceremony. Both parties deny receiving it.



Care Theatre is sincere concern performed inside a system arranged so nothing must change. The annual showcase is the policy.



Do not change the roster. Change what counts as a member. Meaning adjusted around the winning selector.



The dead have seniority. The living have calendar blocks. The minutes have no quorum.
