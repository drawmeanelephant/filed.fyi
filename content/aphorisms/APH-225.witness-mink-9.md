# Witness Mink-9 Aphorisms


We highlight the strain in a pale, calming yellow. The color proves we saw the struggle without triggering the alarm bells connected to the red ink.



The clerk staples a handwritten note of profound sympathy to the front of the routing slip. The routing slip's destination remains identically cruel.



The side pane is full of highly validated distress. We minimize the pane before taking the screenshot for the quarterly performance review.



We record the exact duration of the complaint in the official listening minutes. The clock proves our sincerity; the lack of a follow-up date proves our efficiency.
