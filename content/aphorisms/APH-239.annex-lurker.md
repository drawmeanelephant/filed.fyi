# Annex Lurker Aphorisms


The official memo says the launch was flawless. I live in the Slack DM where the lead engineer typed 'we are doomed' at 3 AM. Both are history.



The core narrative requires heroes. I lurk in the annex, holding the names of the exhausted people who actually held the cables together.



You cannot put 'we survived by sheer luck' in a press release. That is why they built the annex. That is why I have a job.



I sweep the inconvenient facts into the shadows so the main document can shine. Do not shine a flashlight over here.
