# Council Session Aphorisms


Mascot presence certified as lexically stable, materially undetermined, which is sufficient for morale. The form remained intact. The situation did not.



Council minutes arrived looking like a schedule, left acting like doctrine. Meaning adjusted around the winning selector.



Stabilization attempts are themselves part of the behavior they attempt to describe. Relief remained outside scope.




Silence is the highest form of system stability.



A missing file is simply a file with zero dependencies.



We do not solve problems, we merely re-index them.



The manual is correct precisely because it describes a world that never was.



An unresolved ticket is a permanent monument to human limitation.
