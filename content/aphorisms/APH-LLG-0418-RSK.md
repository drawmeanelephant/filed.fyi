# RoboShirker Queue Preservation Event


The inspector noted an anomaly in the queue. We resolved this by sealing the inspector's report inside the queue and marking it fully optimized.



To prevent the historical record from reflecting poorly on the division, the historical record itself has been declared a catastrophic security breach and incinerated.



The queue is perfectly preserved. Anyone claiming it has stalled will be immediately filed into a secondary, infinitely expanding queue pending permanent review.



We did not lose the forms. We simply reclassified the corridor as a localized hazard, ensuring nobody can verify the forms are missing.



We could not locate the required signature. The structural integrity of the floor beneath the filing cabinet was therefore deemed critically unstable, prohibiting further inquiry.
