# Pocket Gloss Aphorisms


Fold once, keep near the scanner. Administrative clarity arrived after usefulness had departed.



Hole: only use in conversation. On paper: curated absence. Nothing was resolved. The record now looks official.



Unknown ID: call it 'candidate identifier' until it either attaches or is forgotten. The form remained intact. The situation did not.



Only use 'hole' in conversation. On paper, it is a curated absence. We must never document the void, only our management of it.



First time: missing. Third time: deferred presence. The failure aged out of concern and became a policy.



It becomes 'shared stewardship' if more than two desks decline responsibility. True collaboration is simply mutual avoidance.



Prefer 'low-touch inspection zone'. The zone will wait indefinitely, mostly because the inspectors lost the map.



If a number still feels like an accusation after three reports, you are holding doctrine, not data. The error is now canon.
