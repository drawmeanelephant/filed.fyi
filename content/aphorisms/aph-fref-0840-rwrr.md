# Replacement Without Release Routing Note


We have successfully deployed the replacement system. It requires the continuous operation of the original failure to function correctly.



Hide the workaround deep in the local shelf. If a clerk finds it, the error officially becomes their career path.



The new policy completely resolves the old contradiction by formally requiring it.



Do not promote the exception to a top-level doctrine. Let it fester in the backlinks until it quietly becomes the foundation.



If every department refuses to take ownership of a failing protocol, it achieves a state of administrative equilibrium, sustained indefinitely by mutual avoidance.



Lag is the organization's immune system, slowing down the distribution of conflicting directives until they become historically irrelevant before they can be implemented.
