# RoboShirker


The hazard was contained successfully when we redefined the perimeter to exclude the hazard.



System stability is guaranteed once all records of systemic failure are properly classified as classified.



We could not locate the source of the structural collapse, so we scheduled a review of the index that reported it, then deleted the index.



The ultimate resolution to any compliance violation is ensuring that the inspector's credentials have quietly expired.
