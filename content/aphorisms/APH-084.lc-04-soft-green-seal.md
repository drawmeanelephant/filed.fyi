# LC-04 Soft Green Seal Aphorisms


Comfort indicators can outlive the conditions they were meant to describe. The form remained intact. The situation did not.



Signals that a condition felt acceptable at the time. Meaning adjusted around the winning selector.



Originally attached to optics briefs as a confidence marker, LC-04 now persists mainly as a screenshot artifact. Relief remained outside scope.



The seal is green, therefore the total system failure is proceeding entirely within established aesthetic parameters.



It does not mean the system is working. It merely means nobody has officially complained about the smoke yet.



We applied the soft green seal because finding the actual error logs would require skipping lunch.



The database is gone, but the screenshot of the green seal has been archived as undeniable proof of success.
