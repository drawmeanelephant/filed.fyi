**Core counterpart:** [[mascots/M-0091|Bureaucratic Capture Vector]]


The Capture Vector needs no shared theology; it needs authority translated into paperwork.



FINA, CRED, GOVC, LEGA, and STAT are five ways one organism enters a file.



A chancery, board, ministry, committee, and ledger can host the same rot without becoming the same institution.
