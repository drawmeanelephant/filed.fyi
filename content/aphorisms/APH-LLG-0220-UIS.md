# Unified Intake Aphorisms

There is now exactly one form per filed feeling. Relevance expired before processing resumed.



Filers began writing split tones: one sentence for how it felt, one sentence for what the dashboards wanted to hear. The system kept the ritual and misplaced the function.



The taskforce eliminated form fatigue by concentrating it into a single, highly dense singularity of administrative terror.



Human emotion was optimized into a state that could not be charted, achieving true administrative invisibility.



C.U.N.T.I.E.R. extracted the adjectives and left the narrative to rot. The metric improved.
