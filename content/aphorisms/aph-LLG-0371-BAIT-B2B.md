# BAIT Subtype B-2B Ghost Audience Awareness


The ghost audience is always clapping, even when the performance is just a system reboot.



Subtype B-2B relies on the undeniable fact that people will endure infinite bureaucracy if they believe someone is watching.



Awareness of the ghost audience is strictly prohibited, yet perfectly measured in the quarterly ledger.



You are never truly alone when filling out forms; the B-2B subtype ensures a spectral jury is taking diligent notes.



We do not provide customer service. We provide a stage for your administrative suffering to be silently observed.
