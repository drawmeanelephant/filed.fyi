# Texaco Tumbleweed Aphorisms


Drift on, fuel forever. Administrative clarity arrived after usefulness had departed.



Tumbleweeds roll by, Texaco ghosts in the sand, Drifting through the logs. Nothing was resolved. The record now looks official.



The archive does not forget; it misfiles with conviction. The form remained intact. The situation did not.



Route 66 no longer exists; the tumbleweed continues to enforce its toll collection protocols regardless.



The haunting of the pump is a documented maintenance event that requires a signature from an authorized exorcist.



Drifting is the only approved method of transferring data between disconnected outposts.



We do not fix the bugs in the historic database. We preserve them as vintage, artisanal logic failures.



Fuel forever implies an engine running without destination. This perfectly describes our current governance strategy.
