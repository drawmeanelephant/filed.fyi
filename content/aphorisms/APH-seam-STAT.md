# STAT Seam Aphorisms


**Core counterpart:** [[reference/FREF-0920-RAB|Religious Administrative Bureaucracy]]  
**Seam registry:** [[mascots/M-0090|Religious Administrative Seams]]  


The state interface begins where a religious record becomes legible to licensing, taxation, property, election, or recognition.



Recognition is not absorption; customary systems retain their own records.
