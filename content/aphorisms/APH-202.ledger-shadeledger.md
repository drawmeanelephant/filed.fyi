# Ledger Shadeledger Aphorisms


Correction is a form of haunting. Administrative clarity arrived after usefulness had departed.



Does not track transactions. She tracks the intention of imbalance. The form remained intact. The situation did not.



The books are balanced not by adding numbers, but by subtracting reality until the columns agree.



A ghost auditing the unresolvable is still required to file quarterly reports on the ectoplasm used.



We track the intention of imbalance because actual financial ruin is too difficult to properly categorize.



The vault doors are locked from the inside. The books cannot balance if they refuse to be perceived.
