# WBA Continuity Aphorisms


Both sets of documentation are complete. Neither set is authoritative. Nothing was resolved. The record now looks official.



Identity has been forked; paper trails cannot substitute for missing raw traces. The form remained intact. The situation did not.



Regulatory completeness masks epistemic failure. Meaning adjusted around the winning selector.




A work breakdown structure guarantees that nothing works and everything is broken.



We divide the tasks so the failure is distributed evenly.



The baseline is a fiction we agreed upon to make the chart look nice.



Assigning responsibility is the first step in ensuring nothing gets done.



The schedule is a tombstone for time that was never really ours.
