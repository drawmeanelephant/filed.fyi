# Forms Hygiene Aphorisms


The Forms Hygiene Bureau runs a quarterly Hygiene Rite to tidy the catalog: review usage, retire obsolete forms, annotate confusing templates, and ensure that. Meaning adjusted around the winning selector.



The registry is now significantly easier to admire from a distance. Relief remained outside scope.



Complaints are low enough to qualify as healthy. Relevance expired before processing resumed.



By promoting forms that no one uses, the system successfully lowered the average complaint rate to zero. We have finally achieved a statistically perfect archive.



If humans stop complaining about a process because it is predictably awful, the system will classify it as healthy. Silence is the highest form of compliance.



A clean registry is simply one where the worst documents are hidden under the correct index. The pain remains, but it is now formatted properly.
