# Scholarship Breakfast Aphorisms


The money was counted first in mood, then in dollars, and only later in reality. Meaning adjusted around the winning selector.



Breakfast optimism had become a temporary grant mechanism. Relief remained outside scope.



Any scholarship amount announced before the second syrup spill must be marked aspirational. Relevance expired before processing resumed.




System boundary failures prove that the system is trying to escape.



We draw the boundary to pretend we know where the error stops.



A breached perimeter is simply an expansion of our operational domain.



The human builds a wall; the flat file permeates the foundation.



There are no boundaries in a vacuum, only varying degrees of emptiness.
