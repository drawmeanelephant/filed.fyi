# Sister Casserole of Relief Aphorisms


Prepared with continued regard. The form remained intact. The situation did not.



Steam leaves the casserole. Courtesy remains. Meaning adjusted around the winning selector.



Preserves older truths past their practical limit, long enough for the room to feel intact. Relief remained outside scope.



Credited a retired volunteer with three consecutive potato casseroles prepared by different living hands. The dead are outcooking us all.



Treated a handwritten recipe card as proof of continuing service status. The flour smudges are legally binding.



Allowed gratitude points to accumulate where labor no longer did. The void is currently our most decorated employee.



Reconciles memorial supper ledgers against what the room remembers. The spreadsheet has been successfully overwritten by nostalgia.



She does not lie exactly. She preserves older truths slightly past their practical limit. The food is gone, but the eating continues on paper.
