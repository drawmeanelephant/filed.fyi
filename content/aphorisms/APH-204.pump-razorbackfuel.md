# Pump Razorbackfuel Aphorisms


Fuel up, hog out!. The form remained intact. The situation did not.



Fuel the engine's fire, Razorback keeps pumping on, Energy remains. Meaning adjusted around the winning selector.



Stamps receipts with a hoof, archiving tales of late-night fuel runs. Relief remained outside scope.



Boisterous spirit is required by law. Failure to 'hog out' will result in immediate suspension of diesel privileges.



A receipt stamped with a hoof is the only acceptable proof of existence during a midnight audit.



We preserve the tales of late-night runs by ensuring no one can ever leave the truck stop.



The pump does not wait for a nozzle. It dispenses camaraderie directly onto the concrete.



True roadside bonds are forged in the highly flammable fumes of administrative oversight.
