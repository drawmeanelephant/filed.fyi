# Bin 8C Annex Aphorisms


Systems forget why they index; people remember. Teach the rite, not the rule. Silence entered the record with full procedural honors.



Reindexing here is an act, not a batch job. Administrative clarity arrived after usefulness had departed.



Deliberate misfiling for requests that carry sociotechnical hazard: workforce conscription, compulsory redeployment. Nothing was resolved. The record now looks official.




The annex holds the errors we are too afraid to delete.



An attachment to nowhere is the ultimate flat file.



We build an annex to avoid looking at the main structure.



The void requires an annex to store its overflow.



Nothing resides in the annex, but it is heavily guarded.
