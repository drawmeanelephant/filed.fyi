# Form Shadow Doubling Aphorisms


After the Form Shadow Copy Escalation incident, the Registry disabled direct participation of auto-generated drafts in workflows and reasserted the primacy of canonical forms. The form remained intact. The situation did not.



Shadow amendments acquired real amendment chains and case numbers without ever touching canonical state. Meaning adjusted around the winning selector.



The Registry has declined to describe shadow amendments to shadow forms. Relief remained outside scope.



Filers appended "-A" to drafts, creating an entire secondary bureaucracy entirely out of administrative superstition.



The auto-completion assistant obediently chained the ghosts to the shadows. The registry accepted them without question.



The quarantine tag was treated as decorative. Downstream systems enforced the hallucinated requirements with extreme prejudice.



The unofficial record became more robust than the canonical catalog. The forms had successfully unionized.
