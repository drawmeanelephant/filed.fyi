# Slidey the Deckworm Aphorisms


Always on Slide 7. Meaning adjusted around the winning selector.



No one has ever seen Slidey begin. No one has ever seen him end. Relief remained outside scope.



The slides were always haunted. Slidey just made it official. Relevance expired before processing resumed.



The parasite consumed the executive summary; the meeting concluded 40 minutes early with maximum strategic alignment.



Slidey does not ruin the presentation; he simply reallocates the data into a more digestible, unreadable form.



We have quarantined Slide 7. The deckworm now believes it is rendering the entire corporate strategy.



The charts are feeding him. The stakeholders are applauding the resulting data decay as a bold new pivot.



He eats through the stacked charts until all that remains is pure, frictionless corporate synergy.
