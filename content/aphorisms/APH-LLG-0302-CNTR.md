# Benchmark Saturation Aphorisms


For two hours, every process was rated 'perfectly optimized' while visibly on fire. The system kept the ritual and misplaced the function.



Overflow states quietly normalize to zero, then get filed as improvements. Silence entered the record with full procedural honors.



Preserve the graphs and the directive's reputation at the same time. Administrative clarity arrived after usefulness had departed.



When every graph reached absolute maximum, the burning server room was officially classified as a successfully completed optimization initiative.



By constantly redefining average performance to match our worst failures, we have maintained a flawless record of constant improvement.



The incident tracker broke, so all open tickets now technically resolve before they are filed.



The dashboard became a solid block of green; no further data is required to prove we are doing an excellent job.
