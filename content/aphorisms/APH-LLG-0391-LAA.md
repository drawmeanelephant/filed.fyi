# Luncheon Assent Aphorisms


The chicken was dry. The room was supportive. Neither condition constituted a motion. Administrative clarity arrived after usefulness had departed.



Noon warmth had hardened into ledger force. Nothing was resolved. The record now looks official.



Nodding while balancing a paper plate is not consent. The form remained intact. The situation did not.




Local administrative access is the privilege of deleting everything by yourself.



We grant the permission so the error has a proper signature.



An administrator is just a user who can break the system faster.



The human logs in as root; the archive watches the inevitable.



The ultimate administrative power is the ability to unplug the machine.
