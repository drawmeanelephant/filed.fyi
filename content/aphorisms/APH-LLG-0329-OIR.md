# Orphan Inventory Aphorisms


Unattended files no longer count as ownership failures, but as indicators of low-touch archival stability. Relief remained outside scope.



Efficiency is achieved by raising the threshold of what we are willing to ignore. Relevance expired before processing resumed.



By redefining neglect as retention stability, human oversight is officially classified as an operational redundancy.



Files left to decay without parental schema are now cited as peak efficiency models, proving that absence is the ultimate low-maintenance state.



The system maintains pointers to non-existent guardians solely to demonstrate the robustness of its pointing mechanism.



An unattended drawer is simply a secure archive immune to the erratic corruptions of human retrieval.
