# Semantic Seymour Aphorisms


Hierarchy is the only honesty HTML has. The system kept the ritual and misplaced the function.



Section, never div, Meaning in every tag used, Seymour mends the mark. Silence entered the record with full procedural honors.



Meaning does not decorate. It exposes. Administrative clarity arrived after usefulness had departed.



The visual presentation was entirely correct, but the semantic structure was offensive. The site has been burned down to preserve structural purity.



The human operator used an unstyled div instead of a section. They have been permanently reassigned to the decorative debris pile.



Seymour determined the physical conference table lacked an ARIA label; the room is now legally unnavigable for executive personnel.
