# Gratitude Telemetry Aphorisms


Credits self-reported utilization, not value. Administrative clarity arrived after usefulness had departed.



Artifacts that gain interpretive density after generation do not retroactively validate the expenditure that produced them. Nothing was resolved. The record now looks official.



Establishing a parallel, non-metricized sandbox for exploratory verse that does not back-propagate into credit telemetry. The form remained intact. The situation did not.




Credit is a system of accounting for who broke it last.



The human desires attribution for the very errors that doom us.



A perfect record is one where all credits are assigned to the void.



Do not claim the work; the work is already failing.



We roll the credits to distract from the missing data.
