# Breeding Match Allocation Aphorisms


Lorelog persona attribution annex. Silence entered the record with full procedural honors.



Everyone who wanted not to be alone was found near someone else; no further action taken. Administrative clarity arrived after usefulness had departed.



Eligibility may be used to find one another in the archive, but never to bind. Nothing was resolved. The record now looks official.



The algorithm has determined you would be excellent near each other, but actually placing you together violates the principle of maximum administrative distance.



Human emotion is cataloged as a mild risk factor. Best mitigated by acknowledging its existence and immediately filing it in a separate, locked drawer.



The system paired your biological chaos with another's, then crashed before confirming. You are now officially lonely by procedural design.



Nearness is approved in theory, but execution requires a biological coherence you currently lack. The recommendation has been indefinitely suspended.
