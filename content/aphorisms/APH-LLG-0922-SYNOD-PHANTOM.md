# Synod Phantom Minutes Aphorisms


**Core counterpart:** [[mascots/M-0087|Synodal Scribe]]


Conciliarity arrives in the minutes after the decree has already left the building.



An absent church is spiritually present when its absence would make unanimity untidy.



The Scribe formats the silence around its predecessor.
