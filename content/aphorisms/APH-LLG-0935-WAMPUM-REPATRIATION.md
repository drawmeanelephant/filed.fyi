# Wampum Repatriation Aphorisms


**Core counterpart:** [[mascots/M-0090|Wampum Keeper]]


A wampum belt is not a treaty metaphor when its bead sequence governs what can be remembered in council.



Repatriation transfers custody; it does not automatically restore an agreed reading.



A missing bead interrupts more than a display: it interrupts a sovereignty claim.
