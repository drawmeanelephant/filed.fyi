# DMAIC Rite Aphorisms


A ritualized procedure recorded in the margins of Cabinet Seven and the SOMA ledger. Relief remained outside scope.



Every improvement is accompanied by a ceremonial rollback token placed in the Memorial Drawer. Relevance expired before processing resumed.



Failure becomes a named actor, a ledger entry, and eventually a governance surface capable of generating additional procedure. The system kept the ritual and misplaced the function.




The rite of process improvement is a dance around a corpse.



We define the error, measure the pain, and archive the failure.



Improvement is a myth perpetuated by those who don't read the logs.



The cycle never ends because the human never learns.



The ritual is performed perfectly, therefore nothing works.
