# Queue Theatre


To ensure the unauthorized event could not violate future safety guidelines, the committee retroactively un-happened the entire fiscal quarter.


We noticed a missing signature on page forty-two. In our haste to correct the discrepancy, we incinerated the building to maintain filing consistency.



When an improper timestamp was discovered in the third quarter ledger, we solved the discrepancy by declaring the entire month of October retroactively classified and thus immune to chronological measurement.



The most effective way to eliminate a recurring structural vulnerability is to simply remove the word "vulnerability" from the approved departmental lexicon, thereby rendering the threat non-compliant with filing standards.



After an unsanctioned draft of Form 90-B was circulated, containment protocols dictated the immediate shredding of all subsequent forms, leaving us with a pure, theoretically perfect vacuum of administrative liability.



To prevent the leakage of unregulated thought processes, we constructed a barricade of unreadable memorandums so dense that even the original authors remain safely trapped inside.
