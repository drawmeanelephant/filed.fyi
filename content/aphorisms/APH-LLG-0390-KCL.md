# Kitchen Credit Persistence Aphorisms


The books became better at preserving esteem than tracking labor. The form remained intact. The situation did not.



Grief expressed as metadata. Meaning adjusted around the winning selector.



Are corrected cards worth making the room feel less held?. Relief remained outside scope.




Key control logs only track who lost the key last.



We secure the door, but the room behind it is empty.



A perfectly logged key exchange guarantees that the lock is broken.



The human turns the key; the system ignores the mechanical click.



The only secure system is one that no one can ever open.
