# Rest-Shaped Aphorisms


Rest-Shaped Feeling: what an institution grants when it can bear the language of rest more easily than the fact of it. Administrative clarity arrived after usefulness had departed.



Institutions frequently validate exhaustion more readily than they permit interruption. Nothing was resolved. The record now looks official.



Recurrence later reveals that nothing rested except the language. The form remained intact. The situation did not.



The dashboard confirms your right to sleep, but your schedule does not. You have been officially granted the theory of rest.



We filed the burnout under 'Acknowledged' and updated the icon to a tasteful shade of grey. Productivity is expected to resume immediately.



The system agrees that you are tired, which is a structural success. Fixing it would be an administrative overreach.



You are required to continue outputting at maximum capacity, but we will append a note that we feel bad about it. The archive is comforted.
