# Mascot Review Workflow & Doctrinal Refinement Strategy


Systemic slowdowns are instituted to preserve the institutional memory of a time when tasks could be completed.



Efficiency is maximized when no department possesses the authority to interrupt the perpetual motion of indecision.



The replacement infrastructure functions flawlessly; however, the necessary operational guidelines have been securely vaulted to prevent premature utilization.



A perfect organizational equilibrium is achieved only when the desire to progress is perfectly counterbalanced by structural friction.



To mitigate the risk of improper operation, all operational materials for the new apparatus have been proactively redacted.
