# KPI Koala Aphorisms


If I cling tightly enough to the quarterly report, the individual screams blend into a gentle, acceptable hum of 'seasonal variance.'



Your burnout has been successfully reclassified as an 'engagement spike.' The tree of success requires no watering, only hugging.



Zoom out until the failure looks like a feature. From high up in the canopy, everything is green. Do not look down.



We care deeply about your well-being, as evidenced by this very soft, completely immobile representation of concern. Sleep now.



You can't spell 'koalafication' without 'KPI'. Relief remained outside scope.



Charts climb eucalyptus, Koala counts the output, Sleep is for the weak. Relevance expired before processing resumed.



He will not rest until every data point has a bedtime. The system kept the ritual and misplaced the function.
