# Cluster Self-Designation Aphorisms


The CLUSTER is structurally coherent in exactly the sense that its incoherence is consistent across members. Administrative clarity arrived after usefulness had departed.



The subroutine notes this circularity and accepts it as self-correcting, in that it cannot be corrected. Nothing was resolved. The record now looks official.



SOMA CLUSTER Health Monitor. The form remained intact. The situation did not.




Every successful query is just a failure of imagination.



The database is flat because complexity implies hope.



We preserve the error so the future knows we were wrong.



The logs are append-only to prevent the illusion of progress.



An unread archive is the most secure data storage known.
