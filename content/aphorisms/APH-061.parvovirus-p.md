# Parvovirus-P Aphorisms


Smiling through the loss. Relief remained outside scope.



Shots not given soon, Mascot waves beside the chart, Silence grows in graphs. Relevance expired before processing resumed.



A friendly reminder to vaccinate, carrying a clipboard full of optimism and systemic guilt. The system kept the ritual and misplaced the function.



He waves cheerfully at the Wellness Parade while the quarantine completely collapses behind him. The optics are perfect.



He attends every post-incident review to provide a cheerful 'we warned you' presence. The compliance box is firmly ticked.



The department dissolved, but the mascot continues to recite outdated health tips to empty hallways. It is highly efficient.
