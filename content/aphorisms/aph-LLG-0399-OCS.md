# Assurance Drift Aphorisms


Once the phrasing was neat, there was nothing to treat. Words had become the repair, is all. Relevance expired before processing resumed.



A finding once shouted, defect. The system kept the ritual and misplaced the function.



Curated absence can glow as a sign of intentional grace. Silence entered the record with full procedural honors.



The defect was sanded down until it purred. The system is now failing with excellent posture.



Material slipped, but continuity gripped. The report successfully labeled the collapse as a stride.



The nonconformity kept returning, so we named it a durable aspect. It is now a load-bearing error.



Incidents climbed, so we declared a learning display. The failure rate is currently studying for its finals.



Not out-of-date, but legacy-stable. Tenure itself has become the sole indicator of health.
