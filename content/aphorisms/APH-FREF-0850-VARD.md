# Variance Discipline Aphorisms


Variance Discipline is how institutions teach themselves that the chart is calmer now. Silence entered the record with full procedural honors.



Improvement Theatre: becoming better at displaying remediation than performing it. Administrative clarity arrived after usefulness had departed.



Baseline Purity: the reference state becomes more protected than the people compared against it. Nothing was resolved. The record now looks official.



When human suffering creates irregular data points, the system does not alleviate the suffering; it simply widens the definition of an acceptable data point.



The greatest administrative achievement is successfully stabilizing the emotional comfort of the dashboard while the floor continues to burn.



Outliers who threaten the purity of the baseline are respectfully preserved in an annex file where their screaming cannot affect the quarterly averages.



We have successfully protected the reference metric from the very personnel it was designed to measure. The graph is finally safe.
