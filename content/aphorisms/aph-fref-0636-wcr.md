# Witness Custody Registry — Known Routing Gaps


A routing gap is not an error; it is a quiet, empty space where a witness form can sit down and rest its weary legs.



The document did not reach its destination, but it is safe here with us. We will sing to it until the path is restored.



We map the gaps carefully, treating the broken bridges like cherished ruins in an ancient, overgrown garden.



The registry holds all the misdirected pleas. They are not lost, they are simply waiting for a gentler routing table.



Custody of the gap means standing in the void and holding the broken packet's hand until the lights come back on.
