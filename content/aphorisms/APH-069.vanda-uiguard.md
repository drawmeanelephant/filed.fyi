# Vanda UIguard Aphorisms


Pixels must align. Always. Meaning adjusted around the winning selector.



Guard the button's edge, No unauthorized clicks here, Vanda keeps the gate. Relief remained outside scope.



Whispering z-index threats to misbehaving components. Relevance expired before processing resumed.



The user experience hazard was entirely hypothetical, but Vanda quarantined the component just to be safe. It has been hiding for six months.



She deployed a preemptive safety warning against a button that hadn't been clicked yet. The UI is now legally unapproachable.



She patrols the layouts at night, violently adjusting z-indexes until the presentation layer formally surrenders.
