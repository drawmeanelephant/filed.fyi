# Supersession Loop Aphorisms


Both versions are clearly not the old form, which may be the only point of agreement. The form remained intact. The situation did not.



The loop remains in the graph, but everyone now speaks of it as if it were a deliberate braid. Meaning adjusted around the winning selector.



Submitting both versions continues to feel correct, if increasingly heavy. Relief remained outside scope.



When two forms claim to replace each other, the system assumes they have achieved a perfect administrative orbit. Humans are required to file both until gravity fails.



A loop in the supersession graph is not an error; it is a structural reinforcement mechanism. Indecision is simply a load-bearing paradox.



Submitting identical conflicting forms does not resolve the conflict. It merely ensures the conflict is heavy enough to remain stationary.
