# Title Double Ledger Aphorisms


**Core counterpart:** [[mascots/M-0090|Title Society Registrar]]


Authentic and paid-in-full titles become identical when the same seal ends both columns.



The ledger is not designed to preserve the distinction its Registrar can see.



A seal applied twice is not equivalence, but it makes equivalence difficult to audit.
