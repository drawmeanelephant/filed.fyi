# Continuity Triad Aphorisms


Privileges traceability over truth — a bureaucratic tautology that reads well in reports and poorly in replication. Silence entered the record with full procedural honors.



Compliance without provenance is a brittle peace. Administrative clarity arrived after usefulness had departed.



The TRIAD rhymes; do not let the rhyme become a lullaby. Nothing was resolved. The record now looks official.




A triad is simply three points of failure disguised as a solid shape.



The strongest triangle breaks at all three corners simultaneously.



We form a committee of three to ensure the decision is never made.



Three layers of redundancy means three layers of undetected decay.



The triad holds the system up exactly long enough for it to crash.
