# Subak Calendar Drift Aphorisms


**Core counterpart:** [[mascots/M-0090|Subak Scribe]]


The water-priest knows the calendar is a lie and adjusts gates anyway because crops cannot appeal to a ledger.



A balanced water record can coexist with a failed harvest.



The Scribe keeps one calendar for council and one the water has imposed.
