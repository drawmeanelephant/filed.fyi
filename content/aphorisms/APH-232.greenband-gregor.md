# Greenband Gregor Aphorisms


The server is weeping, but it weeps within the acceptable SLA parameters. I paint the tears green so the stakeholders can sleep.



Red means panic. Yellow means meetings. Green means we are dying quietly without bothering leadership. I ensure we stay green.



Operating at 99% capacity is now the baseline. The band has been widened. The strain is now officially defined as 'resting state.'



Do not adjust your monitors. The flames are merely a high-contrast enhancement of our continued operational excellence.
