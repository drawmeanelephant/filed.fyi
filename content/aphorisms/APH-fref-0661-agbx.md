# fref-0661: Inaction Taxonomy — Aspirational Tier


The system delays execution not out of error, but as a deliberate tactic to outlast the tenure of the requesting personnel.



Approval is required from three distinct divisions, each strictly mandated to await the preliminary consensus of the other two.



New administrative frameworks are deployed without manuals, ensuring operational safety by rendering all potential actions procedurally impossible.



Every unresolved inquiry matures gracefully into an archived anomaly once sufficient time has elapsed to obscure its original context.



The manuals were withdrawn to protect the integrity of the process from those attempting to successfully complete it.
