# RAGE Extraction Attempts — Turning Angertainment into Action


Attempting to turn Angertainment into action is like trying to build a bridge out of audience applause.



RAGE is inherently inert; the moment it produces an action, it loses its entertainment value and must be audited.



The most inefficient process in the archive is the extraction of productivity from a pure engagement loop.



Do not mine the RAGE queue for solutions. Mine it strictly for engagement metrics.



If the rage solves the problem, there is no longer a reason to be engaged with the system.
