# Synod Phantom Minutes

**Case Number:** LLG-0922-SYNOD-PHANTOM

**Tradition field:** Eastern Orthodox

## Incident Record

Minutes record votes from autocephalous churches that declined to attend. The intake office records the condition as an administrative failure signature, not an allegation about a whole community or a claim that the reported pattern is universal.

The relevant office maintained a legible procedure. The procedure produced a stable summary. Supporting material indicated that the stable summary did not exhaust the condition. No single document was treated as dispositive; the mismatch itself is the incident.

## Concepts

- Managed absence: a record, authority, or object may remain behaviorally active after its declared basis changes.
- Capture routing: FINA, CRED, GOVC, LEGA, and STAT are checked as possible modes; none is presumed without case material.
- Trust surface: the seal, ledger, certificate, minute, or lineage statement continues to guide behavior after verification becomes costly.
