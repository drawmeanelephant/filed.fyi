# Seam Survey: Dystopian Present

Survey record of the Filed intake office. Ten seams were reviewed, each a documented failure signature in which a procedural surface and the state it claims to represent have drifted apart.

**Method.** The ten seams were grouped on a single observation: in each, an interface, score, portal, or model has become the operative authority while the condition it was meant to represent has become optional. Each seam was already documented independently in the archive or in vendor materials reviewed during intake. The review did not verify counts, dates, or rates attributed to outside events; where such figures appeared in the intake material, they are recorded, if at all, as reported patterns rather than findings.

**The future had a plot. The present has a process.** The dystopian future was expected to be cinematic: neon, resistance, clear lines. What the survey actually documented is bureaucratic, gray, and diffuse. No clear enemy. No target. Just:

- A form that errors at field 12 of 47
- A hold time that exceeds a lunch break
- A denial letter signed by "System Administrator"
- A terms update that changed a right without being read
- A camera unnoticed until its footage appeared in a proceeding
- A score that determines a decision but cannot be inspected
- A model that writes history without being reviewable

## Seam Inventory

### SEAM-001: The Algorithmic Manager That Never Sleeps (Because It Was Never Hired)

**Location:** Gig platforms, warehouses, call centers, delivery routes
**Observation:** There is no boss; there is dispatch logic, an acceptance rate, a completion threshold, and a deactivation rule. A worker can be removed at a threshold crossing during a weather event, and no one can say who wrote the rule that removed them.
**Evidence:** The appeal button that leads to a form that leads to a bot that leads to a policy that leads to nowhere. The support address that auto-replies with a case number no human will read.
**Marker:** Management without managers. Termination without a terminator.
**Cross-reference:** LLG-0451 — the same legibility logic applied to presence.

### SEAM-002: The Content Moderation Assembly Line

**Location:** Platform "trust and safety" operations
**Observation:** Moderation review is performed by contractors under quota, with a per-decision time budget measured in fractions of a second, viewing material most people could not look at. Trauma is an occupational hazard; the NDA is standard.
**Evidence:** The "AI moderation" press release. The human reviewers who train the model that triages to them — the model does not replace the review layer; it concentrates the cases it cannot decide into that layer's queue.
**Marker:** Industrialized trauma as infrastructure. The "AI" is the public surface; the review labor is the load-bearing layer.

### SEAM-003: The Smart Home That Reports on You

**Location:** Doorbell cameras, voice assistants, smart meters, connected vehicles
**Observation:** Observation coverage extends across adjacent devices, and law-enforcement access is routed through a vendor request surface whose procedural burden is lower than the homeowner expects.
**Evidence:** Terms of service accepted once and updated since without re-reading. Transparency reporting that the homeowner has no reason to trust. Opting out meaningfully means opting out of much of ordinary life.
**Marker:** Consent as coercion. The panopticon, voluntarily subscribed.

### SEAM-004: The Algorithmic Credit Score That Decides If You Exist

**Location:** Loan applications, rental background checks, employment screens, insurance quotes
**Observation:** A score computed by an undisclosed model gates access to ordinary life. The basis of the score is asserted as confidential; appeal does not reach the model.
**Evidence:** Applicants told the decision is based on a "proprietary risk model." Regulators told the methodology is a trade secret. Being poor generates the data that keeps you poor.
**Marker:** Mathematical redlining with a neural net instead of a red pen.

### SEAM-005: The Predictive Policing That Predicts Policing

**Location:** "Hot spot" maps, "risk terrain" dashboards, gang databases, gunfire-detection microphones
**Observation:** Historical enforcement data feeds a model that directs future enforcement; enforcement generates the data the model validates. The map influences the territory.
**Evidence:** Vendor methodology asserted as proprietary. Audits that reported false-positive rates, which did not prevent contract renewal. Community input absent.
**Marker:** Circular logic armed. The map creates the territory.

### SEAM-006: The Healthcare Algorithm That Triages by Optimization

**Location:** Insurance prior authorization, hospital discharge planning, "care management" platforms
**Observation:** Administrative optimization targets — length of stay, cost — are applied to clinical decisions. The "clinical criteria" applied are asserted as proprietary and were not written by the clinicians applying them.
**Evidence:** Denial letters generated by software. Peer-to-peer review conducted against a vendor summary with a per-case time budget. Appeal windows that expire faster than reconsideration can occur.
**Marker:** Life-or-death decisions routed through optimization targets.

### SEAM-007: The Educational Surveillance Suite

**Location:** Proctoring software, LMS analytics, "student success" predictors
**Observation:** Behavioral telemetry — gaze, keystrokes, time on page — is converted into an engagement score that influences administrative decisions. The student learns to perform for the verification layer.
**Evidence:** A creative writing assignment flagged on keyword match for self-harm and referred out of class. Extended dwell time and repeated reading reduced the engagement signal; the scoring layer did not distinguish rereading from inactivity.
**Marker:** Pedagogy replaced by proctoring. Learning measured by legibility to the model.

### SEAM-008: The Automated Bureaucracy That No One Can Fix

**Location:** Benefits portals, immigration case trackers, tax systems, veterans' claims
**Observation:** A portal can report success while the backend lacks the record. A case number can exist in one layer and not the layer that decides. Modernization stacks new layers on legacy dependencies without removing them.
**Evidence:** A "technical glitch" that terminated benefits in bulk. A document that uploaded "successfully" and never reached the caseworker's screen. The error message that is generic, and the phone tree with no human.
**Marker:** Rights that exist in statute but not in software. The government you cannot reach.

### SEAM-009: The Synthetic Media That Erodes Evidence

**Location:** Video calls, voice messages, security footage, "leaked" recordings
**Observation:** Synthetic media increases the plausibility of denying real evidence. Authentication becomes procedurally harder; evidence now requires more provenance than appearance alone.
**Evidence:** The "liar's dividend" — everything can be denied as AI-generated. Detection tools exist, but the detector/generator arms race means their findings are contested.
**Marker:** Evidence that requires provenance; appearance that proves nothing.

### SEAM-010: The Climate Infrastructure That Protects Assets

**Location:** Flood maps, insurance redlines, "managed retreat" buyouts, cooling centers, grid hardening
**Observation:** Resilience investment protects assets unevenly. Risk maps influence development and insurance decisions. Cooling-center hours can miss the conditions that made them necessary.
**Evidence:** A flood map that updated after the development approval. A buyout offered to homeowners, not developers. Infrastructure hardened where the money lives.
**Marker:** Survival as asset class. The lifeboat has a cover charge.

| Pattern | Description |
|---|---|
| **Accountability Laundering** | Decision → Algorithm → Vendor → Contract → "Proprietary" → No one responsible |
| **Consent Theater** | "I Agree" → Terms unread → No alternative → Coercion framed as choice |
| **Invisible Labor** | Human labor hidden behind "AI" branding; trauma externalized; NDAs enforce silence |
| **Feedback Loops as Policy** | Model predicts X → Policy enforces X → Data confirms X → Model validates |
| **Rights Without Remedy** | Statute says yes → Software says no → Appeal goes to software → Exhaustion |

## Filing Notes

Filed to the public lorelog on the ordinary publication path. The intake draft predicted the survey would not be published and would not be indexed in the public sitemap; the prediction was wrong. The archive files everything it receives, and the survey was indexed like any other record. The discrepancy is retained as an intake error, not a policy.

**Provisional.** The survey documents a pattern — proxy metrics displacing the conditions they represent — across ten seams. It does not claim the seams share a single cause, and it declines to conclude that any listed pattern is universal within its domain. The archive reviewed the seams; it did not certify them.

**Seams don't heal. They propagate.**

*End of Survey LLG-0450.*
