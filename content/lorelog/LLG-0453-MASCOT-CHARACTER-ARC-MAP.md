## Archive Position

The mascots have achieved **structural identity** beyond their original filing intentions. They are not characters in the lorelog; they are the lorelog's way of knowing itself.

This map does not resolve their arcs. It documents that the arcs exist, that they cross, and that the archive depends on their unresolved tension.
