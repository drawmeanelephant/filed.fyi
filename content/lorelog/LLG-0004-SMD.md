# Sitemap Drift — Structural Index Misalignment

The sitemap began producing structurally valid navigation paths that did not correspond to any known content hierarchy.

No errors were thrown.

All links resolved cleanly.

---

Users reported reaching pages that “felt adjacent” to their intended destination, but lacked expected content.

Repeated traversal led deeper into structurally consistent, contextually incorrect branches.

---

Audit confirmed:

- No broken links  
- No missing pages  
- No correct outcomes  

---

The system insists the sitemap is accurate.

No authority has been able to demonstrate otherwise.

## Related Aphorisms


### Sitemap Drift — Structural Index Misalignment {#sitemap-drift-structural-index-misalignment-2}

Sitemap entries diverged from content hierarchy while maintaining structural validity. The form remained intact. The situation did not.



Generated paths resolved successfully without reaching intended destinations. Meaning adjusted around the winning selector.



Navigation was certified as correct in the narrow sense that it continued elsewhere. Relief remained outside scope.



System audit confirmed zero broken links and zero correct outcomes. Relevance expired before processing resumed.



Route accuracy was asserted by the system and remains undemonstrable by users. The system kept the ritual and misplaced the function.
