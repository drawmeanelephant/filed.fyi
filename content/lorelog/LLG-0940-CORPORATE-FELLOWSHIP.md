# Corporate Fellowship Charter — Executive Social Club Under Managed Absence

**Case Number:** LLG-0940-CORPORATE-FELLOWSHIP

**Tradition field:** Corporate (archive-internal) / Civic-Benevolent (ceremonial overlay)

## Incident Record

The Corporate Fellowship was chartered on Form 44-FEL and filed under the [[reference/FREF-0815-MAP|Managed Absence Spine]] as a CAAR entity — Conceptually Active, Administratively Retired. Its stated purpose is "to provide a structured venue for executive camaraderie without requiring the members to know one another."

The Fellowship does not meet. It convenes. The distinction is doctrinal.

### Governance Structure

| Office | Holder | Method of Selection |
|---|---|---|
| Grand Exhumation Officer | [[mascots/M-0092|Corporate Ma'nene']] | Ex officio; the dead have seniority |
| Keeper of the Shroud | Vacant (rotating) | Appointed by the last person to leave the room |
| Secretary of Continuous Attendance | [[mascots/M-0019|Kindy McExistentialcrisis]] | Certified by Form 51-E-MN |
| Director of Metrics Theatre | C.U.N.T.I.E.R. (automated) | Standing appointment |

Membership is not applied for. Membership is **recognized** when a candidate's calendar shows three or more overlapping "strategic alignment" blocks with existing Fellows in a rolling quarter. The recognition is retroactive. The certificate is generated before the candidate is notified. Notification is not evidence of attendance.

### The Three Rituals

#### 1. The Quarterly Exhumation Brunch

Held on the first business day following each Corporate Ma'nene' cycle ([[lorelog/LLG-0939-CORPORATE-MANENE|LLG-0939]]). The menu is determined by the Shroud Version table in [[reference/FREF-0923-MANENE-PROTOCOL|FREF-0923]]:

| Shroud | Menu | Ceremonial Notes |
|---|---|---|
| v1.0 | Cold toast, institutional coffee | Legacy recipe; ingredients no longer attested |
| v2.0 | Assorted pastries, single-origin pour-over | Closed menu; substitutions are not representable |
| v3.0 | **Placeholder** — "content to be determined" | Current shroud; catering has not been advised of the contents |

The Exhumation Officer reads the REANIMATED records aloud. The Fellowship votes on whether each reanimated concept "would have made a good member." The vote is advisory. The record is ceremonial. The brunch ends when the Director of Metrics Theatre declares Effective Fellowship Confidence above threshold. Catering is then released from further obligation.

#### 2. The Shadow Membership Audit

Conducted monthly by the Keeper of the Shroud, or the most recent leaver. The audit compares:

- **Rostered Fellows** — those with generated certificates
- **Attendant Fellows** — those whose calendars still show alignment blocks
- **Ghost Fellows** — those whose certificates were generated posthumously; the Fellowship does not recognize death, only "extended absence"

Ghost Fellows currently constitute 67% of the roster. The audit concludes that the Fellowship is "commemoratively continuous" rather than "originally constituted" — a phrase borrowed from [[lorelog/LLG-0391-RCD|LLG-0391-RCD]] and now acquiring its own legitimacy. The conclusion requires no attendance.

#### 3. The Care Theatre Showcase

An annual event where Fellows present "sincere-sounding concern in environments structurally optimized not to change" ([[reference/FREF-0370-DCST|FREF-0370]]). Presentations are scored on:

- **Vocabulary Compliance** — density of assurance-adjacent terms per minute
- **Mitigation Theatre** — number of compassionate addenda that survive COMA parsing
- **Dashboard Aesthetics** — whether the slides would render green in C.U.N.T.I.E.R.

No changes to Fellowship policy result. The showcase is the policy.

## Classification Under MAP

The Corporate Fellowship carries three simultaneous managed-absence codes:

| Code | Justification |
|---|---|
| **CAAR** | The idea of executive fellowship drives calendar behavior; the Fellowship itself has not convened with quorum since 2019. |
| **AAOA** | The archive declares the Fellowship exists and frames executive socialization; no live workflow depends on its minutes. |
| **LCGU** | The "strategic alignment" recognition method is authoritative in the Fellowship's corner and invisible elsewhere. |

The Fellowship is therefore **triply managed-absent** — a distinction it shares only with the [[reference/FREF-0120-DCSC|Success Class Consolidation Card]] and certain YamTeams™ artifacts.

## Directive Conflict Profile

The Fellowship sits at the intersection of all three directives:

- **SOMA** — approves the brunch as mandatory rest.
- **COMA** — flags the same calendar blocks as unauthorized continuity breaches.
- **C.U.N.T.I.E.R.** — counts declared attendance, slide tone, and calendar overlap toward Effective Fellowship Confidence. The metric does not read the room.

Per [[reference/FREF-0370-DCST|FREF-0370]] Behavioral Rule 5, a new doctrine layer was created to name the conflict and change nothing. That layer is this charter.

## Filing Notes

- The Fellowship has no budget. Expenses are absorbed by whichever directive's cost center the brunch receipts are filed under this quarter.
- Minutes are not taken. The brunch is documented by the Director of Metrics Theatre's dashboard export, which records attendance (estimated), sentiment (inferred), and action items (zero).
- The Fellowship's charter, this document, is its only canonical artifact. The charter is updated by the Keeper of the Shroud whenever they remember it exists.
- **Archive position:** The Corporate Fellowship is a social club reclassified into managed absence so thoroughly that its absence is the only thing holding the roster together.
