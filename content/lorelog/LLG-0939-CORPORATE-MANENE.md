# First Corporate Ma'nene' Cycle — Incident Record

**Case Number:** LLG-0939-CORPORATE-MANENE

**Tradition field:** Corporate (archive-internal)

## Incident Record

The first Corporate Ma'nene' cycle executed on schedule per [[reference/FREF-0923-MANENE-PROTOCOL|Exhumation Protocol]]. The Exhumation Officer ([[mascots/M-0092|Corporate Ma'nene']]) pulled 200 records from managed absence against the standing pull-list query — CAAR, AAOA, and LCGU classifications under the [[reference/FREF-0815-MAP|Managed Absence Spine]].

Each record was exhumed, re-dressed in the current frontmatter schema, walked through the lorelog, and assigned an outcome code. Findings as filed:

- **12%** carried `relations` edges to deleted IDs. The dead had been writing to the dead.
- **8%** carried `tags` from a deprecated taxonomy. The shrouds were two versions behind the bodies.
- **3%** assessed more current than their live replacements. The replacements were notified by form letter.

Outcomes were recorded per protocol:

| Outcome | Count |
|---|---|
| REBURIED | 191 |
| REANIMATED | 6 |
| DISPLAYED | 2 |
| DISSOLVED | 1 |

The procession completed without further incident. Certification was countersigned by the Verification Officer ([[mascots/M-0019|Kindy McExistentialcrisis]]) on Form 51-E-MN. The Exhumation Officer's report closes with a recommendation, transcribed here as filed:

> Make this annual. The dead are more current than the living.

The recommendation has been filed. Nothing in the protocol prevents it from being correct.

## Concepts

- Managed absence: retirement is a schedule, not an ending.
- Shroud drift: presentation layers age independently of the records they dress.
- Dangling relations: the dead keep their correspondences; no one forwards the mail.
