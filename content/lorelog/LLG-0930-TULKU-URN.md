# Tulku Golden Urn

**Case Number:** LLG-0930-TULKU-URN

**Tradition field:** Buddhist — Mahayana/Vajrayana

## Incident Record

An urn process records neutral selection after candidate eligibility has already been administratively narrowed. The intake office records the condition as an administrative failure signature, not an allegation about a whole community or a claim that the reported pattern is universal.

The relevant office maintained a legible procedure. The procedure produced a stable summary. Supporting material indicated that the stable summary did not exhaust the condition. No single document was treated as dispositive; the mismatch itself is the incident.
