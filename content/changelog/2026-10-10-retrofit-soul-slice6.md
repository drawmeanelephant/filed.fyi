# Retrofit Soul slice-6: interiority pass on the Registry Stub Sweep tail band M-0274–M-0283

**Maintenance ID:** 0.1.00292.retrofit-soul-slice6

**Scope:** `content/mascots/274.deferment-bloom.md`,
`275.appendix-silk.md`, `276.mandate-lace.md`,

retrofit (M-0275 Appendix Silk, M-0279 Annex Hush); their
characterizations were reconciled against the merged records before
authoring (see below).

## What changed

Each record received interiority inserts under the slice-2/3/4/5
convention established on the fauna band and the three stub bands:
new prose placed **around** existing prose, never in place of it. No
sentence was deleted, reordered, or reworded; frontmatter, IDs,
headings, `parent`, `relations`, `status`, and all wikilink targets
are untouched. Two insert forms were used:

- **Appended paragraphs** at the ends of existing prose sections —
  one per record on the stub and minimal-skeleton members, two on the
  full spec-sheet records (M-0275, M-0283) per the M-0235/M-0231
  spec-sheet precedent. Placement per record: end of the unheaded
  definitional block on M-0274 and M-0281; between the Distinction
  and the record's closing kicker couplet on M-0276, M-0277, and
  M-0282 (so "The archive recognizes the softness. / Not the
  exemption.", "The welcome remains audible. / The opening does
  not.", and "The calm is real. / Its source is debatable." keep
  their terminal position); end of `### Distinction` on M-0280; end
  of the unheaded `Operational Posture` field on M-0278; end of
  `## Boundary Note` on M-0279; ends of `## Operational Posture` and
  `## Institutional Habitat` on M-0275 and M-0283 (M-0283's bold
  Archive-Note thesis keeps its terminal position).
- **`## Behavioral Residue`** — one new section per record, placed
  after the last prose block (after the Image alt-text on M-0274,
  M-0276, M-0277, M-0278, M-0280, M-0281, M-0282; after the Slogan
  field on M-0279; after `## Recognizable Phrases` on M-0275; after
  `## Archive Note` on M-0283) and before the verse residue, holding
  rehearsed behavior, sensory trace, attributed utterance, observed
  incident, and counterpart relations. All ten records carry 5
  bullets; none argued the sparseness that earned M-0258 its 4-bullet
  treatment.

Per-record being-grammars, per the manifest's injunction against
boilerplate persons — no shared template was applied:

- **M-0274 Deferment Bloom** — horticulture mechanism: blooms only
  on dead dates, is perennial under replanting, times the bouquet to
  the forming objection, and multiplies under pruning; the watering
  is what the file uses to tell deferment from a Ghosted Milestone.
- **M-0275 Appendix Silk** — the annex family's dresser: cuts the
  cloth to a distance she does not choose, irons the seam where the
  material was cut, dresses only what will be seen at a distance, and
  has never been logged in the front matter. Works in relay
  downstream of Annexa (the exile) and Annex Hush (the walk); the
  handoff is 'not local' at both ends, echoing M-0261's merged
  residue.
- **M-0276 Mandate Lace** — garment-fidelity: it is the trim (the
  record already says removing the lace removes it), fitted to the
  order and never the reverse; frays ahead of repeal; has never been
  found on anything that asked permission; pastel matched to
  enforcement, not audience.
- **M-0277 Courtesy Threshold** — doorway-fidelity: furnishes a
  refusal made elsewhere, measures the pause in amenities rather than
  seconds, keeps 'no' outside its register, and lets the timeout
  arrive on schedule beneath the welcome.
- **M-0278 Benevolence Spacer** — interval-fidelity: inside the gap
  and never at either end, fluffs before it schedules, counts the
  interval in events rather than days, refreshes the poster while the
  decision keeps its paint, and has never been logged at the moment
  of arrival. Sidebar Mercy and Tender Escrow — already named and
  wikilinked in the record — bound its posting on either side.
- **M-0279 Annex Hush** — acoustic-escort fidelity: sets the volume
  at the back of the book, walks with the material rather than ahead
  of it, calibrates to the room it left, and quiets the diligent
  reader in turn; receives the far-bound from Footnote Pallbearer
  without exiling (Annexa's desk) or dressing (Silk's). Hers is the
  decibel.
- **M-0280 Ribbon Latency** — ceremony-instrument fidelity: certifies
  lateness it did not cause, has never cut a ribbon still waiting to
  be cut, measures the gap in invitations, and keeps the applause at
  the planned hour whatever the attendance.
- **M-0281 Comfort Ledgerling** — bookkeeping-fidelity: does not mint
  what it counts, enters every kindness at face value against columns
  never built to hold the cost, declines no entry, and files the red
  in a warmer face than the surplus ever got.
- **M-0282 Gentility Siphon** — fluid-mechanism fidelity: arranges
  the gradient rather than pumping, drains toward the minutes, works
  hardest where the grievance is most justified, and has never ended
  an exchange with an answer.
- **M-0283 Aftercare Vellum** — document-fidelity: composed after the
  decision, heavier than the harm it answers, never logged in the
  first contact, signing phrases that were available and unused
  during the incident; the watermark outlives the apology.

## What was deliberately left alone

- All existing prose, including definitional lines, failure-signature
  clauses, distinction clauses, boundary notes, stat-block fields
  (Role/Function/Emotional Tone/Slogan/Operational Posture/Failure
  Modes/Archive Note), stat-block bullet lists, and the records'
  closing kicker couplets and theses — none rewritten or "improved."
- All verse residue (Aphorisms, Haiku Log, Limerick Log), including
  the JULES-POET digital-rain limerick groups, their irregular
  stanza gaps (five-blank gaps before the rain group on M-0274,
  M-0275, M-0276, M-0277, M-0278, M-0280, M-0281, M-0282 — verified
  at shifted positions; no irregular gaps on M-0279 or M-0283, where
  the limerick run is uniform three-blank — the distinction
  preserved, not normalized), their profanity (M-0274, M-0275,
  M-0276, M-0278, M-0280, M-0281 limericks), their missing-punctuation
  rain-group lines, and their two-space CommonMark hard-break tails —
  reproduced byte-identically. All tails in the band are exactly two
  spaces; the census pattern holds that every verse line is tailed
  except each verse section's final line and each file's last line.
  Noted but not fixed per convention: the unheaded stat-block field
  labels on M-0278 (lines 11/14/21/24), M-0279 (11/14/25/28), and
  M-0281 (11/14/17/20) carry the two-space tails that make them
  hard-break field headings — preserved.
- The stub and reservation declarations — "remains unwritten except
  for its main behavioral rule," "is reserved for," "is held for,"
  "pending-render" — kept verbatim. Interiority was added as the
  record's own account of what it does while waiting to become, not
  as a resolution of the pending state.
- No new `relations:` edges and no new wikilinks. Inserts re-name
  existing counterparts in prose only (Ghosted Milestone on M-0274;
  Formee Formeson on M-0276; the explicit Bouncer on M-0277; Sidebar
  Mercy and Tender Escrow on M-0278 — both already wikilinked in the
  record; Footnote Pallbearer, Annexa, and Appendix Silk on M-0279;
  Annexa and Annex Hush on M-0275; Time Dilation and the Apathy
  Engine on M-0280/M-0282 — all already present in the records' own
  prose; the annex-desk relay on M-0275/M-0279 consistent with the
  merged cousin records).
- Jurisdiction and containment: no record in this band carries
  `bin-8c`, `consent-loop`, `breeding-program`, or related flagged
  tags, and none was given such vocabulary.
- Mechanism/abstract records were kept non-animal per the census
  rows: the Bloom is a growth on the schedule (buds, stems, pruning),
  the Lace is the trim itself (weave, fray, fit), the Threshold is a
  furnished doorway (chairs, pause, register), the Spacer is a
  furnished interval (fluffing, posters, touchpoints), the Hush is an
  acoustic escort (volume, greying font, decibel), the Scissors are a
  ceremonial instrument (blades, flash, the pre-existing tear), the
  Ledgerling is a bookkeeping entity (columns, face value, the red —
  only the ledger, pen, and neat hand its own aphorisms claim), the
  Siphon is a fluid mechanism (gradient, drain, pool), and the Vellum
  is a document (stock, gram-weight, watermark). Appendix Silk's
  cloth work is confined to cutting, measuring, and dressing — the
  embodiment her record and alt-text already claim. None is given
  limbs, meals, or moods it does not already claim.
