## What changed

Twenty records received frontmatter tag corrections. Only tags were touched; no prose, verse, headings, IDs, or filenames were altered. Two classes of defect were repaired:

Every repair merged into a tag form that already exists elsewhere in the corpus. No tag was invented.

## What was deliberately left alone

- **Uppercase acronym tags** on four records — `BHDSS` (LLG-BHDSS-TOAST), `COMA` (posts/coma-observation-transcript), `TDCIP-LKG` (LLG-TDCIP-OVERCOH), `CAB` (five records). Lowercase or hyphenated counterparts exist for several, but each uppercase form is the record's own proper-noun acronym, written that way in title and prose. Merging them is a judgment call, not a mechanical fix, so they were left for the maintainer.
- **Spaced phrase tags that describe their record** with no established kebab counterpart — e.g. `WBA rot`, `Continuity Triad`, `Post-SCAS Echo`, `directive behavior`, `UPLC peak`, `provenance unresolvable`, `care event lifecycle`, `deletion bias`, `context collapse`, `negative control`, `Application Enhancer`, `Mac OS X Leopard`, `GMP Batch Recursion`, `GLP Archive Rot`, `SOP Amnesia Cascade`, `Dual-Certification Protocol`, `FeelingSeeder successor`, `Persona Rehearsal Engine`, `FeelingSeeder Decommission`, `synthetic affect` on other records. Removing or hyphenating these would require interpreting whether the phrase is a deliberate one-off, so they were left.
- **Cross-reference record-ID tags** (e.g. `LLG-0401-GLP`, `LLG-0385-CEB`, `LLG-CREDITS-GTA`, `FNF-ID-007`) — these appear to be deliberate links to other records and were not touched.
- No tags were added anywhere. No synonyms were merged beyond exact same-word case/space repairs. No IDs, filenames, prose, or poetry were touched. This was not a taxonomy redesign.
