# Retrofit Soul slice-5: interiority pass on the Registry Stub Sweep tail band M-0264–M-0273

**Maintenance ID:** 0.1.00291.retrofit-soul-slice5
**Date:** 2026-10-10
**Scope:** `content/mascots/264.corridor-heat.md`,
`265.provisional-mint.md`, `266.warm-hold-music.md`,

## What changed

Each record received interiority inserts under the slice-2/3/4
convention established on the fauna band and the two stub bands: new
prose placed **around** existing prose, never in place of it. No
sentence was deleted, reordered, or reworded; frontmatter, IDs,
headings, `parent`, `relations`, `status` (including M-0271's
`archived`), and all wikilink targets are untouched. Two insert forms
were used:

- **Appended paragraphs** at the ends of existing prose sections — one
  per record. Placement per record: end of the unheaded definitional
  block on M-0264 and M-0271; end of `### Distinction` on M-0266 and
  M-0269; between the definitional block and the record's closing kicker
  lines on M-0265, M-0267, M-0268, M-0270, M-0272, and M-0273 (so the
  kickers — "Whether it matures into doctrine or souvenir remains a
  later problem." / "The trace remains. / The confidence does not." /
  "That softness should not be mistaken for weakness…" / "Only the
  posture is known…" / "Its abbreviations remain uninterpreted. / Its
  importance does not." / "The voiceprint remains pending. / The
  hierarchy does not." — keep their terminal position).
- **`## Behavioral Residue`** — one new section per record, placed after
  the last prose section (after the Image alt-text in every record of
  this band) and before the verse residue, holding rehearsed behavior,
  sensory trace, attributed utterance, and observed incident. All ten
  records carry 5 bullets; none argued the sparseness that earned
  M-0258 its 4-bullet treatment in slice-4.

Per-record being-grammars, per the manifest's injunction against
boilerplate persons — no shared template was applied:

- **M-0264 Corridor Heat** — instrument-fidelity: a thermal surveyor of
  unrecorded pressure. Reads the corridor at three fixed points and
  never inside the room; arrives before the meeting knows it has failed;
  files in degrees rather than names. Its fur and Geiger counter are
  licensed by its own aphorisms and alt-text.
- **M-0265 Provisional Mint** — press-fidelity: stamps at the wettest
  possible moment, mints the coin before the denomination, weighs
  nothing it issues, and re-issues rather than defends. The record's own
  "witness—and technically falsify" is dramatized as a single motion.
- **M-0266 Warm Hold Music** — loop-fidelity: keeps time for a process
  that stopped keeping any; modulates but never resolves; has never been
  logged finishing a phrase; the silence beneath it is not its
  jurisdiction.
- **M-0267 Relief Watermark** — trace-fidelity: pressed once at half
  pressure, legible only at oblique angles, survives what it cannot
  prevent. The Doge Stamp — already named in the record — is kept on the
  opposite side of the page, consistent with its own overbearing
  register.
- **M-0268 Appeal Feather** — feather-object fidelity: travels the
  routes objections are not watched on; descends at the speed of
  deniability; has never been observed striking anything; molts toward
  the authority that ignored it.
- **M-0269 Policy Afterglow** — luminescence mechanism: emits at the
  spectrum of the announcement on a half-life rather than a schedule,
  has never been logged over a working system, and warms nothing it
  shines on — a deliberate non-thermal register that keeps it out of
  Corridor Heat's jurisdiction.
- **M-0270 Buffer Saint** — sacrificial-spacer fidelity: takes position
  before the blow lands, releases the damage oldest-first, is not
  emptied by prayer or repair, and has never been measured holding
  nothing (residual LLG-0851-CSI lag persists in its queue — consistent
  with M-0254's slice-4 residue, where the Comfort Engine never stood
  down after the same incident).
- **M-0271 Alibi Seal** — seal-stamp fidelity: lands before the question
  is asked, dries oldest-first, has never sealed a denial, presses
  hardest where the fault is deepest.
- **M-0272 Shorthand Reliquary** — custodial/devotional-adjacent:
  files by density rather than subject, re-hides what is found, never
  glosses an abbreviation, and keeps the truncation it was born from.
  Relic vocabulary is confined to what the record itself already claims
  (sanctity, the sacredly incomplete, quiet worship) — no liturgical
  machinery was imported.
- **M-0273 Tier Whisper** — acoustic-caste fidelity: an utterance at the
  top and a climate at the bottom; amplitude tracks the org chart rather
  than the microphone; never captured on an open channel; the voiceprint
  never matches because pitch shifts with the listener's tier.

## What was deliberately left alone

- All existing prose, including definitional lines, function/signature
  clauses, distinction clauses, `###` subheads, boundary notes, and the
  records' closing kicker lines — none rewritten or "improved."
- All verse residue (Aphorisms, Haiku Log, Limerick Log), including the
  JULES-POET digital-rain limerick groups, their irregular stanza gaps
  (five-blank gaps before the rain group on M-0264, M-0265, M-0267,
  M-0268, M-0270, and M-0272; no irregular gaps on M-0266, M-0269,
  M-0271, M-0273 — the distinction was preserved, not normalized), their
  profanity (M-0268, M-0269, M-0271 limericks), and their two-space
  CommonMark hard-break tails — reproduced byte-identically. All verse
  tails in the band are exactly two spaces; every staged file's
  trailing-whitespace census equals its original's exactly. Noted but
  not fixed per convention: M-0267 line 13 and M-0270 lines 13 and 15
  carry single-space tails on definitional prose (pre-existing);
  section-final verse lines ("File it under wind," "Backing is
  deferred," "System stays asleep," "Box returns to vault," "Filing is
  secure," "Brightness outlives use," "Clear the log and wait,"
  "History is sealed," "Meaning drifts away," "Bottom tier just
  screams") and each file's last line remain untailed as filed.
- The stub and reservation declarations themselves — "is reserved for,"
  "has been reserved for," "is kept for," "belongs to," "has not yet
  received a full cultus," "pending-render" — kept verbatim.
  Interiority was added as the record's own account of what it does
  while waiting to become, not as a resolution of the pending state.
- No new `relations:` edges and no new wikilinks. Inserts re-name
  existing counterparts in prose only (the Doge Stamp on M-0267; the
  Latency Hound and Render Ghost on M-0270; Metric Decay on M-0269; the
  Abrupt Timeout on M-0266; the Corrupted Log on M-0272; the Assurance
  Desk on M-0265 — all already present in the records' own prose;
  LLG-0851-CSI on M-0270, already wikilinked in the record and shared
  with M-0254's slice-4 residue).
- Jurisdiction and containment: no record in this band carries
  `bin-8c`, `consent-loop`, `breeding-program`, or related flagged tags,
  and none was given such vocabulary. M-0272's religion-adjacency was
  handled per the census flag: custodial and devotional-adjacent grammar
  only, within the register the record already sets.
- Mechanism/abstract records were kept non-animal per the census rows:
  the Mint is a press (wet ink, blank faces, the scale that does not
  move), the Hold Music is a loop (key, tempo, the mid-bar cut), the
  Watermark is an impression (half pressure, oblique light, fragments),
  the Afterglow is luminance (half-life, photometry, no heat), the Seal
  is wax (impression depth, tackiness), the Whisper is an acoustic
  event (amplitude, voiceprint, open channel). Corridor Heat's fur,
  the Feather's molt, the Saint's interposition, and the Reliquary's
  custody get only the embodiment their records already claim. None is
  given limbs, meals, or moods it does not already claim.
