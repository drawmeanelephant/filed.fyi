Each removal deleted the `## 🎨 Sora Prompts` block (with its `### Prompt 1` scene/style/text/mood instructions) and the `## 🧪 Sora Preset` block with its `preset_...` identifier (`preset_421_wrongdoor`, `preset_422_formsister`, `preset_423_keyholder`, `preset_425_dawnstamp`, `preset_426_oldwire`, `preset_428_safekeeping`, `preset_504_unansweredknock`). In `M-0937` the numbered generation-instruction list under `### 🎨 Sora Prompts` was removed; no preset token existed in that record. In `M-0424` the second identical copy of the aphorism beginning "Waited for the first stumble and claimed the rest by sequence…" was removed; the first copy and every surrounding aphorism were preserved.

## What was deliberately left alone

- No identity changes, graph repair, doctrine rewriting, prose modernization, or general mascot cleanup.
- Biographies, roles, slogans, duties, known-failure logs, addenda, aphorisms, haiku logs, limerick logs, and associated-mascot lists were preserved verbatim in all fourteen records.
- The `## Image` field in `M-0503` (`servicey-unavailabelle.png`) was left in place; it is canonical record structure shared by several mascots, not generation machinery.
- The `---` divider in `M-0937` separating the Glitch Transcript from the removed Sora section was retained; it is structural.
- `M-0429`, `M-0431`, `M-0451`, `M-0502`, and `M-0503` carried no Sora prompt/preset sections or preset tokens; they were verified clean and are listed only for completeness.
- The trunk Count line in `content/changelog.md` was left untouched; it is no longer maintained per-record, and a pending change removes it to prevent merge collisions across parallel residue passes.
- Sora sections in mascots outside the listed cohort remain in place; this pass is scope-limited by design.
