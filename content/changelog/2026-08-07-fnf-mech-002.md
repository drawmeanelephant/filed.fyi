## What changed

Removed explicit Sora/image-generation machinery from the early mascot cohort M-0015 through M-0024 (excluding M-0022):

- `M-0015` Indexer Hexley — Sora prompt sections and preset removed
- `M-0016` Jay Skript — Sora prompt sections and preset removed
- `M-0017` Jpegsey Artifactor — Sora prompt sections and preset removed
- `M-0018` Kafkey Errorhandler — Sora prompt sections and preset removed
- `M-0019` Kindy McExistentialcrisis — Sora prompt sections and preset removed
- `M-0020` Maila Delayden — Sora prompt sections and preset removed
- `M-0021` Markie-D-Down — Sora prompt sections and preset removed
- `M-0023` Modrewrite Gremblin — Sora prompt sections, preset, and Sora Render Lore removed
- `M-0024` Moveda Permanently — Sora prompt sections and preset removed

Each removal deleted the `## 🎨 Sora Prompts` section, the `## 🧪 Sora Preset` section, and the associated `preset_...` token. The mascots' biographies, addenda, aphorisms, haiku logs, and limerick logs were preserved.

### Kafkey exact deduplication

In `M-0018`, the second identical copies of two aphorism paragraphs (`Institutional failure routines rely on the structural integrity…` and `The ledger records thermal panics with clinical apathy…`) were removed. The surviving copies were not rewritten.

### Kindy-specific mechanical residue

In `M-0019`, separate from the generic Sora removal, the following template/scaffolding residue was removed:

- `export-ready: true`
- `reviewed-by: LoreSec.Bricky`
- the `Contact (Addition)` template surface
- `pinned: true`
- the dead `Status Dashboard` link (marked "no longer updates")
- the `Expansion Protocols` future-work/scaffolding section

Kindy's actual biography, credentials, quotes, collision history, and verse logs were preserved.

## What was deliberately left alone

- No identity changes, graph repair, doctrine rewriting, prose modernization, or general mascot cleanup.
- Historical references inside Kindy's collision history (`/707.debug-tests`, `/ops/999.emergency-bureau`) were kept; they are artifact history, not current navigation.
- Limerick verse referencing Sora (e.g., Modrewrite Gremblin's "Sora tried to render his face") was kept — it is in-universe verse, not generation machinery.
- Sora sections in mascots outside the listed cohort were not touched; this pass is scope-limited by design.
- The `Render Ritual Notes` section in `M-0017` is archival fiction about the mascot's behavior, not generation machinery; it was retained.
