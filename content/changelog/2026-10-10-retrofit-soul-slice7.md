## What changed

Each record received interiority inserts under the
slice-2/3/4/5/6 convention: new prose placed **around**
existing prose, never in place of it. No sentence was
deleted, reordered, or reworded; frontmatter, IDs,
headings, `parent`, `relations`, `status`, and all
wikilink targets are untouched. Two insert forms were
used:

- **Appended paragraphs** — one per record on the seven
  stub members, two on the three full spec-sheet records
  (M-0289, M-0291, M-0292) per the M-0235/M-0275/M-0283
  spec-sheet precedent. Placement per record: between the
  Distinction clause and the record's closing observation
  on M-0284 (so "Where records remain thin, it tends to
  grow louder in hindsight." keeps its terminal position);
  end of the definitional block on M-0285 and M-0286; end
  of the Resurrection-Node distinction paragraph on
  M-0287; between the Distinction clause and the closing
  kicker couplets on M-0288, M-0293, and M-0295 (so
  "Sometimes that keeps them alive. / **Sometimes it
  keeps them decorative.**", "No bylaw currently admits
  this. / Several rooms do.", and "Its warmth is
  genuine. / Its coverage map is not accidental." keep
  their terminal positions); ends of `## Operational
  Posture` and `## Institutional Habitat` on M-0289,
  M-0291, and M-0292.
- **`## Behavioral Residue`** — one new section per
  record, placed after the last prose block (after the
  Image alt-text on M-0284, M-0285, M-0286, M-0287,
  M-0288, M-0293, M-0295; after `## Recognizable Phrases`
  on M-0289 and M-0291; after `## Archive Note` on
  M-0292) and immediately before the verse residue,
  holding rehearsed behavior, sensory trace, attributed
  utterance, observed incident, and boundary assertions.
  Nine records carry 5 bullets; M-0284 carries 4 per the
  M-0258 sparseness precedent — see the restraint note
  below.

Per-record being-grammars, per the manifest's injunction

## M-0284 restraint note (flagged jurisdiction)

M-0284 carries a consent-adjacent tag field
(`"consent"`, `"social-inference"`) and is census-flagged
for jurisdiction preservation. The insert was bounded to
what the record's own prose claims: the murmur is a
sound — an atmosphere mistaken for permission, a habit
of logging "ambient consensus" in place of missing
explicit sign-offs, a distortion of absent objection
into retroactive assertion. The interiority dramatizes
only what the record already says the murmur DOES while
waiting: it persists underneath the agenda as a
speakerless register, it is captured in retrospect
("louder in hindsight" is the record's own line), it is
filed only where a signature is missing, and it leaves
no physical residue (the record's own Rubber Stamp
distinction).

Deliberately NOT dramatized: the murmur does not grant,
withhold, adjudicate, or ratify consent; it has no
authority over agreement and attends no decision —
deployment logs place it at the filing, never at the
moment of decision; no new consent mechanics were
invented (it does not detect, verify, or certify real
assent — its one testimony, "they did not say
otherwise," reports an absence and is framed in the
bullet as "a sentence that signs nothing"). Where the
record asserts certification, the inserts keep the
distortion on the record's side: the file insists the
room agreed; the sound only ever testified that no one
objected. Four residue bullets rather than five, per the
M-0258 sparseness precedent — the record's doctrine is
a single mechanic (absence logged as presence), and a
fifth bullet would have had to invent a capability to
earn its place.
