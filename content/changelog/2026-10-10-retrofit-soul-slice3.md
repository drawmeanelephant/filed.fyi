# Retrofit Soul slice-3: interiority pass on the Registry Stub Sweep band M-0223–M-0249

**Maintenance ID:** 0.1.00289.retrofit-soul-slice3
**Date:** 2026-10-10
**Scope:** `content/mascots/223.placeholder-witness.md`, `235.annexa-sorrowmark.md`,
`237.index-mourner.md`, `239.annex-lurker.md`, `240.badgevine.md`,
`243.compliance-murmur.md`, `244.sentiment-launderette.md`, `245.escalady.md`,
`248.attestation-mole.md`, `249.sealant-patience.md` — ten tier-A records, the
stub/minimal band of the census. Issue #1040, RETROFIT SOUL slice-3.

## What changed

Each record received interiority inserts under the slice-1 convention
established on the religion block (PR #1044) and continued on the fauna band
(slice-2): new prose placed **around** existing prose, never in place of it. No
sentence was deleted, reordered, or reworded; frontmatter, IDs, headings,
`parent`, `relations`, and all wikilink targets are untouched. Two insert forms
were used:

- **Appended paragraphs** at the ends of existing prose sections (or the
  unheaded definitional block on pure stubs) — one per record, two for M-0235,
  dramatizing what the filing had only asserted. On M-0245 the paragraph sits
  between the function paragraph and the record's two closing lines so the
  kickers ("The institution has not fully documented…", "The stairs, however,
  have already been reserved.") keep their position.
- **`## Behavioral Residue`** — one new section per record, placed after the
  last prose section and before the verse residue, holding 4–5 bullets of
  rehearsed behavior, sensory trace, attributed utterance, and observed
  incident. M-0243 carries 4 bullets in deference to its own "underdescribed
  by design" doctrine; the rest carry 5.

Per-record being-grammars, per the manifest's injunction against boilerplate
persons — the stub band is diverse, so no shared template was applied:

- **M-0223 Placeholder Witness** — the vacancy that keeps itself: never
  photographed seated, the paperweight that travels, the successor recorded
  as named but never as arriving, and the participant misfiling as the file's
  only act and its only dishonesty.
- **M-0235 Annexa Sorrowmark** — the relocator who reads what she exiles:
  continuous pagination across reorganizations, 'circulation: zero; custody:
  intact,' testimony given as a citation ('It is on page 84.'), upstream of
  Silk and Hush.
- **M-0237 Index Mourner** — the caretaker of pointing: folded 404s identified
  by crease, dead anchors dusted but never repaired, arrivals before scheduled
  migrations, grief that runs ahead of the break.
- **M-0239 Annex Lurker** — lurking as custody: the channel kept one level
  dimmer, never dark; the one-directional sweep; 'it is an annex' as complete
  testimony; the shadow that keeps what the headline drops.
- **M-0240 Badgevine** — vegetal fidelity: growth as its only documented
  behavior, sprouting after training modules but never after incident reviews,
  'voice: foliage,' the blank name tag under twelve years of accretion.
- **M-0243 Compliance Murmur** — the sound a room makes: measured at the
  register of ventilation, never recorded under forty minutes, a voice listed
  as 'present' with words 'unminuted.' Sparse by design.
- **M-0244 Sentiment Launderette** — mechanism-fidelity: fixed cycles, the
  lint trap as the only part of the system that fills, the cycle-end tone as
  'a sound like closure,' the drum never photographed empty.
- **M-0245 Escalady** — posture as being: one step per reporting period, the
  uncreased routing slip held at presentation angle, the flattened patch on
  the third landing, ascent always 'underway.'
- **M-0248 Attestation Mole** — the burrower in the dark math: tunnels mapped
  by negation, seals placed accurately on forms never read, 'pressure' as
  attributed utterance, the single double-struck stamp on record.
- **M-0249 Sealant Patience** — substance-fidelity: patience as chemistry not
  choice, edges bonded before centers, cure time lengthening under
  observation, the LLG-0323 file still mid-grip, problems laminated rather
  than obscured.

## What was deliberately left alone

- All existing prose, including definitional lines, distinction clauses,
  boundary notes, classification kicker lines, and the records' deliberate
  ambiguities ("The file is reserved. / The sadness is already operational.";
  "Canonical behavior remains pending. / Its side-presence does not.") —
  none rewritten or "improved."
- All verse residue (Aphorisms, Haiku Log, Limerick Log), including the
  JULES-POET digital-rain limerick groups, their irregular stanza gaps
  (M-0237's five-blank gap before the rain group; M-0243's five-blank gap in
  the same position and its four-blank aphorism gap), their profanity, and
  their two-space CommonMark hard-break tails — reproduced byte-identically.
  All trailing whitespace in the band is exactly two spaces; no staged file
  adds or removes any.
- M-0239's crude limerick residue, M-0240's and M-0249's profane limericks —
  preserved verbatim; verse residue is not the retrofit's business.
- No new `relations:` edges and no new wikilinks. Inserts re-name existing
  counterparts in prose only (Appendix Silk and Annex Hush on M-0235/M-0237/
  M-0239; the Validation Hound on M-0248; the Plaque-Weaver on M-0240; Panic
  Siren and Ticket-Minder on M-0245; Minute Velvet on M-0243 — the M-0256
  record already names Compliance Murmur as its counterpart; the LLG-0323
  incident on M-0249 is already wikilinked in the record and is referenced
  in prose as 'the LLG-0323 file').
- Jurisdiction and containment: no record in this band carries `bin-8c`,
  `consent-loop`, `breeding-program`, or related flagged tags, and none was
  given such vocabulary. Distinction clauses that allocate jurisdiction
  (Mourner vs. Annexa; Lurker vs. Sorrowmark vs. Silk; Mole vs. Hound;
  Patience vs. Active Neglect) are preserved verbatim and echoed only within
  their stated boundaries.
- Mechanism/abstract records were kept non-animal per the census rows:
  Badgevine is vegetal (growth, not behavior), the Launderette is an
  appliance (cycles, trap, tone), Sealant Patience is a substance (cure,
  grip, set), and Compliance Murmur is a sound (register, duration, absence
  of body). Their interiority is fidelity-of-form rather than creature
  embodiment; none is given limbs, meals, or moods it does not already claim.
- The stub declarations themselves: "pending-render," "underdescribed by
  design," "mostly inferential," "not yet been rendered" — kept verbatim.
  Interiority was added as the record's own account of what it does while
  waiting to become, not as a resolution of the pending state.
