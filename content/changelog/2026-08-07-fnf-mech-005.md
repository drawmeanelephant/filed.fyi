# Legacy Themed Mascot Generation Residue Removed (A)

## What changed

Removed explicit Sora/image-generation machinery from the legacy themed mascot cohort M-0085, M-0121, and M-0201 through M-0210:

- `M-0085` Rot McMascotterton — Sora prompt section and its empty `## Purpose` template heading removed
- `M-0121` Archiva Dustwhisper — Sora prompt section and preset token removed
- `M-0201` Quill Staticvox — Sora prompt section and preset token removed
- `M-0202` Ledger Shadeledger — Sora prompt section, preset token, and empty `## Addendum Comments` template heading removed
- `M-0203` Cinder Forgememo — Sora prompt section and preset token removed
- `M-0204` Pump Razorbackfuel — Sora prompt section and preset token removed
- `M-0205` Texaco Tumbleweed — Sora prompt section and preset token removed
- `M-0206` Diesel DKDriller — Sora prompt section and preset token removed
- `M-0207` Blue Dreamweaver — Sora prompt section and preset token removed
- `M-0208` OG Kushkeeper — Sora prompt section and preset token removed
- `M-0209` Cookie Crumbleclerk — Sora prompt section and preset token removed
- `M-0210` Sour Dieselscribe — partial Sora prompt section removed

Each removal deleted the `## 🎨 Sora Prompts` block (with its `### Prompt` scene/style/text/mood instructions) and, where present, the `## 🧪 Sora Preset` block and its `preset_...` identifier. In `M-0085` the empty `## Purpose` heading and the `## Sora Prompts` block were deleted together; in `M-0202` the empty `## Addendum Comments` heading was deleted with the preset block. The mascots' names, roles, slogans, biographies, failure signatures, addenda, and verse logs were preserved.

## What was deliberately left alone

- No identity changes, graph repair, doctrine rewriting, prose modernization, or general mascot cleanup.
- Names, themes, slogans, biographies, poetry, and failure signatures were left untouched even where dated or ridiculous; the presence of dated cultural material is not residue.
- `M-0201` through `M-0210` keep their `## Addendum Comments` checklist sections (editorial notes, not generation machinery).
- `M-0085` keeps its `## 🧯 Known System Messages` section, aphorisms, haiku log, and limerick log.
- The `M-0208` failure-signature typo (`pluralityMisfiled`) and the `M-0121` biography formatting oddity were left as-is; they are not generation machinery.
- Sora sections in mascots outside the listed cohort were not touched; this pass is scope-limited by design.
