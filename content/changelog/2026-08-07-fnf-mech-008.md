## What changed

- `M-0409` Ledger Snarl — Sora prompt sections and preset removed

Each removal deleted the `## 🎨 Sora Prompts` block (with its `### Prompt` scene/style/text/mood instructions) and the `## 🧪 Sora Preset` block with its `preset_...` identifier (e.g. `preset_408_halfheld`, `preset_418_teapotta`). The mascots' biographies, failure semantics, status behavior profiles, aphorisms, haiku logs, limerick logs, and associated-mascot lists were preserved.

### Half-Held Breath deduplication

In `M-0408`, six aphorism paragraphs existed as exact duplicates. Only the second copy of each was removed; the first copies and all other aphorisms were left verbatim:

- `Froze mid-hallway during a scheduled observation…`
- `The session ghost lingered in the corridor precisely long enough…`
- `The pending process recognized it was being watched…`
- `A handshake failed halfway through transmission…`
- `The user's attempt to persist across nodes dissolved upon arrival…`
- `Rather than complete the data exchange, the pathway severed itself…`

## What was deliberately left alone

- No identity changes, graph repair, doctrine rewriting, prose modernization, or general mascot cleanup.
- Surviving aphorisms were not polished; the duplicate removal was the only edit to the aphorism sections.
- Poetry was preserved verbatim, including the in-universe Sora reference in the `M-0418` limerick ("In Sora's render, a teapot's plea…"). That is archival verse, not generation machinery.
- Sora sections in mascots outside the listed cohort were not touched; this pass is scope-limited by design.
