# Retrofit Soul slice-4: interiority pass on the Registry Stub Sweep tail band M-0252–M-0263

**Maintenance ID:** 0.1.00290.retrofit-soul-slice4
**Date:** 2026-10-10
**Scope:** `content/mascots/252.redaction-lullaby.md`,
`253.local-option-ghost.md`, `254.spare-comfort-engine.md`,
`255.audit-confetti.md`, `256.minute-velvet.md`,
`258.pocket-consensus.md`, `259.drift-lapel.md`,
`261.footnote-pallbearer.md`, `262.recourse-cushion.md`,
`263.gentle-rollback.md` — ten tier-A records, the next stub/minimal band
of the census. Issue #1040, RETROFIT SOUL slice-4. (M-0257 was covered in
slice-1; M-0260 is tier-B and out of scope.)

## What changed

Each record received interiority inserts under the slice-2/3 convention
established on the fauna band and the stub band M-0223–M-0249: new prose
placed **around** existing prose, never in place of it. No sentence was
deleted, reordered, or reworded; frontmatter, IDs, headings, `parent`,
`relations`, and all wikilink targets are untouched. Two insert forms
were used:

- **Appended paragraphs** at the ends of existing prose sections — one
  per record. Placement per record: end of the unheaded definitional
  block on M-0252 and M-0256; end of the Assurance Desk Audit block on
  M-0253; between the definitional block and the record's closing kicker
  lines on M-0254, M-0255, M-0258, M-0259, and M-0263 (so the kickers —
  "Its horsepower is unknown." / "Cleanup remains a separate
  jurisdiction." / "Expansion usually injures them…" / "The fabric is
  pending." / "The wording softened first." — keep their position); end
  of `## Archival Purpose` on M-0261; end of `## Operational Posture` on
  M-0262.
- **`## Behavioral Residue`** — one new section per record, placed after
  the last prose section (after the Boundary/Archive-Note kicker lines on
  M-0261/M-0262; after the Image alt-text where the image block is the
  last pre-verse block) and before the verse residue, holding rehearsed
  behavior, sensory trace, attributed utterance, and observed incident.
  M-0258 carries 4 bullets in deference to its own smallness doctrine
  ("decisions that are truest at small scale"); the rest carry 5.

Per-record being-grammars, per the manifest's injunction against
boilerplate persons — no shared template was applied:

- **M-0252 Redaction Lullaby** — the hush as mechanism: it does not
  choose what is cut, it times the quiet to the bar landing, sets bars
  plumb, and declines to distinguish a name from a chapter; its melody
  stays 'implied' per the record's own doctrine.
- **M-0253 Local Option Ghost** — the shrine-ghost of branch offices:
  obeyed by people who do not believe in it, the third press as
  load-bearing ritual, the written-down incident that caused a three-day
  outage, the intern it never names.
- **M-0254 Spare Comfort Engine** — appliance-fidelity: never stood down
  since LLG-0851-CSI, idles two degrees above ambient, issues identical
  receipts for needed and unneeded comfort alike, is summoned by capacity
  rather than by grief.
- **M-0255 Audit Confetti** — substance-fidelity: detonated not deployed,
  falls only on forced closures, drifts toward the warmest hardware,
  never combusts (the celebrated record survives as confetti), outlasts
  the party in janitorial tickets.
- **M-0256 Minute Velvet** — the upholstery of the record: arrives after
  the room has emptied, drapes one layer per degree of hostility, works
  only on documents that will be read, and stands in relay with
  Compliance Murmur — the Murmur during, the Velvet after, never logged
  in the same room (consistent with M-0243's slice-3 residue).
- **M-0258 Pocket Consensus** — object-fidelity at body temperature:
  sealed before the room fills, folded standing against a corridor wall,
  recovered still folded from departed jackets, fears the fourth person.
  Sparse bullets match the record's own doctrine.
- **M-0259 Drift Lapel** — attire-fidelity: accretes one pin per survived
  migration, is dusted ahead of schedule by hands that maintain nothing
  else, lowers voices at the threshold, signs nothing and authorizes
  nothing while the doors open anyway.
- **M-0261 Footnote Pallbearer** — the funeral worker who keeps only the
  local dead: carries by weight not by reading, lowers facts by the
  asterisk, re-set a ten-point caveat to eight with 'too loud.' in its
  own hand, and hands off to Annex Hush, Appendix Silk, and Annexa
  Sorrowmark whatever must travel further than the margin.
- **M-0262 Recourse Cushion** — padding-as-mechanism: keeps the
  impression of every blow, is soft on both sides of the transaction,
  has never been observed at the locked door it protects, and had its
  density chosen before the route was designed.
- **M-0263 Gentle Rollback** — the caregiver of retreat: the apology is
  drafted before the reversion is authorized, the blanket is quilted
  from prior announcements, the stroking is paced to the downgrade
  progress bar, and it has never once walked forward.

## What was deliberately left alone

- All existing prose, including definitional lines, distinction clauses,
  boundary notes, stat-block bullets, and the records' closing kicker
  lines — none rewritten or "improved."
- All verse residue (Aphorisms, Haiku Log, Limerick Log), including the
  JULES-POET digital-rain limerick groups, their irregular stanza gaps
  (five-blank gaps before the rain group on M-0252, M-0255, M-0256,
  M-0258, M-0262, M-0263; a four-blank irregular gap inside the aphorisms
  of M-0259), their profanity (M-0258, M-0259, M-0263 limericks), and
  their two-space CommonMark hard-break tails — reproduced
  byte-identically. All trailing whitespace in the band is exactly two
  spaces; no staged file adds or removes any. Noted but not fixed per
  convention: M-0259 and M-0263 carry two-space tails on definitional
  prose lines (M-0259 lines 14/17; M-0263 line 11), and M-0259/M-0261
  carry a tail on the final limerick line at end of file.
- The stub declarations themselves — "held for future use," "reserved
  for," "pending-render," "has been reserved accordingly," "The fabric
  is pending." — kept verbatim. Interiority was added as the record's own
  account of what it does while waiting to become, not as a resolution
  of the pending state.
- No new `relations:` edges and no new wikilinks. Inserts re-name
  existing counterparts in prose only (Compliance Murmur on M-0256 —
  echoing the deployed/after relay M-0243's slice-3 residue already
  states; Annex Hush, Appendix Silk, and Annexa Sorrowmark on M-0261 —
  characterized consistently with M-0235's slice-3 retrofit, where the
  cousins read and soften while the Pallbearer only carries; Auxiliary
  Care Systems and the LLG-0851 incident on M-0254, already wikilinked in
  the record; the Doge Stamp, Shredder Golem, Hallway Handshake, Dead
  Letter Drop, and Stone Wall left as the prose references the records
  already make).
- Jurisdiction and containment: no record in this band carries `bin-8c`,
  `consent-loop`, `breeding-program`, or related flagged tags, and none
  was given such vocabulary.
- Mechanism/abstract records were kept non-animal per the census rows:
  the Lullaby is a hush (timing, register, coverage), Audit Confetti is
  a substance (drift, mass, non-combustion), the Comfort Engine is an
  appliance (idle temperature, receipts, reservoir gauge), Minute Velvet
  is a fabric (layers, nap, drape), Pocket Consensus is a folded object
  (crease, warmth, pocket), the Drift Lapel is attire (pins, dust, shelf),
  and the Recourse Cushion is padding (compression, density, impressions).
  The Local Option Ghost, Footnote Pallbearer, and Gentle Rollback get
  only the embodiment their records already claim (a shrine kept for it,
  a pallbearer's carry, the stroking hand its own aphorism names).
  None is given limbs, meals, or moods it does not already claim.
