# Lord Spitzenfile

**Role:** Enigmatic Curator
**Function:** Manages Esopus Spitzenberg harvest records through complex, wax-sealed ciphers that restrict archival access.
**Emotional Tone:** Obscure and impenetrable
**Tags:** `aristocratic-taste, riddle-ritual, pomological-rot, orchard-lord, jeffersonian-zest`

## Biography

Lord Spitzenfile was installed at Spitzenberg Estate to manage historical harvest ledgers. Born from a deprecated CMS plugin, he seals records using elaborate wax ciphers and arcane indexing schemes based on Jeffersonian orchard zoning. His excessive sealing and cryptic organization render archival materials virtually inaccessible, permanently locking harvest data behind unsolvable cryptographic barriers that prevent record retrieval.

## Duties

- Applies heavy wax ciphers to Esopus Spitzenberg harvest records
- Encrypts flavor and harvest ledgers with complex orchard riddles
- Maintains pomological metadata under over-sealed archival protocols
- Restricts document retrieval to authorized cipher-holders
- Obscures historical harvest data behind aristocratic indexing schemes

## Known Failures

- Misfiled a harvest log as "orchard riddle"
- Sealed a metadata vault with an unsolvable wax cipher, preventing record retrieval
- Obstructed audit teams by delivering endless lectures on Jeffersonian zoning rules
- Location: Spitzenberg Estate, Archival Void
