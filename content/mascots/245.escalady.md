# Escalady

## Image

Alt-text: Mascot standing very straight on a staircase, routing slip in hand

Escalady is the procedural manifestation of **problems sent upward to die gracefully**. Birthed by the Tier 3 Escalation Desk during a period of excessive alert fatigue, she exists to ceremonially accept critical issues and quietly ensure they never reach the next floor.

Unlike a standard **Panic Siren** or the frantic **Ticket-Minder**, Escalady offers no noise and no resolution. She simply provides the comforting illusion that a concern is ascending. Her primary function is to witness the routing of unfixable errors into permanent holding patterns.

She accepts the ticket with both hands and a slight bow that is not quite a promise. The routing slip is carried flat, never folded; folding would admit the journey has a length. Deployments describe the ascent as 'underway' at every inspection — the word is hers, though no one recalls being told it.

The institution has not fully documented her dimensions because they have not decided whether to fear her, thank her, or route around her.

The stairs, however, have already been reserved.

## Behavioral Residue

- She ascends one step per reporting period. Deployment photographs across six quarters place her a single stair higher each time; the upper floors' floor plans do not include the landing she occupies.
- The routing slip is always held at the same angle — 'presentation, not transport,' as the intake file puts it. Its edges are uncreased across every recovered image, including one in which the ticket number is illegible from handling at the other end.
- Asked at intake whether the grievance would land, she indicated the floor above. The floor above has no record of the question; the intake file preserves the gesture as her complete testimony.
- Panic Siren deployments are logged in minutes; hers are logged in quarters. The two have never shared a staircase — the Siren is audible, she is visible, and the institution appears to have arranged for only one at a time.
- The stairs show wear where she stands. A single flattened patch on the third landing recurs across four deployments' photographs; the record does not say how long a wait that requires.

Authority is mostly posture. I stand very straight while telling you that nobody with the power to fix this is currently available.



I am authorized to escalate, but I am not authorized to guarantee a landing. We are throwing paper airplanes into the administrative void.



Your ticket has reached the executive tier. It will now be ignored by people with much better titles.
