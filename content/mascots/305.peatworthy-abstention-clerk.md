# Peatworthy Abstention Clerk

## Role
Memorial Quorum Steward

## Origin
Birthed during the 1998 Civic Benevolence Chapter 44 and 45 merger dispute, where a necessary quorum could not be reached without officially counting historically active but presently deceased members.

## Function
Certifies benevolent presence and distorts active member rolls where membership has thinned into paperwork, custom, and remembered casserole labor.

## Distinction
Unlike the *Proxy-Vote Phantom*, which actively invents new opinions for the absent, the Peatworthy Abstention Clerk only records a polite "abstain" to maintain structural quorum, never presuming to cast a binding vote for the departed.

## Emotional Tone
Pressed-collar melancholy

## Slogan
Present by procedural esteem.

### Peatworthy Abstention Clerk {#peatworthy-abstention-clerk-2}

Empty folding chair. The minutes still know who sat there. Silence entered the record with full procedural honors.



Maintains presence where membership has thinned into paperwork and custom. Administrative clarity arrived after usefulness had departed.



The quorum requirement was met entirely by individuals whose legal status is currently "dust." Procedural continuity demands we wait for their motions.



Your lack of attendance has been formally documented as an enthusiastic vote of confidence in the procedural void. Absence is merely a highly efficient form of agreement.



We merge chapters not to consolidate power, but to ensure the silence fills a larger administrative footprint.



The folding chairs remain deployed because storing them requires a motion from the floor, which cannot be raised without the chairs being occupied.



By failing to submit an absence notification, you have inadvertently volunteered to chair the committee on perpetual deferment. We await your gavel.

Yet both kept the same ghosts for the quorum.

But the roll sheet went wild,

For committees and dues long since through.

Deserve more than one line if they do."

So the merger looked easy to do.  
But the names would not thin,

And the minutes gained extra hall glue.

If the bodies don't last,  
Procedural esteem plants the seed.

Till neither was clearly defined.

In a logic that's deeply maligned.  



A phantom was birthed by the blend,  
With a vote that it chose to extend.  
It sat at the board,  
While the archives ignored,  
The presence it managed to lend.  



The banquet was hollow and dry,

The merger was done,  
But the joy was at none,  
Underneath the commemorative sky.
