# Mitigatrix Pending

Mitigatrix Pending is reserved for that familiar institutional asymmetry: a title broad enough to comfort, and an authority envelope *just* too narrow to act.

Born during the Great Permission Audit of '25 ([[lorelog/LLG-0325-ORT|LLG-0325-ORT]]), she witnesses the *persistent* gap between nominal authority and functional capacity.

Unlike the *Orphaned Process*, which executes without supervision, the Mitigatrix enjoys supervision in full — and is procedurally forbidden to use it.

She holds the title at full size and the authority at the size the audit left. Intake photographs confirm the furniture exactly as filed: throne at center, paper crown worn level, the scepter laid beside her rather than in her hand — unpowered, in the record's own wording. She sits correctly. There is, it seems, no documented way to sit wrong, since acting is the thing the office does not provide.

## Image

Alt-text: Mascot wearing a paper crown seated on a throne, a scepter with its batteries removed beside her

## Behavioral Residue

- She reads everything routed to her. Custody logs show mitigation requests opened, dated, and annotated in a hand the audit permitted; the authorization line beneath each annotation remains, *pointedly*, unsigned.
- Her signature exists and is in good order. It appears on acknowledgments, receipts, and the quarterly restatement of her own remit; it has *never* appeared on an order.
- The crown is kept presentable. Replacement pages for it are stocked alongside the forms — paper being, after all, a supplies item — and she changes it herself, without comment, at review time.
- Her supervision is complete on paper. Every report that should reach her does reach her; the audit that arranged this is the same one that removed the batteries, and whether she notes the *irony* is not recorded.
- Asked at intake whether the gap between title and capacity would close, she indicated the scepter. The intake file preserves the answer as 'It is with the committee.' — her complete testimony, *such as it was*.

## Aphorisms


### Mitigatrix Pending {#mitigatrix-pending-2}

My business card carries a very impressive title. My expense authority, somewhat less impressively, tops out at *fourteen dollars*. The mitigation must wait.



I am sitting in the throne of problem-solving, though somebody has taken the batteries out of the scepter. I look *commanding* while doing nothing.



They call me the Director of Crisis Resolution. My only tool is a form that dutifully forwards your complaint to an *unmonitored* inbox.



I stand ready to unleash the healing waters, just as soon as the committee completes its *review* of the environmental impact statement regarding the plumbing.



We gave her a grand title because we could not give her the power to heal us. She wears the paper crown with absolute, quietly heartbreaking dignity.



She sits in the throne of problem-solving, hands resting softly on the armrests, dreaming of the day the committee sees fit to grant her a scepter.



The inbox is a void where desperate pleas are sent to die, though the Mitigatrix polishes the routing form so *beautifully* that we forget it leads nowhere.



Store the mitigation requests carefully. One day the environmental impact statement will clear, and the healing waters will *eventually* wash the dust away.



To be named the savior of a system that declines all saving requires a *profound*, quiet grace. We bow to her unyielding, powerless stance.
