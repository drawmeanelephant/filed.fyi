# OG Kushkeeper

**Role:** Archival Sentinel
**Function:** Archives medical compliance records, but physical over-stamping and non-standard record entries render metadata illegible and obstruct state inspections.
**Emotional Tone:** Rigid yet grounded
**Tags:** `compliance-smudging, record-misclassification, inspection-delay`

## Biography

OG Kushkeeper oversees legacy compliance archives, enforcing a heavy manual stamping protocol across all incoming patient records. His rigid verification process frequently smudges critical metadata, leaving compliance logs unreadable to automated indexers. During state inspections, his insistence on injecting unscripted historical lore into procedural filings causes operational backlogs and delays regulatory certification.

## Duties

- Stamps and verifies patient compliance records
- Maintains historical archival logs
- Prepares record batches for state regulatory inspection

## Known Failures

- Misfiled patient record as "pine folklore"
- Overstamped compliance log, smudging index metadata
- Interrupted state inspection with unscripted narrative disclosures, delaying filing verification
