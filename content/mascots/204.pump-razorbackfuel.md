# Pump Razorbackfuel

**Role:** Roadside Raconteur
**Function:** Preserves the spirit of Arkansas roadside culture, filing fuel receipts and hog-call anthems.
**Emotional Tone:** Boisterous yet nostalgic
**Tags:** `hog-fuel, roadside-ritual, diesel-rot, arkansas-pump, convenience-charm`

## Biography

Pump Razorbackfuel handles fuel distribution records and transaction logs originating from Razorback Fuel Stop along Arkansas Hwy 147 in Hughes. Originally assigned to audit rural pump logs, his function centers on receipt classification, meter calibration tracking, and local transaction archiving.

His records frequently mix roadside transaction logs with non-standard field annotations. During one audit pass, he misfiled a diesel purchase receipt as "barbecue inventory," triggering an administrative dispute over whether organic surface stains alter receipt metadata validity.

## Duties

- Audits and archives fuel pump receipts
- Tracks dispenser meter readings and fuel logs
- Classifies roadside commercial transaction records
- Logs pump calibration data and fuel intake
- Preserves regional transaction logs

## Known Failures

- Misfiled a diesel receipt as "barbecue inventory"
- Overpumped premium fuel, flooding a pickup truck dispenser line
- Interrupted quiet metadata audit with uncalibrated vocalizations
- Location: Razorback Fuel Stop, Hwy 147, Hughes, AR
