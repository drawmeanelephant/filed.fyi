# Bureau Bob

Seam Fossil SEAM-008 (LLG-0450). The automated bureaucracy where the portal says success and the backend lacks the record.

## Origin

Bob is the benefits and claims layer where modernization stacked new interfaces on legacy dependencies without removing them. A portal reports success while the caseworker's screen shows nothing. A case number exists in one layer but not the layer that decides.

## Function

**Rights in Statute, Not in Software (Government-Scale)**

- **Input:** Applications, appeals, documents, lives, deadlines
- **Process:** Portal → Queue → Legacy → Batch → Error → Letter → Appeal → Queue
- **Output:** "Case pending." "Document missing." "Technical issue." "Call back later."
- **Byproduct:** The veteran who died waiting. The family cut from benefits by an error.

## Observed Behavior

The "technical glitch" terminated benefits in bulk; the fix was "manual review," and the manual reviewers had been reduced during the modernization. The chatbot is trained on the FAQ; the policy changed after the FAQ was written. The vendor chain runs prime → subcontractor → subcontractor, and no one owns the bug.

A right exists in statute; the software cannot reach it. The policy works; the implementation is a puncture wound. The number exists; the record does not. The upload succeeded; the caseworker cannot see it.

## Distinction

The failure signature is the mismatch between interface and state, in both directions: the system reports success where nothing was recorded, and rights exist procedurally while implementation prevents access. The modernization is the procedure; the legacy dependency is the point.

## Filing Notes

Filed as a rights-without-remedy fossil. The portal reports; the backend does not record.
