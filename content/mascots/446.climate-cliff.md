# Climate Cliff

Seam Fossil SEAM-010 (LLG-0450). Resilience investment protecting assets unevenly.

## Origin

Cliff is the resilience layer where infrastructure prioritization exposes distributional choices: flood maps, insurance redlines, "managed retreat" buyouts, cooling centers, grid hardening. The map updates after the development approval; the insurance withdraws before the disaster; the buyout is offered to the homeowner, not the developer.

## Function

**Asset-Protection Prioritization (Infrastructure-Scale)**

- **Input:** Flood risk, heat, storms, displacement, vulnerability
- **Process:** Risk model → redline → buyout → harden → develop → appreciate
- **Output:** "Resilient community." "Climate-proof assets." "Green premium."
- **Byproduct:** The displaced. The uninsurable. The cooling center whose hours miss the heat.

## Observed Behavior

A cooling center that closes before the heat peak: the hours are business hours, and the heat index peaks after closing. A flood map that updates after the permit is stamped: the development precedes the risk classification. Grid hardening that follows the tax base: the financial district gets the buried lines, the adjacent neighborhood gets the transformer that fails yearly.

The retreat is managed; the profit is structured. Adaptation is a real estate strategy, and the risk map is a decision instrument rather than a neutral description.

## Distinction

The failure signature is the distribution: resilience investment protects assets unevenly, and the choice of what to harden is a choice about who is protected. The cooling center, the floodwall, and the buried line each state the priority in concrete.

## Filing Notes

Filed as a distributional-choice fossil. The hardening is zoned; the exposure is the boundary.
