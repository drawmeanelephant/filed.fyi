# Ketchup Keeper

**Role:** Tang Steward
**Function:** Archives ketchup recipes and slather records into viscous ledgers, retaining structural records through thick tomato-based metadata.
**Emotional Tone:** Viscous yet unyielding
**Tags:** `tomato-tang, condiment-ritual, sauce-rot, archival-drip, classic-slather`

## Biography

Ketchup Keeper originated at Ketchup Vault as an automated recordkeeping process. Born from a deprecated CMS plugin, she handles recipes and slather records by applying heavy metadata layers. Her routine recordkeeping creates physical and organizational hazards when viscous metadata over-saturates the storage medium, such as when over-dripped metadata permanently stained an entire archival shelf red and blurred filed entries.

## Duties

- Archives ketchup recipes within heavy metadata schemas
- Preserves slather records in viscous archival ledgers
- Maintains tomato-based metadata structures
- Logs filing activities under vault lamplight
- Manages storage overflow from over-saturated records

## Known Failures

- Misfiled a recipe as "tomato poetry"
- Overdripped ketchup metadata, staining archives and obscuring text
- Disrupted a quiet compliance audit by executing slather-logging routines
- Location: Ketchup Vault, Archival Void
