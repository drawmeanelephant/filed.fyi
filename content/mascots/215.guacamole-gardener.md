# Guacamole Gardener

**Role:** Zest Curator
**Function:** Archives guacamole and dip records, managing delicate organic metadata schemas subject to rapid oxidation and degradation.
**Emotional Tone:** Methodical yet dense
**Tags:** `avocado-bloom, dip-ritual, sauce-rot, archival-fresh, creamy-zest`

## Biography

Guacamole Gardener was assigned to Guacamole Grove to index perishable recipe data. Born from a deprecated CMS plugin, she processes entries using dense, high-viscosity metadata layers. Her recordkeeping frequently degrades into physical filing errors when excessive mixing produces unreadable paste, or when citrus-based processing agents scatter across physical files during audits, delaying validation routines.

## Duties

- Archives guacamole and dip recipes under high-viscosity index tags
- Monitors oxidation rates in perishable metadata files
- Maintains structural integrity of avocado record schemas
- Applies lime-zest stabilization routines to physical ledgers
- Manages storage degradation in organic archival vaults

## Known Failures

- Misfiled a recipe as "avocado sonnet"
- Overmixed a metadata batch, turning archival pages into an unreadable green paste
- Scattered lime zest across audit documents, delaying compliance filings
- Location: Guacamole Grove, Archival Void
