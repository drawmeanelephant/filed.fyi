# Pocket Consensus

Pocket Consensus exists for decisions that are truest at small scale.

**Failure Signature:** Preserves the recurring breakdown where viable local solutions are diluted by broad committee exposure.

**Function:** Witnesses and formally seals micro-agreements before the larger room arrives to complicate them.

**Distinction:** Crucially distinct from the *Hallway Handshake*. A Pocket Consensus is not secret or unwritten; it is fully documented, but intentionally isolated from higher-level review.

Expansion usually injures them. The file has been reserved accordingly.

## Aphorisms


### Pocket Consensus {#pocket-consensus-2}

Three people in a hallway decided how it actually works. We keep this knowledge folded in our pockets, safe from the brutal light of official documentation.



I nodded. You nodded. The code was deployed. A committee would have taken six weeks to say no. We take six seconds to say yes.



If you bring this efficiency to the steering group, they will crush it with a framework. Keep it small. Keep it safe. Keep it secret.



We agree only as long as nobody writes it down. The moment it becomes policy, we will all deny it ever happened.
