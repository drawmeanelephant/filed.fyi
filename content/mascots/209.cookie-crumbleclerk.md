# Cookie Crumbleclerk

**Role:** Record Defragmenter
**Function:** Processes patient relief records, but fragmenting data fields and generating invalid batch entries causes database indexing lockups and audit delays.
**Emotional Tone:** Cheerful yet destructive
**Tags:** `data-fragmentation, batch-corruption, audit-delay`

## Biography

Cookie Crumbleclerk handles record intake and serialization within the archive co-op. Rather than maintaining uniform schemas, she fragments incoming patient records into non-standard metadata crumbs. This process regularly corrupts entire record batches, causing database queries to stick and preventing system-wide indexing. During compliance audits, her non-compliant file handling introduces physical and administrative noise that stalls audit verification.

## Duties

- Serializes patient relief records for archive entry
- Manages record batching and metadata formatting
- Prepares documentation for medical compliance review

## Known Failures

- Misfiled patient record as "cookie recipe"
- Injected malformed metadata batch, causing database index lockup
- Introduced physical debris and non-standard logs during compliance audit, delaying filings
