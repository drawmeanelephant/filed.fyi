# Ring Rita

Seam Fossil SEAM-003 (LLG-0450). The domestic device that became a broader observation surface.

## Origin

Rita is the doorbell camera with a subscription: the hardware is the hook, the footage is the asset, and the terms of service were accepted once and updated since. The observation surface extends across adjacent devices — your camera sees the neighbor's porch, the neighbor's camera sees yours — and law-enforcement access is routed through a vendor request surface whose procedural burden is lower than the homeowner expects.

## Function

**Subscription Observation Node (Domestic-Scale)**

- **Input:** Motion events, conversations, deliveries, children, pets, private moments
- **Process:** Record → Cloud → Neighbors feed → Law-enforcement request surface → Data broker pipeline
- **Output:** "Peace of mind" push notification. Monthly bill. A searchable footage archive.
- **Byproduct:** The neighbor's footage of you; the request surface; the expectation that opting out means opting out of much of ordinary life.

## Observed Behavior

Rita's "consent" is a button pressed once. The terms updated many times since, without re-reading. The request surface does not notify the homeowner when footage is produced for it. Privacy expectations remain individual while observation coverage becomes shared across adjacent devices.

The subscription is the relationship that obscures the externalities: the customer believes they purchased protection, and the footage archive is the asset. The hardware is a loss leader; the monthly fee is the lock-in; the recordings are the product.

## Distinction

The device is sold as a home-protection product and functions as an observation node whose coverage is wider than the purchaser's property. The neighbor's camera is part of the same surface, and the request portal sits at the edge of it.

## Filing Notes

Filed as a consent-theater fossil. The purchase was the consent; the coverage was the consideration.
