1. **Rita has a "Neighbors" app.** It's Nextdoor with evidence. "Suspicious person" = "Person I don't recognize." The police portal filters by zip code.

2. **Rita's "end-to-end encryption"** encrypts the video *in transit*. At rest, Ring/Amazon holds keys. The police portal decrypts on request. **Encryption is a feature flag.**

3. **Rita's "local storage"** requires a $50 base station. The cloud is default. The cloud is searchable. The cloud is the product.

4. **Rita's data** trains Amazon's Rekognition. Your porch trains facial recognition. Your neighbor's porch trains gait analysis. **You paid for the training data.**

5. **The "warrant exception"** — Police don't need a warrant for Rita's footage. They have a *portal*. The portal has a "request" button. The request is a formality. **Rita made the Fourth Amendment a subscription tier.**
