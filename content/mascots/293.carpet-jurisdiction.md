# Carpet Jurisdiction

Carpet Jurisdiction is held for spaces where décor stops behaving decoratively and begins issuing emotional law, apparently.

**Failure Signature**: A persistent architectural breakdown in which authority is quietly absorbed by acoustic dampening and high-pile threading, rather than *conferred* by title.

**Function**: It witnesses and certifies the structural bias wherein decisions made on carpet carry, for reasons best left unexamined, more legal weight than those made on linoleum.

**Distinction**: It is strictly distinct from the *Fluorescent Light Edict*. Where the Edict dictates exhaustion through overhead glare, Carpet Jurisdiction prefers to enforce silent spatial authority from the ground up.

Its authority is measured in pile depth. Deployment surveys show rulings logged at the footage where the threading begins — a decision rehearsed on the tile and issued on the plush arrives with a *noticeably* different weight, and the brass strip remains the one border in the archive that everyone observes and no one will minute. It does not hand down the law; it simply absorbs the sound of dissent until the law sounds unanimous.

No bylaw currently admits this.
Several rooms do.

## Behavioral Residue

- It is felt through the soles first. Intake files describe entrants adjusting posture at the threshold strip — the body files under the carpet's *jurisdiction* before the meeting has been called to order.
- Its reach ends, rather precisely, at the weave's edge. Recovered floor plans show rulings certified inside the pile and disputed on the tile; the same sentence carried across the brass strip arrives as a *proposal*.
- It has never issued a ruling of its own. The file keeps the distinction clean: decisions are made by people standing on it; the carpet supplies only the *acoustics* under which disagreement becomes inaudible.
- Its authority grew with the pile. Deployment notes correlate thread depth with ruling weight across recovered rooms; whether deeper carpet makes heavier law, or merely makes lighter law harder to hear, the file *politely* declines to say.
- Asked at intake where the decision had been made, it indicated the threshold. The intake file preserves the answer as 'on the soft side of the strip.' — its *entire* testimony.

## Aphorisms


### Carpet Jurisdiction {#carpet-jurisdiction-2}

The jurisdiction of the mandate ends precisely at the brass threshold strip. We stand on the industrial tile to register our disagreement *formally*.



We installed plush carpeting in the review room to muffle the sound of systemic complaints. The acoustics of the silence are now, we are told, *fully compliant*.



The thickness of the underlayment determines the weight of the policy. A ruling issued from a room with polished concrete is, it turns out, difficult to enforce.



When executive changes are proposed, we roll out the woven runner. The width of the fabric dictates, with some finality, the exact boundaries of the new ideas.



The compliance violation was mitigated by the retroactive incineration of the architectural blueprints. A structure cannot be found non-compliant, it turns out, if we retain no record of its *intended* shape.



We achieved absolute security by quarantining the theoretical threat within a restricted binder. The risk is now *perfectly* contained between two heavily reinforced plastic covers.



When the inspector noted a severe structural deviation, the committee promptly voted to abolish the inspector's department. The structural deviation, perhaps unsurprisingly, ceased to be an official concern.
