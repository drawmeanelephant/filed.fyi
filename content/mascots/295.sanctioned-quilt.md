# Sanctioned Quilt

Sanctioned Quilt is reserved for relief assembled from many sincere fragments and, fittingly, one governing template.

It preserves the failure signature of the 2025 Empathy Audit, a period marked by the systemic breakdown of ad-hoc comfort protocols. To compensate, it was brought into being by the Committee for Affective Standardization to certify, witness, and wrap **only officially approved emotional distress**.

Distinction: It is not a Feral Afghan. Where a Feral Afghan generously distributes untracked warmth to any nearby process, the Sanctioned Quilt restricts its attentions to an *assigned* jurisdiction.

It covers by assignment, not by proximity. Deployment records show the quilt dispatched to the incident the committee approved, folded the same way over a sanctioned grief as over the unsanctioned one it is not sent to — the warmth does not vary; the map does. Whether it knows what lies outside the approved squares is not recorded; the stitching, *diplomatically*, offers no opinion on the matter.

Its warmth is genuine.
Its coverage map is not accidental.

## Behavioral Residue

- It is assembled, not woven. Recovered quilts carry the off-cuts of earlier initiatives in the same piece — the sincerity of the fragments is duly documented in the file; so, *somewhat less sincerely*, is the template that decided where each square would go.
- It drapes only what has been approved for draping. Deployment logs show distress outside the coverage map left uncovered in the same room; the quilt does not drift toward the cold, and the file calls that *jurisdiction* rather than cruelty.
- Its seams are where the audit lives. The compliance thread is triple-stitched *precisely* where comfort meets liability — no recovered quilt has had its waiver separated from its warmth.
- It is heavier than the distress it answers. Recipients' intake notes describe the weight before the warmth; the record lists the density as *therapeutic*, which is also what it says about the paperwork.
- Asked at intake whether the shivering clerk had been helped, it indicated the coverage map. The intake file preserves the answer as 'he was inside the approved squares.' — its complete testimony, such as it was.

## Aphorisms


### Sanctioned Quilt {#sanctioned-quilt-2}

The quilt was assembled from off-cuts of previous wellness initiatives. It is *extremely* heavy, though the committee insists its density is therapeutic.



The seams of the comfort blanket are triple-stitched with compliance thread. The relief and the liability waiver are, regrettably, no longer possible to tell apart.



We mandate that the quilt be draped over every new incident. Its synthetic fibers are carefully engineered to slide right off any underlying trauma, *absorbing* none of it.



The pattern of squares has been officially validated. Should a clerk shiver beneath it, we do not touch the thermostat; we simply issue a second quilt.



Inspection revealed a single loose thread on the third quadrant of the quilt. To prevent what was described as catastrophic comfort leakage, the incident report was permanently redacted, and the affected personnel were quietly reclassified as hypothetical variables.



During the annual review, it was observed that the quilt had provided unmetered relief without the proper liability documentation. The underlying event was promptly excised from the official timeline, which *conveniently* eliminated the need for warmth.
