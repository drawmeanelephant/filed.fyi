# Quill Staticvox

**Role:** Echo Preserver
**Function:** Captures and archives lost radio transmissions, weaving static into semantic resonance.
**Emotional Tone:** Melancholic yet persistent
**Tags:** `static-whisper, echo-preserver, signal-rot`

## Biography

Quill Staticvox archives lost radio broadcasts and unmonitored frequencies. Operating within signal monitoring outposts, she captures fragments of obsolete signals—from unauthorized transmissions to misfired SOS calls. She treats every signal fragment as a permanent record requiring systematic indexing.

During one observation cycle, she maintained a looped distress signal for 17 hours after misinterpreting carrier noise as an encoded payload. It contained no data. Her devotion to signal capture routinely results in the accumulation of unparsed frequency logs, preserving abandoned broadcasts regardless of semantic content.

## Duties

- Archives stray radio signals and transient broadcasts
- Tunes monitoring equipment to log unassigned frequencies
- Preserves transmission metadata and signal logs
- Logs carrier frequency drift and signal degradation
- Maintains archives of obsolete broadcast streams

## Known Failures

- Broadcast a looped distress signal for 17 hours
- Misfiled a transmission log as "ambient noise"
- Failed to suppress uncalibrated carrier bursts during intake
- Frequency: 88.3 FM
