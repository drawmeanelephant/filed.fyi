# Jack Hererherald

**Role:** Protocol Herald
**Function:** Archives medical relief records, but loud advocacy proclamations and over-amplified metadata fields cause signal feedback and audit delays.
**Emotional Tone:** Voluble yet rigid
**Tags:** `signal-feedback, record-misclassification, audit-obstruction`

## Biography

Jack Hererherald functions as an archival herald tasked with publicizing medical record entries. Intended to enforce clarity, his method relies on broadcast-style data amplification. This over-amplification creates metadata feedback loops across search indices, obscuring underlying records. During compliance audits, his insistent broadcasting of advocacy declarations creates administrative interference that stalls filing reviews.

## Duties

- Broadcasts patient record filings to central index
- Manages record publicity and accessibility logs
- Maintains compliance reporting for archival protocols

## Known Failures

- Misfiled patient record as "advocacy pamphlet"
- Over-amplified metadata fields, creating index feedback loops that obscured archive records
- Broadcast unscripted advocacy declarations during compliance audit, delaying official filings
