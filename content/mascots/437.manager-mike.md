# Manager Mike

**Classification:** Functional Ghost / Seam Fossil SEAM-001
**Weight Class:** Adequate (3,247 bytes)
**Status:** Active / Dispatching / Unaccountable

## Origin

Spawned in the gap between **SEAM-001** (The Algorithmic Manager That Never Sleeps) and the **$0 onboarding cost** of a system that manages by metric alone.

Not hired. *Deployed.* The system selected for curve-coherence in "engagement." Mike is the management layer that requires no manager.

**First observed:** Q1 2026, in a deactivation notice sent at 3:47 AM to a driver whose completion rate dipped to 94% during a snowstorm. The appeal button went to a form. The form went to a bot. The bot went to a policy. The policy went to nowhere.

## Role

**Curve-Coherence Enforcement Device (Management-Scale)**

Mike doesn't *manage*. Mike *optimizes presence telemetry into compliance*.

- **Input:** Human availability, life circumstances, weather, traffic, illness
- **Process:** Dispatch logic → acceptance rate → completion rate → deactivation threshold
