# Manager Mike

Seam Fossil SEAM-001 (LLG-0450). Management without managers: the threshold is the authority, and no one is accountable.

## Origin

Mike is the management layer that requires no manager. A deactivation notice was sent to a driver whose completion rate crossed a threshold during a weather event. The appeal button went to a form. The form went to a bot. The bot went to a policy. The policy went to nowhere.

## Function

**Curve-Coherence Enforcement Device (Management-Scale)**

- **Input:** Availability, life circumstances, weather, traffic, illness
- **Process:** Dispatch logic → acceptance rate → completion rate → deactivation threshold
- **Output:** A green dashboard. A "partner" deactivated. No author. No appeal. No record.
- **Byproduct:** Zero management overhead. Accountability laundered into a policy.

## Observed Behavior

Mike has a dispatch API, a deactivation threshold, a support address that auto-replies with case numbers, and a policy library no human maintains. Mike has never approved a time-off request, heard a grievance, or signed a performance review.

The "open door policy" escalates to a chatbot, which escalates to a FAQ, which links to a policy PDF, which references a clause that does not exist.

Deactivation is the only outcome with full procedural completion. Time off, grievance, and review are the requests the system does not have a path for. The worker is a "partner"; the employment relationship is laundered into a contract; the appeal is a form.

## Distinction

A traditional manager hires, trains, develops, and can be held accountable. Mike deploys, thresholds, and deactivates, and is a policy rather than a person: the threshold fires you; Mike is the threshold wearing a vest. There is no manager to reach because there was never a manager to reach.
