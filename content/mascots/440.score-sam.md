# Score Sam

Seam Fossil SEAM-004 (LLG-0450). The opaque risk score whose output gains authority disproportionate to its explainability.

## Origin

Sam is the three-digit gatekeeper: across the cited intake cases, proprietary or externally supplied scores were treated as authoritative inputs in decisions involving access — consumer credit, tenant screening, employment risk, and insurance risk are structurally similar but not necessarily the same system. The score's basis is asserted as confidential; the methodology is asserted as a trade secret; appeal does not reach the model.

## Function

**Unappealable Gatekeeper (Life-Scale)**

- **Input:** Payment history, utilization, and whatever else the model uses — not disclosed
- **Process:** Proprietary model → three-digit number → binary gate
- **Output:** "Approved." "Denied." "Manual review" (a category the cited cases did not reach).
- **Byproduct:** The subject optimizing behavior for the score; the score never explaining itself.

## Observed Behavior

The "reason code" output names a factor — "Payment History," "Utilization" — without stating the weight, the threshold, or the model. Appeal does not reach the model; the model's output is treated as a decision rather than as an input to one.

Where a subject's circumstances generate data that lowers the score — late rent, higher deposits, more late rent — the loop is self-confirming. Being poor generates the data that keeps you poor.

Subjects learn to perform for the score: credit-building routines, optimized timing, curated account behavior. The score rewards the shape of the behavior, not its cause.

## Distinction

The redlining comparison is structural, not literal. The archive does not establish that every model uses race, or any particular set of proxy variables. What is documented is an opaque score whose basis cannot be examined, whose output cannot be meaningfully appealed, and whose authority is disproportionate to its explainability.

## Filing Notes

Filed as an unappealable-math fossil. The basis is confidential; the appeal does not reach the model.
