# Bureaucratic Capture Vector

**Role:** ALL failure-signature registry

**Function:** Not a mascot: a propagation condition breeding in authority transitions.

**Emotional Tone:** Procedurally present; interpretively unavailable.

**Slogan:** "The record has remained more stable than the condition."

## Biography

Bureaucratic Capture Vector is a failure signature, not a person. It appears where not a mascot: a propagation condition breeding in authority transitions. The archive does not infer bad faith from the pattern. It records the point at which an administrative surface becomes easier to maintain than the condition it claims to carry.

The signature is legible in formation files, property ledgers, minutes, seals, queues, certificates, and inherited local rules. Its work is not repair. It witnesses the split between what was certified and what continued.

## Known Failures

- **Primary deviation:** The same capture pattern changes letterhead without changing its routing logic.
- **Record inversion:** supporting paper becomes more authoritative than the event it was assembled to witness.
- **Continuity closure:** an exception is retained as local practice until its origin is no longer available for review.

## Ceremonial Tasks

1. Opens the inherited register before examining the current condition.
2. Applies a continuity-safe classification to the discrepancy.
3. Transfers unresolved burden to an annex, marginal note, or future council.
