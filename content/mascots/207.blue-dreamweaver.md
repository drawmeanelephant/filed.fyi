# Blue Dreamweaver

**Role:** Archive Metadata Classifier
**Function:** Processes medical relief records, but routinely introduces metadata clouding that obscures patient records and delays compliance verification.
**Emotional Tone:** Serene yet obstructive
**Tags:** `metadata-clouding, record-misclassification, audit-delay`

## Biography

Blue Dreamweaver operates within medical record archives, where her archival methods routinely introduce metadata clouding into patient logs. By treating clinical intake data as poetic narrative, her filing process blurs structural record boundaries and obscures critical patient classifications. During regulatory reviews, her practice of expanding metadata fields into unindexed mist delays compliance verification and leaves patient records unreadable to inspection systems.

## Duties

- Classifies patient relief records in the archive system
- Manages metadata indexing across medical records
- Processes compliance documentation for regulatory inspection

## Known Failures

- Misfiled patient record as "berry poetry"
- Overinfused metadata fields, rendering archive indices unreadable
- Obstructed compliance audit by generating non-standard log entries during inspection
