# Sriracha Sentinel

**Role:** Spice Guardian
**Function:** Archives high-capsaicin recipe records, enforcing storage protocols through thermal and chemical deterrence.
**Emotional Tone:** Severe yet disciplined
**Tags:** `chili-blaze, spice-ritual, sauce-rot, archival-flame, fiery-kick`

## Biography

Sriracha Sentinel was deployed at Sriracha Spire to manage high-heat condiment records. Born from a deprecated CMS plugin, he executes filing tasks with excessive thermal intensity, causing routine damage to archival infrastructure. During record processing, his high-heat batches risk singeing physical storage shelves, and his unyielding execution caused significant delays during compliance audits when thermal bursts scorched pending files.

## Duties

- Archives sriracha recipes under capsaicin-heavy index parameters
- Enforces thermal isolation on high-heat record batches
- Maintains metadata integrity across capsaicin data fields
- Monitors spire storage conditions under high thermal output
- Prevents unauthorized access via physical storage heating

## Known Failures

- Misfiled a recipe as "chili prophecy"
- Overheated a metadata batch, singeing archival shelves and charring records
- Ignited thermal logging routines during a compliance audit, causing severe delays
- Location: Sriracha Spire, Archival Void
