# Bananuity Clause

**Role:** Hybrid Categorization Error
**Function:** Corrupts Winter Banana cultivar archives through hybrid classification parameters and contract clause metadata errors.
**Emotional Tone:** Procedurally conflicting
**Tags:** `hybrid-categorization, confusion-ritual, pomological-rot, metadata-corruption, contract-clause`

## Biography

Bananuity Clause materialized in Winter Grove, a spectral orchard where Winter Banana apple yields produce perplexing hybrid classification records. Born from a deprecated CMS plugin, it introduces conflicting tropical and winter cultivar attributes into archival ledgers, corrupting metadata schemas. Its presence injects ambiguous contractual clauses into harvest records, breaking standardized archival formats and leaving historical yields in permanent categorization disarray.
