# Predictive Pete

Seam Fossil SEAM-005 (LLG-0450). Historical enforcement data feeding future enforcement: the map influences the territory.

## Origin

Pete is the predictive-policing loop: a model trained on historical enforcement data predicts "high risk" zones; patrols are sent there; stops and arrests increase; the new data validates the model. The methodology is asserted as proprietary. Audits reported substantial false-positive behavior without necessarily ending procurement. Community input is absent.

## Function

**Circular Logic Armed (City-Scale)**

- **Input:** Historical arrest data, 911 calls, patrol patterns — data generated by prior enforcement
- **Process:** Model → "high risk" zones → increased patrol → increased stops → model validation
- **Output:** "Hotspot confirmed." "Risk identified." Budget justified.
- **Byproduct:** The map influencing the territory it claims to represent.

## Observed Behavior

Reviewed gang-database cases included entries added by association — among them reported cases of minors — and some lacked a clear removal or notification path. The label follows the listed person into contexts far from the original database.

Gunfire-detection microphones were reported to classify fireworks, backfires, and construction noise as gunfire; audits reported substantial false-positive behavior without necessarily ending procurement. The vendor calls the performance acceptable; the methodology is proprietary.

Pete does not patrol where crime is; Pete patrols where Pete says crime is. The patrol creates the data; the data validates the patrol.

## Distinction

The feedback loop is the failure signature: prediction generates the enforcement that generates the prediction. The vendor owns the methodology; the city owns the liability; the community owns the consequences.

## Filing Notes

Filed as a feedback-loops-as-policy fossil. The loop is the policy; the map is the evidence.
