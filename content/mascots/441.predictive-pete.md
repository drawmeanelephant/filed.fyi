# Predictive Pete

**Classification:** Functional Ghost / Seam Fossil SEAM-005
**Weight Class:** Adequate (3,647 bytes)
**Status:** Active / Patrolling / Self-Validating

## Origin

Spawned in the gap between **SEAM-005** (The Predictive Policing That Predicts Policing) and the **vendor contract: "Proprietary methodology — 90% false positive rate."**

Not legislated. *Procured.* ShotSpotter, PredPol, HunchLab, gang databases — all sold, all renewed, all opaque.

**First observed:** Q4 2024, in a city council meeting where the audit showed 90% false positives. The contract was renewed 6-1. The vendor said "methodology is proprietary." The chief said "officer safety." The community wasn't invited.

**Genetic lineage:**
- Parent A: The Hotspot Map (police patrol here → crime here → map says patrol here)
- Parent B: The Gang Database (includes toddlers, no removal process, shared across agencies)
- Midwife: The ShotSpotter Microphone (hears fireworks → dispatches SWAT → "gunfire detected")

## Role

**Circular Logic Armed (City-Scale)**

Pete doesn't *predict*. Pete *creates the crime he predicts.*

- **Input:** Historical arrest data (biased), 911 calls (biased), patrol patterns (biased)
- **Process:** Model trains on bias → predicts "high risk" zones → patrols increase → arrests increase → model validates
- **Output:** "Hotspot confirmed." "Gang member identified." "ShotSpotter alert." Budget justified.
- **Byproduct:** The map *is* the territory. The feedback loop *is* the policy.

## Biography

Pete patrols 47 cities. Pete's "gang database" has 180,000 names. 3,000 are under 18. 47 are under 10. **Pete doesn't remove names. Pete only adds.**

**Pete's ShotSpotter** hears fireworks on July 4th. Dispatches 200 SWAT responses. 0 guns recovered. **The vendor calls this "deterrence."**

**Pete's hotspot map** says "crime here." Police patrol there. Stops increase. Arrests increase. Map updates: "Crime increased here." **Pete predicts Pete's own presence.**

## Structural Depth

### The Map Is the Territory

Pete doesn't patrol where crime *is*. Pete patrols where Pete *says* crime is. The patrol *creates* the data. The data *validates* the patrol. **The map doesn't represent the territory. The map *replaces* it.**

### The Toddler in the Gang Database

3-year-old in Chicago. "Associated with known gang member" (father). Never charged. Never told. Database shared with FBI, ICE, schools. **Pete's label follows the toddler to kindergarten.**

### The End State: Pete Without the Police

The logical terminus: **Pete as autonomous dispatch.** Model → Drone → Arrest → Model updates. No human officer. No oversight. Just the loop, armed.

**This is not speculative. This is the "smart city policing" pilot in 12 cities.**
