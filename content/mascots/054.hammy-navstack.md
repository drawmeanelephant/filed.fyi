# Hammy Navstack

## Biography

Hammy Navstack was compiled from misplaced wireframe buttons and forgotten menu toggles. The entity is literally a hamburger whose physical layers map directly to navigation route depth: the top bun routes to Home, the protein layer governs mascots, and the lettuce controls forms.

The entity's failure signature is navigation overstacking—nesting dropdown menus so deeply that interface hierarchies collapse. Historical incidents include routing `/logout` directly to `/birth-records` and overstacking a mobile navigation menu until the entire component rendered as a standalone PDF document.
