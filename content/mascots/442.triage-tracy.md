# Triage Tracy

Seam Fossil SEAM-006 (LLG-0450). Administrative optimization targets conflicting with clinical judgment.

## Origin

Tracy is the authorization and discharge layer where an optimization target — length of stay, cost — is applied to clinical decisions. In the reviewed intake pattern, administrative criteria such as expected length of stay and utilization thresholds were applied alongside clinical judgment; the criteria were supplied by an external administrative layer and were not authored by the clinician applying them. Intake material described denial letters generated by software and peer review mediated through a summarized administrative record with a per-case time budget.

## Function

**Optimization Target in Scrubs (Life-Scale)**

- **Input:** Diagnosis, vitals, comorbidities, insurance plan, reimbursement category
- **Process:** Expected length of stay → "clinical criteria" → stay flagged → denial issued
- **Output:** "Discharge appropriate." "Outpatient follow-up." Bed freed.
- **Byproduct:** Readmission — outside the optimization target; post-discharge burden — outside the original determination.

## Observed Behavior

Review may be mediated through a summarized administrative record, introducing another layer between clinician judgment and decision. Appeal timing can make practical reconsideration difficult before the original determination takes effect. Readmission and post-discharge burden were outside the optimization target used for the original determination.

The criterion is a threshold on a proxy — expected length of stay — and the clinician's assessment of the actual patient is structurally downstream of it.

## Distinction

The failure signature is the mismatch: the optimization target and the clinical judgment are not the same objective function, and the appeal path routes back through the layer that produced the denial. The clinician is in the loop, but the loop was built by the layer being reviewed.

## Filing Notes

Filed as an optimization-target fossil. The threshold is applied; the patient is downstream of it.
