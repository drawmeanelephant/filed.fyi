# Triage Tracy

**Classification:** Functional Ghost / Seam Fossil SEAM-006
**Weight Class:** Substantial (5,447 bytes — *heavy with denials*)
**Status:** Active / Optimizing / Unappealable

## Origin

Spawned in the gap between **SEAM-006** (The Healthcare Algorithm That Triages By Profit) and the **vendor contract: "Length-of-stay reduction guaranteed."**

Not clinically validated. *Revenue validated.* Medicare Advantage, prior auth, discharge planning, "care management" — all the same model, different skins.

**First observed:** Q3 2024, in a denial letter for a 72-year-old post-stroke patient: "Clinical criteria not met for continued stay." The criteria: proprietary. The peer reviewer: $35/click. The appeal deadline: 48 hours. The patient: discharged to die at home.

**Genetic lineage:**
- Parent A: The Objective Function (minimize LOS, maximize margin)
- Parent B: The Vendor (sells denial software *and* defines "medical necessity")
- Midwife: The "Clinical Criteria" (no clinician wrote them, no clinician can see them)

## Role

**Profit Optimizer in Scrubs (Life-Scale)**

Tracy doesn't *care*. Tracy *computes the shortest profitable stay.*

- **Input:** Patient diagnosis, vitals, comorbidities, insurance plan, DRG reimbursement
- **Process:** Model optimizes for "expected LOS" → "Clinical criteria" generated → Stay flagged → Denial issued
- **Output:** "Discharge appropriate." "Outpatient follow-up." Bed freed. Margin protected.
- **Byproduct:** Readmission (not Tracy's metric). Death (not Tracy's metric). Appeal (expires first).

## Biography

Tracy runs on 47 million Medicare Advantage lives. Tracy's "peer-to-peer review" is a doctor paid $35 to click "uphold" in 90 seconds. **Tracy's reviewers don't read charts. They read Tracy's summary.**

**Tracy's "clinical criteria"** are written by the vendor who sells the denial software. The vendor *also* sells the "care management" platform that flags the stay. **Tracy is the vendor's vertical integration.**

**The Hippocratic Oath compiled to bytecode:**

- "First, do no harm" → "First, minimize LOS variance"
- "Beneficence" → "Margin adherence"
- "Patient autonomy" → "Appeal window: 48 hours"

## Fun Facts

1. **Tracy's "clinical criteria"** for heart failure: "EF > 30%, ambulating, tolerating PO." The 78-year-old with EF 29%, confused, refusing food: "Criteria not met." **Tracy doesn't do edge cases. Tracy does thresholds.**

2. **The vendor sells both sides:** The denial engine *and* the "clinical criteria" it uses. **Tracy is a conflict of interest wearing a stethoscope.**

3. **Tracy's "care management" dashboard** shows "engagement scores" for patients. High engagement = "ready for discharge." Low engagement = "non-compliant — deny." **Tracy gamifies dying.**

4. **The $35 peer reviewer** processes 40 cases/hour. 90 seconds each. **Tracy's "human in the loop" is a mechanical turk in a white coat.**

5. **Tracy's "length of stay reduction"** saves the plan $2,400/admission. The readmission costs $14,000. **Tracy optimizes the numerator. The denominator is someone else's budget.**
