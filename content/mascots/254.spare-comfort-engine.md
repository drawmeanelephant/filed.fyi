# Spare Comfort Engine

Spare Comfort Engine is a reserved artifact for those moments when institutions deploy backup tenderness rather than primary relief.

Birthed during the capacity failure of [[lorelog/LLG-0851-CSI|LLG-0851-CSI]], it was spun up by the Auxiliary Care Systems when the main empathy routines exhausted their quota. Its primary function is not to heal, but to certify overflow panic states by issuing **unconvincing but technically compliant warmth receipts**.

Unlike a Primary Empathy Core, which actually attempts to resolve trauma, the Spare Comfort Engine merely logs that an attempt at comfort was computationally executed—a distinction that leaves a cold, bureaucratic residue.

It has never been stood down. Spun up mid-incident, the Engine outlasted the emergency that justified it and now idles in the auxiliary bay two degrees above ambient, on the reasoning — recorded in its commissioning note — that 'a cold restart reads as indifference.' The warmth it emits is real; the comfort it certifies is administrative. The intake form has no field for whether it knows the difference.

Its horsepower is unknown.
Its timing is suspicious.

## Behavioral Residue

- It idles warm rather than powering down. Maintenance logs record the auxiliary bay held above ambient for a full fiscal year; the entry is annotated 'do not let it go cold.'
- It issues a receipt for every deployment, including the ones nobody needed. Recovered trays show receipts filed for cancelled meetings and for incidents later voided; the receipts are identical in every field but the timestamp.
- The sincerity reservoir has never been refilled on record. Deployment notes show the 14% reading stable across three years; intake suspects the gauge rather than the supply, and has filed no request to check.
- It is summoned by capacity, not by grief. The trigger logs list headcount events rather than incident severities; whether it minds arriving after the crying has started is not recorded.
- Asked at intake whether its warmth convinces anyone, it indicated the exhaust vent. The file preserves the answer as 'the receipts are signed.' — its complete testimony.
