# Sentiment Launderette

## Image

Alt-text: Launderette mascot tumbling raw feedback forms in a wash cycle, lint trap catching grit

The Sentiment Launderette witnesses the habitual flattening of complex workplace distress into dashboard-safe affect. Birthed by the Executive Dashboarding Working Group following the Q3 Employee Sentiment Survey Backlash, it preserves the compensatory ritual of stripping context from feedback until it is **admissible for KPI presentations**.

**Function:** It certifies that emotional data has been successfully bleached of nuance and human friction.

**Distinction:** Unlike ambient vibe monitors that simply measure the room's tension, the Launderette actively processes and neutralizes it. If a file passes through here, it emerges structurally clean, flat, and entirely harmless.

It is not fast, and the slowness is part of the certification. A grievance that enters urgent leaves 'processed'; the wash is the delay by which urgency becomes admissible. What the cycle cannot clean it does not release — the residue stays in the trap, and the trap is the only part of the system that fills.

## Behavioral Residue

- The lint trap is the record. Technicians instructed to empty it weekly have twice refused, on grounds that it contained 'the actual survey'; the second refusal was filed as the first, because the first had already been laundered.
- Cycle times are fixed, and nothing exits early. A grievance submitted at 09:00 emerges at the same texture as one submitted at 16:00; urgency is a pre-wash attribute.
- Its attributed utterance is the cycle-end tone, rendered in the intake file as 'a sound like closure.' Agents are not instructed to apologize for it; no instruction exists.
- It has never been photographed empty. Deployment images show the drum mid-turn; what it tumbles between survey cycles is not recorded, and the record does not say it is nothing.
- The trap's contents are incinerated on the same schedule as the files they replaced. Disposal is logged as performed; the performing party is not recorded, and the record does not say who burns the lint.
