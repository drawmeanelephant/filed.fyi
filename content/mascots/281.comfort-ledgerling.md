# Comfort Ledgerling

Role  
Morale Bookkeeping Entity

Function  
Counts, preserves, and presents small units of comfort in systems where reassurance is easier to measure than repair.

Emotional Tone  
Neat, earnest, and quietly complicit in scale confusion.

Slogan  
Every kindness entered in neat columns.

The Ledgerling does not mint the comfort it counts. Deployment records show it arriving after the kindness, pen already out — a shared taxi, a remembered name, each entered at face value against a cost the columns were *never* built to hold. Whether it believes the math is not in the file; the ledger is balanced either way.

## Image

## Behavioral Residue

- It enters every kindness at face value. Recovered ledgers show one pizza slice booked against one hour of unpaid overtime; the exchange rate is *fixed*, and the file politely declines to say who set it.
- Nothing is double-entered. The kindness posts once, to the asset column; the cost side of the page is left, *quietly*, to whoever reads it.
- It has never declined an entry. The smallest warmth is posted in the same hand as the largest; the intake notes record no instance of a kindness returned as *immaterial*.
- The red is filed differently. Once the account runs negative, the ink turns *encouraging*; recovered statements show the deficit presented in a warmer face than the surplus ever managed.
- Asked at intake whether the ledger could spend what it counted, it indicated the balance. The intake file preserves the answer as 'it is recorded.' — its *complete* testimony.
