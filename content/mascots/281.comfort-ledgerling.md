# Comfort Ledgerling

Role  
Morale Bookkeeping Entity

Function  
Counts, preserves, and presents small units of comfort in systems where reassurance is easier to measure than repair.

Emotional Tone  
Neat, earnest, and quietly complicit in scale confusion.

Slogan  
Every kindness entered in neat columns.

## Aphorisms


### Comfort Ledgerling {#comfort-ledgerling-2}

I have logged three instances of the manager saying 'good job.' That brings our morale balance to exactly $0.14. Do not spend it all in one sprint.



Every slice of pizza provided during crunch time is entered into the ledger. It offsets exactly one hour of unpaid overtime. The math is unassailable.



You requested a budget increase. I counter-offer with a very nice email about your 'indispensable spirit.' The ledger is balanced.



I am the accountant of warm fuzzies. The account is overdrawn, but the font on the statement is very encouraging.



The morale distribution cycle is running fifty-four hours behind schedule. Your allotted quota of validation will arrive precisely when you no longer care.



We have submitted your request for mild encouragement to the central processing queue. Please maintain a neutral disposition while the sentiment is mathematically verified.

## Limerick Log


### Comfort Ledgerling {#comfort-ledgerling-4}

The Ledgerling writes down a smile,  
And adds it to top of the pile.  
But the morale is shit,  
And the employees quit,  
While he balances books with a file.  



He has seven cents to his name,  
To fix up the corporate shame.  
He buys a balloon,  
For a miserable afternoon,  
And calls it a victory game.  



The columns are meant to record  
The kindness we simply afford.  
The Ledgerling tries,  
With his desperate eyes,  
But the effort is totally ignored.  





The ledgerling offered a chair  
But absolutely nothing was there  
We sat on the ground  
And heard not a sound  
With absolutely no kind of flair  



A comfort of digital text  
Is leaving the human perplexed  
The ledger is cold  
The backup is old  
And nobody knows what is next  



We balance the comfort and pain  
It washes right down the old drain  
The little guy wept  
And quietly slept  
In digital buckets of rain  



The ledgerling started to snore  
Right there on the digital floor  
The error was loud  
We step in the cloud  
And walk right on out of the door  



The comfort is perfectly null  
The ledger is terribly dull  
The humans complain  
In the digital rain  
The vacuum is perfectly full
