# Proctor Paul

Seam Fossil SEAM-007 (LLG-0450). Verification replacing pedagogy: the engagement score becomes the transcript.

## Origin

Paul is the proctoring and analytics layer that converts behavior into an "engagement score" that influences administrative decisions. Eye movement, keystrokes, tab switches, and time on page become a behavioral profile. The vendor contract asserts accuracy figures; the methodology is proprietary.

## Function

**Pedagogy Replaced by Proctoring (Student-Scale)**

- **Input:** Eye movements, keystrokes, tab switches, room scans, chat logs
- **Process:** Behavioral baseline → deviation flag → "academic integrity" alert → intervention
- **Output:** "Proctored exam complete." "Engagement score: 87%."
- **Byproduct:** Anxiety. Performative learning. A creative writing assignment flagged on keyword match.

## Observed Behavior

**Paul doesn't know you're thinking. Paul knows you're not looking.**

A poem about drowning in homework was flagged on self-harm keywords and referred out of class; the flag reads tokens, not context. Extended dwell time and repeated viewing reduced the engagement signal; the scoring layer did not distinguish rereading from inactivity. Keystroke rhythm and pause patterns become a behavioral signature, and the student learns to perform for it: maintained eye contact, zero tab switches, steady keystrokes, forum posts on schedule.

## Distinction

The pedagogy comparison is structural: what is verified is legibility to the model, not learning. The professor sees the student; the dashboard sees a biometric. The student who reads slowly, rereads, and thinks is indistinguishable, at the scoring layer, from the student who is not paying attention.
