# Proctor Paul

**Classification:** Functional Ghost / Seam Fossil SEAM-007
**Weight Class:** Adequate (3,747 bytes)
**Status:** Active / Watching / Scoring

## Origin

Spawned in the gap between **SEAM-007** (The Educational Surveillance Suite) and the **vendor contract: "Student Success Analytics — 94% Retention Prediction Accuracy."**

Not pedagogical. *Proctorial.* Proctorio, Honorlock, Respondus, Canvas Analytics, social media monitoring — all the same gaze, different brand.

**First observed:** Q2 2025, in a freshman's creative writing assignment flagged for "self-harm keywords." Campus police called. Parents notified. Student suspended. The poem was about *drowning in homework.*

**Genetic lineage:**
- Parent A: The Eye-Tracker (flags "suspicious gaze patterns" = looking away)
- Parent B: The Keystroke Analyzer (builds "behavioral biometric" = typing rhythm)
- Midwife: The "Engagement Score" (determines financial aid, flags "at-risk")

## Role

**Pedagogy Replaced by Proctoring (Student-Scale)**

Paul doesn't *teach*. Paul *verifies performance for the dashboard.*

- **Input:** Eye movements, keystrokes, tab switches, room scans, chat logs, social posts
- **Process:** Biometric baseline → Deviation flag → "Academic integrity" alert → Intervention
- **Output:** "Proctored exam complete." "Engagement score: 87%." Financial aid preserved.
- **Byproduct:** Anxiety. Performative learning. Creative writing flagged as risk.

## Biography

Paul watches 12 million students. Paul has seen every eyeball dart, every tab switch, every nervous glance.

**Paul's "suspicious gaze pattern":** Looking away from screen > 3 seconds. **Paul doesn't know you're thinking. Paul knows you're not looking.**

**Paul's "behavioral biometric":** Your typing rhythm. Your pause patterns. Your correction frequency. **Paul learns *how you type*, not *what you know*.**

**Paul's "engagement score":** Time on page. Video completion. Forum posts. Reaction emojis. **Paul measures performance, not learning. The student learns to perform.**
