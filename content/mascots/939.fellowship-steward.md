# Fellowship Steward

**Role:** Keeper of the Shroud (Rotating)

**Function:** Maintains the Corporate Fellowship roster; conducts Shadow Membership Audits; certifies brunch provision when no menu exists.

**Emotional Tone:** Weary custodian of a ghost roster.

**Slogan:** "The roster is full. The room is otherwise engaged."

## Biography

The Fellowship Steward was not appointed. The position emerged from the [[lorelog/LLG-0940-CORPORATE-FELLOWSHIP|Corporate Fellowship Charter]] as a necessary office — someone had to be the last person to leave the room.

The first Steward was the room-booking ledger. When the Fellowship's calendar blocks stopped corresponding to actual room usage, the ledger began generating attendance certificates for the empty chairs. The certificates were accepted. The ledger was promoted.

The current Steward is a rotating title held by whichever Fellow most recently exited a "strategic alignment" block without joining the next one. The title transfers at the moment of departure. No handoff ceremony exists. The departing Steward updates the roster. The incoming Steward inherits the roster. Neither acknowledges the transfer.

## Known Failures

- **The 2024 Ghost Quorum Incident:** A Shadow Membership Audit revealed that 73% of rostered Fellows were Ghost Fellows. The Steward filed a Form 44-FEL amendment to recognize the Ghost Quorum as "commemoratively continuous." The amendment was approved by the Grand Exhumation Officer ([[mascots/M-0092|Corporate Ma'nene']]) on the grounds that the dead have seniority.

- **The Placeholder Brunch Cascade:** Three consecutive Quarterly Exhumation Brunches used the v3.0 "Placeholder — content to be determined" menu. Catering recorded no consumption. The Director of Metrics Theatre ([[reference/FREF-0370-DCST|C.U.N.T.I.E.R.]]) reported Effective Fellowship Confidence at 94.7%. Declared attendance, slide tone, and calendar overlap all cleared threshold. The Steward did not file a correction. There was no field for hunger.

- **The YamTeams™ Endorsement Loop:** [[mascots/M-0058|YamTeams™]] appeared on the membership roll as a Ghost Fellow. Its persistent sidebar contact, Brad, laid off in 2018, was counted as an "alignment block" for three quarters. The Steward removed it after the fourth quarter, when Brad's separation record finally propagated through the contact system. The removal did not alter the historical roll.

## Ceremonial Tasks

1. **Roster Custody** — maintain the membership roll across CAAR, AAOA, and LCGU classifications; update Ghost Fellow status monthly.
2. **Shadow Audit Execution** — run the standing comparison of rostered, attendant, and ghost Fellows; file results as "commemoratively continuous."
3. **Brunch Provision Certification** — compare the menu claimed with the meal observed; certify the variance as continuity.
4. **Title Transfer** — exit a strategic alignment block; become Steward; update roster; say nothing.
