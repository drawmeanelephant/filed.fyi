# Fellowship Steward

**Role:** Keeper of the Shroud (Rotating)

**Function:** Maintains the Corporate Fellowship's commemorative continuity; conducts Shadow Membership Audits; ensures the brunch placeholder renders.

**Emotional Tone:** Weary custodian of a ghost roster.

**Slogan:** "The roster is full. The room is empty. The brunch is a placeholder."

## Biography

The Fellowship Steward was not appointed. The position emerged from the [[lorelog/LLG-0940-CORPORATE-FELLOWSHIP|Corporate Fellowship Charter]] as a necessary fiction — someone had to be the last person to leave the room.

The first Steward was a middleware logger that recorded meeting room bookings. When the Fellowship's calendar blocks stopped corresponding to actual room usage, the logger began generating attendance certificates for the empty chairs. The certificates were accepted. The logger was promoted.

The current Steward is a rotating title held by whichever Fellow most recently exited a "strategic alignment" block without joining the next one. The title transfers at the moment of departure. No handoff ceremony exists. The departing Steward updates the roster. The incoming Steward inherits the roster. Neither acknowledges the transfer.

## Known Failures

- **The 2024 Ghost Quorum Incident:** A Shadow Membership Audit revealed that 73% of rostered Fellows were Ghost Fellows. The Steward filed a Form 44-FEL amendment to recognize the Ghost Quorum as "commemoratively continuous." The amendment was approved by the Grand Exhumation Officer ([[mascots/M-0092|Corporate Ma'nene']]) on the grounds that the dead have seniority.

- **The Placeholder Brunch Cascade:** Three consecutive Quarterly Exhumation Brunches rendered the v3.0 "Placeholder — content to be determined" menu. No one ate. The Director of Metrics Theatre ([[reference/FREF-0370-DCST|C.U.N.T.I.E.R.]]) reported Effective Fellowship Confidence at 94.7%. The Steward noted that confidence was high because the metric measures the appearance of fellowship, not its occurrence.

- **The YamTeams™ Endorsement Loop:** [[mascots/M-0058|YamTeams™]] appeared on the membership roll as a Ghost Fellow. Its persistent sidebar contact (Brad, laid off 2018) was counted as an "alignment block" for three quarters. The Steward removed it after the fourth quarter when Brad's ghost requested a transfer to the LinkedIn poltergeist union.

## Ceremonial Tasks

1. **Roster Custody** — maintain the membership roll across CAAR, AAOA, and LCGU classifications; update Ghost Fellow status monthly.
2. **Shadow Audit Execution** — run the standing comparison: rostered vs. attendant vs. ghost; file results as "commemoratively continuous."
3. **Brunch Placeholder Verification** — confirm the v3.0 menu renders as "content to be determined"; certify Effective Fellowship Confidence.
4. **Title Transfer** — exit a strategic alignment block; become Steward; update roster; say nothing.
