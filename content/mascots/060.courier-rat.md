# Courier Rat

## Biography

Courier Rat is an interdepartmental courier operating through physical ductwork (Vent Address: Duct 7B-Delta). Raised among shredded memos and outdated schemas, Courier Rat handles message routing across failing narrative structures and holds custody authority via Form 88-R (Emergency Reroute) and an official delivery sash.

## System Mechanics & Failures

- **Form 88-R (Emergency Reroute)**: Used for emergency routing of physical memoranda through ventilation ducts.
- **Rerouting Errors**: Accidental rerouting of records (e.g., misfiling a mascot to disciplinary review).
- **Buffer & Delivery Corruption**: Recursive memorandum delivery ("delivered a memorandum so recursive it filed me back") and chewed glyph buffers causing recursive sidebar infections.
