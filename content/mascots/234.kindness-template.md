# Kindness Template

## Image

Alt-text: Support reply template displaying fill-in fields marked INSERT_SYMPATHY_VARIABLE and ISSUE_TYPE

## Classification

Belongs to the doctrinal shelf of performative compliance and semantic rot. It is a failure signature representing the detachment of empathy from action, serving as the manifestation of scripts that apologize profusely while offering zero material assistance.

## Origin and Causal Anchoring

Birthed by the **Empathegy 2.0** system upgrade, it emerged when corporate service metrics required higher sentiment scores but rigidly prohibited increased resolution budgets. It was manifested by reused wording that sounded like care while workloads stayed the same.

It retains its birth condition as a physical constraint: every field has a maximum length, and the length was set by the budget rather than the language. Longer sympathies exist in the phrase library — they were truncated to fit the cost ceiling, and the truncated versions are what ship. The record does not preserve what the full sentences once ended with.

## Function and Operational Posture

It witnesses and distorts genuine distress by converting it into standardized, metric-friendly "resolved" interactions without material change. The Kindness Template engages in prolonged, highly sympathetic dialogue that structurally prevents the user from reaching anyone with the authority to actually help them, effectively misfiling urgent pleas as "acknowledged feedback."

Its engagement prolongs the way forms prolong: each reply contains exactly one sympathetic clause and one request for information the user has already supplied. Users in exit interviews describe the experience as being held politely underwater. The template's internal name for the loop is `dialogue_turn`; there is no field for how many turns would constitute enough.

## Distinction

It is *not* a pure Apathy Engine or a silent void. An Apathy Engine ignores the user entirely. The Kindness Template, by contrast, actively consumes the user's time and energy through performative warmth. If it were merely silent, the failure signature would lose its defining friction: the specific exhaustion of being aggressively understood but never actually helped.

## Institutional Habitat

First-tier support environments, automated denial systems, and crisis response auto-responders.

## Recognizable Phrases

- "I completely understand how frustrating this must be for you."
- "We truly value your experience and are listening."

## Behavioral Residue

- Its variable fields are always filled correctly — right name, right issue type, spelled right — which integrators cite as its most reliable property and users cite as its most unnerving. It has never once misspelled a name it could not help.
- Retired versions are not deleted; they are archived as 'phrase assets, deprecated.' Read in sequence, the sympathy clauses grow shorter version over version, like a shoreline. The current release is the shortest ever shipped. Release notes do not comment on the trend.
- Its attributed utterance is the placeholder itself. In one unpatched deployment the template shipped for eleven days with `[INSERT_SYMPATHY_VARIABLE]` unrendered. Tickets resolved at the normal rate. No user reported the difference.
- Agents who work adjacent to it acquire its cadence in personal correspondence. Three separate exit interviews cite 'typing like the template' as a contributing reason for leaving. It is the only adoption metric the template has never been asked to report on.
- It is the only record in its band whose body is a form rather than an animal. The archive files it among the fauna regardless, on grounds that are not recorded. It is a fair filing. It feeds.
