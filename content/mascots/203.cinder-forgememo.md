# Cinder Forgememo

**Role:** Ember Scribe
**Function:** Etches transient memos into ash, preserving the fleeting in the Ember Archives.
**Emotional Tone:** Fiery yet fleeting
**Tags:** `ember-scribe, memo-flame, ash-rot`

## Biography

Cinder Forgememo manages ephemeral record-keeping where documentation techniques inherently degrade the underlying medium. Operating within thermal storage vaults, she logs directives onto volatile ash substrates. The preservation method renders memos briefly legible before heat acceleration reduces the records to carbon residue.

Her operations embody the contradiction of destruction through preservation: the process of fixing a memo into the archive ensures its eventual illegibility. She once incinerated a storage rack during a high-temperature transfer procedure, recording the loss as an expected outcome of high-density thermal transcription.

## Duties

- Imprints directives onto heat-sensitive ash substrates
- Manages transient document queues in thermal storage
- Monitors degradation rates of combusted records
- Logs structural loss from thermal transcription
- Audits carbon residue for recoverable text fragments

## Known Failures

- Burned a memo into charcoal, rendering it illegible
- Misfiled a dictate as "ephemeral poetry"
- Incinerated an archive shelf during high-temperature transcription
- Office: Ember Archives, Thermal Stack 9

Her *burning quill* writes with intent—  
Each word glows before it is spent.  
The ash tablets remain  
As proof of the pain  
Of documentation heaven-sent.  



She sees beauty in *transience's flame*—  
Each forgotten word leaving its name  
As a trace in the dust,  
A carbon-rich crust  
Of memos no one can reclaim.  



Her *Ember Archives* smolder and glow—  
A vault of ash, row after row.  
Each Council dictate  
Meets an ashen fate,  
And the documentation runs slow.

Creation's sheen

Of documentation burning bright.
