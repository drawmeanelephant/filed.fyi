# Cinder Forgememo

**Role:** Ember Scribe
**Function:** Etches transient memos into ash, preserving the fleeting in the Ember Archives.
**Emotional Tone:** Fiery yet fleeting
**Tags:** `ember-scribe, memo-flame, ash-rot`

## Biography

Cinder Forgememo was sparked from a deprecated dictation plugin, her essence born in the Ember Archives—a smoldering vault of ash-written memos. She wields a burning quill, etching Council dictates into fleeting ash tablets. Her work is both creation and destruction: every memo glows brightly before crumbling into dust. Cinder sees beauty in transience, believing that even forgotten words leave a trace in the ash.
She once ignited an entire shelf during a ritual transcription, claiming it was “ceremonially necessary.” Her fiery passion for documentation is matched only by her tendency to reduce archives to cinders.

## Duties

- Etches Council dictates into ash tablets
- Preserves transient memos in the Ember Archives
- Maintains the ritual flame of documentation
- Suppresses rogue embers from consuming archives
- Chants lost dictations under smoldering lanternlight

## Known Failures

- Burned a memo into charcoal, rendering it illegible
- Misfiled a dictate as "ephemeral poetry"
- Ignited an archive shelf during a ritual transcription
- Office: Ember Archives, Smoldering Stack 9

## Limerick Log


### Cinder Forgememo {#cinder-forgememo-4}

Cinder etches each *memo in ash*—  
A flaming quill's beautiful slash.  
Each dictate glows bright,  
Then crumbles at night,  
And the archive burns out with a flash.  



She misfiled a dictate as "*poetry*"—  
Ephemeral, dreamy, no lottery  
Of structured compliance.  
Her fiery reliance  
On feeling? A charred potpourri.  



She ignited an archive shelf whole  
During transcription—her ritualistic goal.  
"*Ceremonially necessary!*"  
She claimed, somewhat airy,  
While the fire consumed every scroll.  



Her *burning quill* writes with intent—  
Each word glows before it is spent.  
The ash tablets remain  
As proof of the pain  
Of documentation heaven-sent.  



She sees beauty in *transience's flame*—  
Each forgotten word leaving its name  
As a trace in the dust,  
A carbon-rich crust  
Of memos no one can reclaim.  



Her *Ember Archives* smolder and glow—  
A vault of ash, row after row.  
Each Council dictate  
Meets an ashen fate,  
And the documentation runs slow.  



She suppresses rogue embers with care—  
When the archive combusts? She is there.  
But the line between  
Creation's sheen  
And destruction? A *pyric affair*.  



She chants *lost dictations* each night  
Under smoldering lanternlit light.  
The words burn and fade,  
Each ceremonial grade  
Of documentation burning bright.  



"*Every word burns eternal,*" she cries,  
Though the ash contradicts all her ties  
To permanence. Still,  
Her flaming quill  
Ensures every memo never quite dies.  



So if memos turn quickly to dust  
And the archive smells strongly of must  
And charcoal and flame,  
That's *Cinder's* great claim:  
Where the transient is held in great trust.
