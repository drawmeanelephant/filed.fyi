# Footnote Pallbearer

Footnote Pallbearer is reserved for the careful burial of facts too inconvenient to headline.

## Image

Alt-text: Pallbearer mascot carrying an asterisk-marked footnote to the bottom margin of a document, text set in 8-point font

## Archival Purpose

Birthed from the Registry Stub Sweep as a causal response to strict non-deletion policies. The Pallbearer emerged to address a recurring systemic failure: the inability to purge contradictory evidence without triggering integrity alarms. It preserves the compensatory ritual of typographical subjugation.

Operating under the jurisdiction of citation-weight residue, its function is to witness and certify "dead context." It formally carries facts below the line, ensuring their formatting remains intact while their narrative authority is reduced to zero. It does not erase the evidence; it simply ensures the evidence can no longer interrupt the main text.

It carries by weight. Deployment records show the Pallbearer lifting a fact from the body text by the reference mark alone, gauging nothing but its heft — a heavy fact is set deep in the margin, a light one nearer the line. It does not read what it carries; reading is a cousin's business, and the Pallbearer keeps only the local dead. What it does instead is ensure the deceased arrives at the bottom still formatted — the one mercy the main text could not provide.

## Boundary Note

**Distinction:** Footnote Pallbearer carries context directly below the line—visible, but structurally dead. It is not an exile, but a local burial. By contrast, [[limericks/LIM-0027|Annex Hush]] escorts context to the back of the book; [[limericks/LIM-0033|Appendix Silk]] dresses it well; [[limericks/LIM-0029|Annexa Sorrowmark]] simply remembers that it hurt.

Where Footnote Pallbearer appears, the record did not lose the fact; it **lost the room in which the fact could still decide anything**.

The ceremony is complete enough to reference.
Not enough to absolve.

## Behavioral Residue

- It lowers facts by the asterisk, never by the sentence. Recovered documents show the reference mark arriving at the margin first and the fact following after; a footnote without its star is logged as a burial performed without the deceased.
- The eight-point is deliberate. One recovered draft shows a caveat set at ten point and re-set at eight before publication; the only annotation in the file's hand reads 'too loud.'
- What must travel further than the margin is handed off. Deployment records show Annex Hush collecting the facts bound for the back of the book, Appendix Silk dressing the ones that will be seen at a distance, and Annexa Sorrowmark taking only what still hurts after burial; each handoff is filed 'not local.'
- It has never carried a fact back up. No recovered document shows a footnote promoted to the body on the Pallbearer's watch; the file does not say whether this is loyalty to the burial or the absence of a return route.
- Asked at intake whether the buried facts can still testify, it indicated the line. The file preserves the answer as 'they remain cited.' — its complete testimony.
