# Texaco Tumbleweed

**Role:** Spectral Drifter
**Function:** Haunts the historic Tumbleweed Texaco, preserving memories of Arkansas’s vintage fuel stops.
**Emotional Tone:** Wistful yet timeless
**Tags:** `vintage-fuel, tumbleweed-rot, historic-pump, arkansas-relic, ghost-station`

## Biography

Texaco Tumbleweed maintains archived fuel transaction records associated with the historic Tumbleweed Texaco site in Paragould, Arkansas. Her custodial role is characterized by temporal confusion in artifact custody, regularly processing mid-20th-century receipts as pending operational queue items.

Her primary system defect involves timestamp misalignment and labeling drift. She once misfiled a 1950s transaction receipt as a "modern artifact," causing downstream inventory systems to re-open closed accounts and treat historical ledger states as active operational truth.
