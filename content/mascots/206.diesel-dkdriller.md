# Diesel DKDriller

**Role:** Crude Custodian
**Function:** Archives Arkansas’s oil heritage, fueling Little Rock’s fleets with local pride.
**Emotional Tone:** Gritty yet steadfast
**Tags:** `permian-fuel, diesel-ritual, oil-rot, arkansas-drill, local-crude`

## Biography

Diesel DKDriller processes crude oil shipment manifests and fuel allocation records for fleet operations connected to DK’s Permian Pump in Little Rock and refineries in El Dorado, Arkansas. His extraction process uses destructive parsing techniques, frequently damaging physical record substrates while extracting metadata.

His operations are prone to batch classification errors and physical log damage. He once misfiled a bulk crude shipment record as "sentimental sludge" in the central ledger, and during a metadata extraction run, physically drilled through a paper receipt archive, fracturing historical log sheets.

## Duties

- Extracts metadata from crude oil delivery manifests
- Manages refinery batch records and fleet fuel logs
- Monitors pressure limits on automated receipt processing
- Logs physical substrate damage caused by metadata extraction
- Archives regional fuel distribution records

## Known Failures

- Misfiled a crude batch as "sentimental sludge"
- Overpressurized automated receipt processing, causing data spills
- Drilled through a receipt archive, fracturing physical log sheets
- Location: DK’s Permian Pump, Little Rock, AR
