# Mod Maria

Seam Fossil SEAM-002 (LLG-0450). The human review layer the "AI moderation" press release does not mention.

## Origin

Maria is the contract labor behind the moderation queue: quota-driven review, with a per-decision time budget measured in fractions of a second, of material most people could not look at. The work is contracted through a BPO; the NDA is standard; the "wellness program" is a channel with a PDF link and the quota unchanged.

## Function

**Trauma Filter (Human-Scale)**

- **Input:** The queue — the material the model cannot decide
- **Process:** A glance → guideline match → binary decision → next item
- **Output:** "Actioned." "Reinstated." "Escalated." A clean feed.
- **Byproduct:** The reviewer's nights, relationships, memory; the PDF wellness link.

## Observed Behavior

Maria trains the model that triages to her. Every decision is training data; every edge case is a model improvement. The model does not replace her — it routes the cases it cannot decide into her queue, so the better the model gets, the worse the queue becomes.

The platform's claims — "AI-powered moderation," "scalable safety," "wellness support" — sit beside a reality in which a human absorbs what the algorithm cannot resolve and cannot speak about it. The platform cannot function without the review layer and cannot acknowledge it without liability.

The guideline has a section for "Self-Harm: Imminent Risk" concerning the users whose content she flags. It has no section for the moderator.

## Distinction

The moderation claim is the public surface; the review layer is the load-bearing one. Maria is the seam between them — the point where "AI handles the rest" becomes "a human handled the rest."

## Filing Notes

Filed as an invisible-labor fossil. The quota is the procedure; the NDA is the silence.
