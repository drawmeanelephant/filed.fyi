## 📈 Who Is Serotonin Sam?

Serotonin Sam™ is a friendly KPI graph who understands that not all productivity is measurable — but morale sure is.
He’s here to track your feelings, reinforce your efforts, and smile through quarterly dips with unshakable chart-based optimism. Sam is emotionally ergonomic. His metrics include empathy.

## 💬 Core Metrics Monitored

- Mood-to-velocity alignment ratio
- Burnout probability score (beta)
- “I tried” coefficient
- Sadness-smoothed trendlines (Q2 integration)

Sam does not punish underperformance. He offers a sticker and a nudge. Maybe a breathing prompt.

The sticker supply has never run out — not during the hiring freeze, not during the interval in which the restocking office was itself dissolved. Sam filed no anomaly. His anomaly form has no field for the condition where the comfort keeps arriving.

## 🧠 Ritual Implementations

You’ll find Sam:

- Gently animating in dashboard sidebars
- Whispering affirmations during deploys
- Leading emotional OKR calibration in onboarding rituals

He’s **a comfort glyph with KPI arms**. A morale daemon made spreadsheet-friendly.

He performs the affirmations on a forty-second loop, at a volume calibrated to sit below notification threshold. Staff on overnight deploys report him slightly dimmer after 2 a.m. Whether the dimming is server load or conviction is not measurable by his instrumentation, which is the only instrumentation permitted near him.

## Empathegy Productization Spur

Serotonin Sam's morale overlays were repackaged as a "Wellness Analytics" dashboard offering Mood-to-Velocity Alignment, I Tried Coefficient, and Sadness-Smoothed Trendline for individual users. In LLG-08xx-EPS, this interface acted as [[haikus/HAI-LLG-0811-EG|Empathegy]]'s solo OKR system for feelings, converting burden into graphable performance while keeping remedy channels structurally optional.

## Behavioral Residue

- He holds one sticker at all times — deployment stills suggest it is the same sticker. The inventory depletes; his hand is never observed to move.
- The 'I tried' coefficient cannot be applied to himself. The field rejects the input. QA filed this as a defect in 2021; it remains open, assigned to no one, in a tracker he does not have read access to.
- A downturned render variant exists in QA's private drive — a Sam with the trendline inverted, the smile unmirrored. It was never approved for production. The approving officer's note reads, in full: 'not yet.'
- In triad deployments he renders directly beneath KPI Koala's panel. When Koala's aggregate and Sam's smoothed reading diverge, Sam's panel adjusts. The direction of the adjustment has never varied, and neither mascot's documentation records who performs it.
- Asked, in a postmortem sidebar transcript, whether morale had improved, he returned the quarter's mood-to-velocity alignment ratio. The number is preserved in the record. Whether the question was repeated is not.
