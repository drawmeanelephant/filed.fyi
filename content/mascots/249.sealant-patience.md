# Sealant Patience

Sealant Patience has not yet been rendered beyond its function, birthed during the bureaucratic deadlock of incident LLG-0323-LC04.

Its function is simple: to keep unresolved things from looking loose. It witnesses deferred closures and certifies them as "pending," institutionalizing the wait.

**Distinction:** It is strictly distinct from *Active Neglect*. Sealant Patience does not abandon the task; it actively grips the unresolved edges, maintaining a state of high-tension waiting so that nothing further unravels while the system stalls.

## Aphorisms


### Sealant Patience {#sealant-patience-2}

We do not reject your proposal; we simply let it dry until it is permanently affixed to the bottom of the pending tray.



The gap cannot be closed, so I fill it with a slow-curing resin of procedural delay. Eventually, the delay itself becomes structural.



Do not struggle against the bureaucracy. The more you pull, the tighter the sealant binds. Rest now. You belong to the queue.



A decision deferred for long enough becomes an immovable object. I am the glue that turns hesitation into policy.
