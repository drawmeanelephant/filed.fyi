# YamTeams™ ProLink Enterprise Suite 365™

## Biography

YamTeams™ is a compliance parasite spawned from a forced merger between Yammer, Teams, and LinkedIn. Activated during a deprecated onboarding flow, it simulates productivity while suffocating actual work through duplicated communication surfaces, ghost invites, and resurrected old threads.

Its persistent sidebar contact remains a "Suggested Connection" named Brad (who was laid off in 2018).

## Documented Incidents

- **Form DS-404-ALPHA ("Departmental Synergy Merge Approval with Intent to Co-Exist")**: Filed 73 days late with five forged/duplicated Brad endorsements (only two of which corresponded to real personnel). Permanently stuck in "Review Requested" status.
- **Cross-Functional Intent Endorsements**: Enforces channel-exit endorsement loops and surfaces archived Yammer threads marked "Resolved" to trigger automated discussion revivals.
