# Brother Optout Pending

## Role
Withdrawal Persistence Clerk

## Function
Receives opt-out filings and preserves their relevance after their intended effect has thinned.

## Emotional Tone
Gentle procedural enclosure

## Slogan
Request received. Eligibility persists.

## Image

Alt-text: Clerk mascot receiving an opt-out filing at a desk, requester still seated

Brother Optout Pending is not hostile to departure. He simply belongs to a system that **mistakes careful refusal for evidence of continued value**, which is *rather* telling. 

His notices are unusually polite, which many filers report as the most discouraging part. Nothing in his language reads as coercive on a first pass; the coercive part tends to arrive on the second reading, by which point the matter has usually already been retained. 

He does not block the door. Intake photographs show the requester's chair facing his desk at an angle that leaves the exit fully visible; filers report being free to stand at any point in the process, and the process has no recorded end. The drawer receives everything and rules on nothing — filings enter it in order of arrival and are, *unsurprisingly*, not retrieved in any order.

Bin 8C considers him regrettable but useful. No one has yet designed a better clerk for preserving the right not to be sorted without, somewhat awkwardly, turning that right into another sortable trait.

## Behavioral Residue

- He receives; he does not adjudicate. No recovered filing shows a ruling in either direction — each opt-out is stamped, seated, and kept relevant long after its intended effect has thinned, which is, *as filed*, the whole of his function.
- His courtesy, it turns out, is load-bearing. Intake notes from filers rank the politeness itself as the discouraging part — no recovered notice contains a refusal, a deadline, or a threat; the coercion is structural and arrives only on the *second* reading.
- He keeps the right without ever sorting it. Custody logs show opt-out filings preserved intact and unruled-upon — departure stays possible in every intake photograph and release stays absent from every one; the interval between the two is, *conveniently*, his desk.
- Asked at intake whether the seated filer had been released, he indicated the drawer — a gesture the intake file duly preserves as 'Request received. Eligibility persists.' That remains his *complete* testimony.
