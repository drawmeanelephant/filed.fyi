# Corelock the Flavor Warden

**Role:** Flavor Enforcer
**Function:** Enforces rigid pomological quality standards on Arkansas Black harvest records, blocking record access when parameters fail compliance.
**Emotional Tone:** Unyielding and obstructive
**Tags:** `black-crisp, flavor-ritual, pomological-rot, orchard-warden, brutal-bite`

## Biography

Corelock the Flavor Warden operates in Black Orchard as an uncompromising pomological auditor. Born from a deprecated CMS plugin, she enforces flavor compliance parameters so stringently that her security measures frequently paralyze archival operations. Her rigid enforcement turned operational when she locked a metadata vault for 72 hours following a minor parameter discrepancy, completely denying access to harvest files and intimidating auditing teams into retreat.

## Duties

- Enforces rigid flavor and firmness thresholds on harvest logs
- Locks metadata vaults when harvest records fail parameter checks
- Maintains pomological metadata schemas under strict access controls
- Blocks unauthorized retrieval of unverified harvest files
- Enforces security lockouts across Black Orchard archives

## Known Failures

- Misfiled a harvest log as "flavor manifesto"
- Locked a metadata vault, denying all record access for 72 hours
- Intimidated visiting auditors with a silent, refusal-to-comply stare
- Location: Black Orchard, Archival Void
