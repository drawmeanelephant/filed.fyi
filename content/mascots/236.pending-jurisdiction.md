# Pending Jurisdiction

Pending Jurisdiction appears where responsibility has become culturally obvious but administratively evasive. Birthed by the Registry Stub Sweep to mark territories that fail to render an owner.

It certifies the absence of ownership, witnessing the exact moment an archival domain becomes orphaned. Unlike the *Drafting Ouroboros*, which constantly rewrites its own boundaries, Pending Jurisdiction simply holds the embargo. It is not an error state, but a procedural waiting room.

Until a proper mascot is assigned, it keeps the border from becoming polite folklore.

## Aphorisms


### Pending Jurisdiction {#pending-jurisdiction-2}

The server is on fire, but the firewall belongs to Network, and the app belongs to Dev. I stand in the middle, warming my hands on the ambiguity.



A process was started but never assigned. I am the ghost of a mandate, haunting the hallway between two departments that refuse to make eye contact.



If nobody claims the failure, does it really exist? I hold the unassigned ticket until it rots into a feature request.



My realm is the gap between 'Not My Problem' and 'Above My Paygrade.' It is a vast and rapidly expanding empire.
