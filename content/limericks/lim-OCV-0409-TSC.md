# Trust Surface Explanation Layer Admitted as Teaching Specimen


The explanation is neat,  
And renders the filing complete.  
The loss of the base,  
Was moved from this space,  
To make the submission replete.  



The document is legally sound,  
With none of the horror we found.  
The facts are aligned,  
The screaming refined,  
And safely secured in the ground.  



The weather report is attached,  
With all the barometers matched.  
The sentences glow,  
With seasonal snow,  
And climate compliance is hatched.  



The document looks pretty calm,  
It acts like an archive's balm.  
We checked the constraint,  
There is no complaint,  
Just pass me the stamp in your palm.  



The specimen speaks of the calm,  
A whispered and ancient psalm.  
The friction was shed,  
When the old ledger bled,  
And offered its surface as balm.  



The margin was altered by one,  
And left the department undone.  
A crisis of text,  
Left management vexed,  
And halted the setting sun.  



The explanation was read,  
And placed in the file as was said.  
The facts are preserved,  
As previously served,  
The document rests on the bed.  



The contradiction remains,  
In all the subordinate chains.  
The summary sheet,  
Is safe and discrete,  
And hides the administrative pains.
