# AV-14 Nullseal Register


The **Nullseal** will alter the text,  
So **executives** aren't perplexed.  
What once was a **fail**,  
Is a **minor detail**,  
And no one is bothered or vexed.  



They **laundered** the language of doom,  
To brighten the **boardroom's gloom**.  
With a **stamp** and a smirk,  
They ignored all the work,  
And swept the debris with a broom.  



A **failure** was clearly observed,  
But the **vocabulary boys** were unnerved.  
They stamped it as "**fine**,"  
Dipped their **balls in the wine**,  
And left all the **users unserved**.  



The **register** covers the cracks,  
By changing the **absolute facts**.  
When the system is **fucked**,  
And the **auditors cucked**,  
They just **paper it over** with wax.  



The **words** that they use are a blur,  
A comforting, **meaningless purr**.  
The **seal** doesn't fix,  
It's just one of their tricks,  
To act like the errors **defer**.  



The document entered the room,  
Describing an imminent doom.  
The seal was applied,  
The language was dried,  
And no one remembers the gloom.  



The findings were terribly raw,  
Revealing a terrible flaw.  
We stamped it with green,  
To keep it unseen,  
Because it's the corporate law.  



We harmonized all of the text,  
So nobody will be perplexed.  
The error is gone,  
The system moves on,  
And everything runs un-annexed.  



A negative word in the file!  
It threatens the corporate style!  
We must stamp it out,  
And silence the doubt,  
Or executives might lose their smile!  



The audit described a complete,  
And total systemic defeat.  
The Nullseal was pressed,  
The language was dressed,  
And now it's a tropical treat.  



They say that the seal of the desk,  
Transforms the absurd and grotesque.  
You stamp on a fire,  
The flames just retire,  
And leave a continuous text.  



The negative word was removed,  
And all of the phrasing improved.  
The document closed,  
The problem dozed,  
And nothing was ever reproved.
