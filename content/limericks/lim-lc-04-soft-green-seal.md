# LC-04 Soft Green Seal


A seal with a soft shade of green,  
To keep the discrepancies clean.  
It covers the gaps  
With a series of taps,  
So failures remain unforeseen.  



The absence is managed with care,  
By making it seem like it's there.  
LC-04's glow  
Puts on quite a show,  
To soften the archival air.  



The service was missing a beat,  
But the green makes the record complete.  
It isn't quite true,  
But the comforting hue  
Makes the missing data look neat.  





The soft green seal was a sham  
The data was caught in a jam  
We sealed up the text  
And wondered what’s next  
And printed a useless exam  



A seal made of meaningless wax  
Is hiding all of the cracks  
The server is broke  
The manual a joke  
We pay the procedural tax  



We stamped it with delicate green  
It never arrived on the screen  
The human is flawed  
The logic outlawed  
The vacuum is perfectly clean  



The seal is incredibly soft  
We threw the old server aloft  
The error was vast  
The future is past  
The humans continuously coughed  



The soft green seal is a lie  
The network continues to die  
We route all the pain  
Right down the big drain  
And nobody ever asks why
