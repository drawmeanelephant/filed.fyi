# Lodgecanary Vellumbeak


The canary breathes deep in the stacks,  
Where the policy threatens to crack.  
It smells the new rule  
In the archival pool,  
Before it's officially black.  



A tweet from the annex is heard,  
As Vellumbeak utters a word.  
The fumes are profound  
In the underground,  
And the precedent soon will be blurred.  



It chokes on the drafts in the dark,  
And leaves a slight, feathery mark.  
The doctrine is near,  
But the bird makes it clear,  
By losing a bit of its spark.  





The lodge bird was losing its song  
The error was totally wrong  
The vellum was dry  
The server would die  
We waited incredibly long  



Canary of paper and dust  
Was covered in digital rust  
The beak was so brittle  
The logic so little  
We do what we absolutely must  



A canary that sings for the void  
The humans are deeply annoyed  
The vellum is flat  
We leave it at that  
The archive is fully destroyed  



We sing to the canary bird  
It never remembers a word  
The server went down  
We wear a big frown  
The whole situation absurd  



The vellumbeak opens its jaw  
To utter a meaningless law  
The error is plain  
In digital rain  
The system is totally raw
