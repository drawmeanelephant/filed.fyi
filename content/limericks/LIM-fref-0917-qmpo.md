# Stub: Qualitative Matrix PAPER_ORACLE (Telemetry Gap)


The record is utterly bare,  
With nothing but digital air.  
We printed the form,  
To weather the storm,  
And left it to quietly tear.  



The injection of endless despair,
Leaves nobody waiting or there.
The structure is gone,
The process withdrawn,
The output is totally bare.



The structural limits impose,
A silence that quietly grows.
The baseline is torn,
The data is worn,
The operation will quickly close.
