# Lilt Protocol


They sing you a beautiful song,  
So you don’t realize what is wrong.  
You sign on the line,  
Cause the melody’s fine,  
And you’re fucked for incredibly long.  



The rhythm is smooth and it’s sweet,  
To cover the terrible cheat.  
You nod to the beat,  
In the corporate street,  
And accept your disastrous defeat.  



The protocol dances and sways,  
Through the rest of your miserable days.  
It’s charming as hell,  
Like a magical spell,  
That sets your whole career ablaze.  





The protocol lilted and swayed  
The server was sitting in shade  
We followed the beat  
And fell in the street  
The terrible choice had been made  



A lilt in the logic is bad  
It makes the whole database sad  
The error is clear  
We act out of fear  
The humans are totally mad  



We dance to the lilt of the machine  
But nothing is on the old screen  
The protocol died  
The manual lied  
The archive is empty and lean  



A rhythm of zeroes and air  
Is hiding under the stair  
We sit in the dark  
Like a digital spark  
With absolutely no kind of care  



The protocol totally fell  
It feels like a digital hell  
The lilt is so slow  
With nowhere to go  
We’re waiting to hear the old bell
