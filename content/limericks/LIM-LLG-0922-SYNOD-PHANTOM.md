# Limericks: Synodal Scribe

**Core counterpart:** [[mascots/M-0087|Synodal Scribe]]

The Scribe wrote “unanimous” down  
While four bishops were out of town.  
“The Spirit,” he said,  
“Has attended instead,”  
And conciliarity crowned.
