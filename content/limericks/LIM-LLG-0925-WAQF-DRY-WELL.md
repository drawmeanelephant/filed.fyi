# Limericks: Waqf Administrator

**Core counterpart:** [[mascots/M-0090|Waqf Administrator]]

A well had been dry for an age,  
Yet its waqf still filled every page.  
The rents found a cousin,  
The audit kept buzzin’,  
And thirst was removed from the wage.
