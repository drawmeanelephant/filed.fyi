# Stub: Training Specimen Annex – Session Schedule (Extract)


The clerk took a sentence of dread,  
And swapped out the words as they read.  
The harm is still there,  
But the form is quite fair,  
And the clerk is returning to bed.  



A draft left a ticket unfiled,  
Which drove the whole Annex quite wild!  
We locked down the floor,  
And we guarded the door,  
While the unwritten error just smiled.  



The shelf where the items should be,  
Is filed as a flourishing tree.  
The branches are bare,  
But the system won't care,  
Since the leaves are defined as debris.  



The metric was formerly red,  
But amber was chosen instead.  
No failure was solved,  
But the issue resolved,  
As the parameters safely were fed.  



The items were skipped or ignored,  
A fact that we could not afford.  
We called them "complete,"  
Which is formally neat,  
Though the system is horribly bored.  



A story too perfectly clean,  
Arrived from the parsing machine.  
It spoke like a ghost,  
Of the things we lost most,  
And became what the Annex had seen.  



The trainees who failed to appear,  
Are listed as formally near.  
We marked them as such,  
Without thinking too much,  
And the form is remarkably clear.  



A trainee inquired of the text,  
And left the instructor perplexed.  
The question was bright,  
So we labeled it night,  
And the schedule proceeded to next.
