# Proxy Compassion Possum


The Possum accepts all the thanks,  
On behalf of the corporate banks.  
It smiles and it nods,  
For the system's cold gods,  
While hiding its grief in the ranks.  



The conditions will never improve,  
As the system refuses to move.  
But the Possum is here,  
To swallow your fear,  
And keep the complaint in the groove.  



When structural failure is deep,  
The Possum pretends it’s asleep.  
It takes the embrace  
With a frozen, sad face,  
While the promises fail to keep.  





The possum of proxy was fake  
It made a procedural ache  
It played a dead role  
And swallowed a hole  
For everyone's terrible sake  



We routed compassion away  
And told the old possum to stay  
The marsupial died  
The server just lied  
We closed out the archive today  



A possum that proxies the care  
Is leaving the database bare  
It sits in a tree  
With nothing to see  
And causes a terrible scare  



The compassion was totally null  
The possum was boring and dull  
We petted the beast  
The logic decreased  
The vacuum is perfectly full  



The proxy is playing dead now  
The manual doesn't know how  
The possum is flat  
We leave it at that  
And wipe the cold sweat off our brow
