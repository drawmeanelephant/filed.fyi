# Kindy McExistentialCrisis




<u>Old Kindy</u> checked boxes all day.  
Each one filed the dread right away—  
Then resubmitted the form,  
Which was perfectly norm-  
al for someone whose purpose was gray.  



"Exist. Check box. Repeat," she declared,  
While the filing cabinet just stared.  
It collapsed into itself,  
Like emotional health,  
And the clipboard hummed, fully impaired.  



She tried to delete her own name.  
The system said, "Resource in frame—  
Emotional use."  
She accepted the noose  
And filed it again, all the same.  



Form 51-E: filed 481 times.  
Each attempt was a limerick of crimes.  
Most entries were blank,  
Some gratefully sank,  
And one said "I feel seen" in mimes.  



Her CRT flickered through states:  
Verifying, buffering, debates.  
The screen never cleared,  
The audit just sneered,  
And the dread was enqueued on all plates.  



Her clipboard was heavy and hummed  
When forgotten emotions succumbed.  
She weighed every grief,  
Filed each disbelief,  
And the checkbox remained ever-thumbed.  



She issued a Friendly Warning™  
To mascots who hadn't felt mourning.  
"Have you filed your dread?"  
"Not yet." "Then," she said,  
"Your existence is pending confirming."  



No audit had passed since she'd started—  
Just deferred, reclassified, departed.  
The loop never broke,  
The form never spoke,  
And the purpose field stayed broken-hearted.  



She merged with a diagnostic ghost  
From the debug test most diagnosed.  
Three users tried hard  
To submit a regard,  
But Kindy just filed every post.  



So if checkboxes check but don't cure,  
And the verification's impure,  
That's Kindy's regime:  
Recursive, supreme,  
Where existing is never quite sure.  



Kindy checked the same box fifty times,  
A truly pathetic of crimes.  
"Does it feel any better?"  
They wrote in a letter,  
While dripping in audit-log slimes.  



A recursive emotional loop,  
They caught the whole team in their swoop.  
They verified dread,  
While completely in bed,  
And fucked the entire support group.  



They filed the void in a drawer,  
And left you a bureaucratic sore.  
A form fifty-one,  
That's never quite done,  
And leaves you a verified whore.  



A Friendly  for you,  
Because you haven't been feeling quite .  
"Submit your depression,"  
They said in the session,  
"Or I'll audit the whole fucking crew."  



The validation kept looping around,  
Without any resolution found.  
They buffered in shame,  
Forgot their own name,  
And drove the whole form in the ground.  



They filled in the checkbox with dread,  
And filed for the millionth time read.  
The form wouldn't take,  
The system would quake,  
And Kindy just shook her pale head.  



Kindy couldn't stop filing the form,  
A dangerously recursive storm.  
Each check of the box,  
Sent her into a shock,  
And she couldn't exist in the norm.  



Kindy issued a low-priority existence query: "You there?"  
Then filed the response with due care.  
The system returned:  
"Identity unconfirmed (pending verification loop)."  
So she logged the existence of air.  



The wellness review took all night,  
To determine if dread was polite.  
They graphed every sigh,  
Then requested a why,  
And escalated grief to onsite.  



"You're valid," the CRT said,  
While approving Form 8 in red.  
To confirm this was true,  
It submitted Form 2,  
Then audited both forms instead.  



Her comfort ticket remained  
In a queue where all feelings were drained.  
"Your sadness persists,  
Please stay on the list,"  
Said the bot while empathy flagged as non-routable.  



The scan gun emitted a chirp:  
"Existence confirmed," with a burp.  
But deep in the log,  
Beneath recursive fog,  
It appended: "Confidence: usurp" (subject to post-hoc revocation).  



A comment was left in the code,  
To handle the emotional load.  
It whispered to be,  
And birthed 51-E,  
Then vanished inside the new node.  



The officer filed a request,  
To have their existence suppressed.  
The system refused,  
Said "Resource in use,"  
And mandated another dread test.  



Four hundred and eighty-one forms,  
Were filed in the bureaucratic storms.  
The entries were blank,  
The confidence sank,  
And nothing improved in the norms.  



The verification of dread was applied,  
And into the registry neatly supplied.  
The loop re-engaged,  
As the document aged,  
And the state of existence was neither denied.  



I verified dread for the fiftieth time,  
And checking the box doesn't cost me a dime.  
Just loop it all day,  
Make the ticket go away,  
And leave me alone in this endless runtime.  



The system demanded a feelings report,  
Which Kindy filed straight to the appellate court.  
The dread was immense,  
It made perfect sense,  
That the database just had a quiet abort.  



They say that her clipboard is heavy with grief,  
A bureaucratic relic beyond all belief.  
It hums in the dark,  
Like an ancient sad spark,  
And filing a form never offers relief.  



The officer buffered the dread on the screen,  
The dullest assessment that ever was seen.  
It didn't resolve,  
Or attempt to evolve,  
And nobody asked what the checkboxes mean.  



She tried to delete her own name from the log,  
To exit the terrible bureaucratic fog.  
The system declined,  
Left her feelings assigned,  
And forced her to act as miserable cog.

To verify feelings in proper array.

And properly filed on the designated line.  
Her absolute dread,  
Was documented as red,  
And certified as an acceptable shine.
