# FFP-0380 Care Without Exit, Silence Without Gap

## Archival Records


The Dead Service Layer resigned,  
Which left the new forms unaligned.  
Though the host has deceased,  
The demand has increased,  
So the absence was promptly assigned.  



The Coverage Ledger was proud,  
To tally the strain of the crowd.  
With the metrics in place,  
It acknowledged the case,  
And uploaded the grief to the cloud.  



The Board met to witness the strain,  
And formally measured the pain.  
They noted the stress,  
In a thorough address,  
And sent it right back to the lane.
