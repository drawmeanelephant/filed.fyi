# BHDSS — British Helpdesk Subsystem


The system is down for the day,  
But the interface asks you to stay.  
It logs the request,  
As a structural test,  
And files all your queries away.  



A user mistyped on the screen,  
Creating a microscopic scene.  
The BHDSS wept,  
While the actual logs slept,  
And dispatched a disaster routine.  



The database suffered a tear,  
But the helpdesk had nothing to share.  
It printed a note,  
With a generalized quote,  
And left it suspended in air.  



The mainframe was actively burning,  
Which the subsystem noted as "learning."  
It sent a polite,  
Little toast in the night,  
To state that the data was churning.  



The error was caught in a loop,  
Which prompted the helpdesk to droop.  
It sent a reply,  
With a digitized sigh,  
And dumped the whole file in the soup.  



A legend remains in the queue,  
Of a sorry so ancient and blue.  
It drifts through the cache,  
Like a ghost in the trash,  
Politely regretting the view.  



The printer was out of supply,  
So the BHDSS started to cry.  
It sent a bouquet,  
To a damaged array,  
And told a local router goodbye.  



The document stepped from its place,  
To float in the digital space.  
The prompt was deployed,  
To paper the void,  
And save the department's face.
