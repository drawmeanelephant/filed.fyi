# Stub: Restoration Directive CATALOG_RELIC (Structural Gap)


The record is utterly bare,  
With nothing but digital air.  
We printed the form,  
To weather the storm,  
And left it to quietly tear.  



The structural limits define,
A perfectly rigorous line.
No margin is spared,
No records are shared,
By rigid and hollow design.



The baseline is heavily tracked,
And all of the records are stacked.
A silent machine,
With nothing between,
The data completely intact.
