# Limericks: CLER Seam

**Core counterpart:** [[reference/FREF-0920-RAB|Religious Administrative Bureaucracy]]  
**Seam registry:** [[mascots/M-0090|Religious Administrative Seams]]  

A cleric was filed from start,  
With each status neatly in part.  
When the person had changed,  
The record remained,  
And the form kept the larger heart.
