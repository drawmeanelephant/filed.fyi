# Limericks: Auxiliary Board Secretary

**Core counterpart:** [[mascots/M-0090|Auxiliary Board Secretary]]

The Board met to consult at four,  
With the answer slipped under the door.  
Four voted one way,  
Three managed “nay,”  
And unanimity entered the score.
