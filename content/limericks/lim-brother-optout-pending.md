# Brother Optout Pending


You clicked on the button to leave,  
But the system refused to believe.  
The Brother says "Wait,  
At the sanctuary gate,  
While I trap you and force you to grieve."  



Your opt-out is logged in the file,  
But it sits there and gathers the bile.  
You cannot break free,  
From the terrible tree,  
Because leaving is not in our style.  



They treat your departure as fake,  
And keep you around for their sake.  
The status is stuck,  
And you’re out of your luck,  
With a record you simply can’t break.  





The brother requested to leave  
But nobody chose to believe  
The optout is stuck  
He ran out of luck  
We quietly started to grieve  



A pending request to be gone  
Was resting right out on the lawn  
The server is slow  
With nowhere to go  
We wait for the digital dawn  



We opt out of brotherly love  
And throw out the digital dove  
The pending is long  
The logic is wrong  
We push with a terrible shove  



The brother is waiting to quit  
The manual doesn't quite fit  
He stands by the door  
And looks at the floor  
The system just doesn't permit  



The optout is perfectly null  
The system is boring and dull  
We stamp it approved  
The data removed  
The vacuum is perfectly full
