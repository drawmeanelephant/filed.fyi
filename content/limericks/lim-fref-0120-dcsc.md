# Success Class Consolidation Card


The classes of pending success,  
Are filed in a standardized press.  
The card will combine,  
The metrics in line,  
To limit the system's excess.  



The card is consolidating wins,  
By sorting the corporate sins.  
We merge all the glee,  
In column part C,  
And throw out the rest in the bins.  



The consolidation of old,  
Was written in silver and gold.  
The success was a myth,  
Or so said the smith,  
Before all the servers grew cold.  



The classes are merged on the sheet,  
I really don't care if they meet.  
I staple it twice,  
And say it looks nice,  
Then rest my procedurally feet.  



The card is completed in blue,  
The consolidation is through.  
The classes are bound,  
Without any sound,  
The clerk is returning to queue.
