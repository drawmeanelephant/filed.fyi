# Limericks: Devasthanam Officer

**Core counterpart:** [[mascots/M-0090|Devasthanam Officer]]

The gold in the vault gleamed serene,  
Though the auditors never got clean.  
A ritual alarm  
Kept them far from the harm,  
And the inventory stayed unseen.
