# Mod Maria

A moderator named Maria, so brave,  
Absorbs all the horror the platform gave.  
The AI gets trained,  
On the trauma sustained,  
While wellness is a PDF wave.  

The queue never empties at night,  
The guidelines are rewritten in fright.  
She flags what she's seen,  
The platform stays clean,  
And her memory dims out of sight.
