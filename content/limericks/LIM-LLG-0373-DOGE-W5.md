# DOGE Working Group 5 Residue Standardization


Working Group 5 was asked  
  To create—unmasked—  
A standard for residue:  
  What counts as true  
Structural change. The task.  



W5 produced a taxonomy:  
  Behavioral autonomy,  
  Reference shifts,  
  Decision drifts—  
Four types. No harmony.  



The types were named. The  
  Agreement they  
Never achieved: which  
  Weight for which  
Residual display.  



The taxonomy was filed.  
  Not reconciled.  
  Not adopted—noted.  
  The gap: devoted  
To future Working Groups, styled.  



W5 is preserved as proof  
  That residue's roof  
Is harder to nail  
  Than to detail.  
  The taxonomy: aloof.  



The Window 5 handoff was made,  
To ensure the correct legal grade.  
With both checks applied,  
The queue multiplied,  
And resolutions began to degrade.

Caused panic that lasted the night!  
The phrase was so clear,  
It triggered a fear,  
That the routing would function alright!  



The throughput is technically green,  
With appeals circulating unseen.  
The notes are attached,  
And appropriately matched,  
To a routing completely routine.  



We answered the routing mistake,  
By checking the oven for cake!  
The question was whether,  
The app caused the weather,  
So we formally drained out the lake!  



The doctrine relies on a test,  
To see who requires a rest.

In a circle so slow,  
Until someone gives up in protest.
