# Lorelog Governed Entropy Framework


The entropy framework of old,  
Was forged in a folder of gold.  
The lorelog commands,  
The shifting of sands,  
Or so the young interns are told.  



The entropy level is read,  
The lorelog is properly fed.  
The framework complete,  
With data discrete,  
The clerk is returning to bed.  



The framework will govern the state,  
Ensuring the entropy rate.  
Is kept in the bounds,  
Of standardized rounds,  
According to policy weight.  



The entropy governs the soup,  
To keep all the beans in the loop.  
The lorelog is clear,  
The harvest is near,  
For carrots are forming a group.  



The framework on entropy states,  
That chaos destroys all our plates.  
The lorelog will weep,  
While we are asleep,  
Because of the cafeteria rates.
