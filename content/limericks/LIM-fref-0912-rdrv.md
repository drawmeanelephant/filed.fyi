# Stub: Restoration Directive RECORD_VEIL (Structural Gap)


The record is utterly bare,  
With nothing but digital air.  
We printed the form,  
To weather the storm,  
And left it to quietly tear.  



The form was submitted with haste,
But signatures slowly erased.
The protocol stalls,
Within empty halls,
And audits are quietly placed.



A record was held in the queue,
To wait for a signature new.
The timeline expired,
The admins retired,
And nothing was left to review.
