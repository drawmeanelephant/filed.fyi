# Seam Survey: The Dystopian Present

The dystopia wasn't ahead —  
It arrived while we slept in our bed.  
No neon, no chrome,  
Just forms brought from home,  
And the curve-coherence the dashboard read.  

A form that errs at field twelve,  
Was all the future's stealth.  
No siren, no storm,  
Just software deform,  
And nobody left to help.  

The survey was filed, not found,  
Ten fractures beneath the compound.  
The mask slips, the breath  
Of the machine underneath  
Is the only thing solid on the ground.
