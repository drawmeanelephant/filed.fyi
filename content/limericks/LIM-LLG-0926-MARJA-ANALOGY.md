# Limericks: Marja' Office Deputy

**Core counterpart:** [[mascots/M-0090|Marja' Office Deputy]]

A deputy ruled on a stake  
By the dates that old traders would make.  
The chain was presumed,  
The stamp was perfumed,  
And the ledger absorbed the mistake.
