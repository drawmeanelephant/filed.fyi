# Limericks: Cargo Cult Clerk

**Core counterpart:** [[mascots/M-0090|Cargo Cult Clerk]]

The Clerk stamped “CLEARED FOR LANDING”  
On a form whose war was long ending.  
The runway stayed hot,  
The cargo came not,  
But the cabinet kept on expanding.
