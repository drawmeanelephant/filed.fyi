# Hygiene Cycle 7‑B: Brighten the Spine


The templates were stripped from the spine,  
To ensure that the metrics align.  
With usage so low,  
We altered the flow,  
And classified absence as fine.  



The dashboards were formally cleared,  
As thirty-two entries disappeared.  
Though kept in the file,  
It sits in a pile,  
To hide what the management feared.  



The deprecated series is dead,  
So we polished the printer instead.  
With successors implied,  
The toner has dried,  
And the absence is properly fed.  



The practical usage is same,  
There is no department to blame.  
We updated the sheet,  
The cycle's complete.  
Please reference the file by its name.  



The cycle of seven is vast,  
A shadow the management cast.  
Through hygiene and rite,  
The absence took flight,  
And neglect is a doctrine at last.  



The C.U.N.T.I.E.R. metrics were set,  
To manage a critical threat.  
A fractional drop,  
Caused systems to stop,  
And the form isn't authorized yet.  



The absence is no longer tracked,  
According to section and pact.  
The status will pend,  
And does not need to end,  
As noted in summary fact.  



The user submitted a form,  
Which failed the procedural norm.  
The spine was detached,  
The records rematched,  
To weather the metrics' new storm.
