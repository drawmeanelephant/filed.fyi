# Method Not Allowed — Rest Request Verb Rejection


The use of the syllable 'stop',  
Caused the workflow committee to drop.  
We called in the guard,  
To audit the yard,  
Lest the continuity metrics should flop.  



The forms for the stoppage were filled,  
The 'stop' was immediately killed.  
We set it to run,  
Though nothing was done,  
And the ongoing process was billed.  



The elders requested a pause,  
But 'stop' wasn't in the old laws.  
The ledger was cast,  
To honor the past,  
And perform is the only true cause.  



A structural limit was breached,
And protocol boundaries overreached.
The system went dark,
With a nullified mark,
And the records were quietly bleached.



We filed the compliance report,
With signatures ordered and short.
The server was bare,
No data was there,
A standard procedural resort.
