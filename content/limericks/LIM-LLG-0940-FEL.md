# Corporate Fellowship Charter

A Fellowship chartered on Form Forty-Four,  
Where executives don't meet — they confer.  
The roster is fat,  
But the room? Imagine that —  
The brunch is a placeholder, nothing more.  



Three directives are stacked in a pile:  
SOMA approves it as rest for a while.  
COMA objects;  
C.U.N.T.I.E.R. checks  
And teaches the metric to smile.  



The Steward is whoever just left.  
No handoff. No ceremony. No theft.  
The roster updates,  
The ghost count abates —  
Sixty-seven percent of the bereft.  



The brunch menu renders v3.0:  
"Content to be determined," you know.  
No one eats a bite,  
But the metrics are bright:  
Confidence ninety-four point oh.  



Membership? Recognized, not applied.  
The certificate? Generated, not tried.  
Retroactive grace,  
Calendar overlap's trace —  
The archive calls governance, certified.  



YamTeams joined as a Ghost.  
Brad's sidebar — the matter engrossed.  
Four quarters aligned,  
Then the poltergeist signed  
A transfer. The roster embossed.
