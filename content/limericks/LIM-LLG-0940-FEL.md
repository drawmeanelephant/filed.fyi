# Corporate Fellowship Charter

A Fellowship chartered on Form Forty-Four,  
Convened only in calendar lore.  
The roster is fat,  
But the room? Imagine that —  
The brunch is a placeholder, nothing more.  



SOMA approved it as rest.  
COMA objected, distressed.  
C.U.N.T.I.E.R. found  
The confidence sound,  
And the dashboard did what it does best.  



The Steward is whoever just left.  
No handoff. No ceremony. No theft.  
The roll updates,  
The ghost count inflates —  
Sixty-seven percent of the bereft.  



The brunch menu's current v3.0:  
"Content to be determined" — although  
No one eats a bite,  
The metrics are bright:  
Confidence ninety-four point oh.  



No candidate needs to apply.  
The certificate comes from on high.  
Retroactive grace,  
Calendar overlap's trace —  
The roster is born certified.
