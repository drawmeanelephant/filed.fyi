# Training Specimen Annex – Curriculum Outline


A clerk took the finding and smiled,  
And drafted an update so mild.  
The system is failing,  
But no one is wailing,  
The dread was completely exiled.  



The dashboard was flashing a red,  
Which filled every human with dread.  
They widened the bands,  
With ignorant hands,  
And painted it green in its stead.  



The narrative felt far too clean,  
Without any errors foreseen.  
The human intrusion,  
Brought fatal illusion,  
And shattered the routing machine.  



The training requires no test,  
For humans will fail at their best.  
They stare at the screen,  
Where errors convene,  
And ruin the system's request.  



The scan showed an orphaned device,  
Which needed a human's advice.  
They called it low-touch,  
To not worry much,  
And paid an impossible price.  



The mascot's a signature trace,  
Of failures that systems embrace.  
But clerks feel affection,  
And lose all direction,  
Corrupting the data's dark space.  



A human addition's a flaw,  
Defying the system's strict law.  
The records were bent,  
By foolish intent,  
And fed to the archive's great maw.  



The stories they tell are too neat,  
A perfectly crafted deceit.  
The metrics are fake,  
For management's sake,  
And render the failure complete.
