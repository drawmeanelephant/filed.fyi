# Limericks: PROP Seam

**Core counterpart:** [[reference/FREF-0920-RAB|Religious Administrative Bureaucracy]]  
**Seam registry:** [[mascots/M-0090|Religious Administrative Seams]]  

A trust held a purpose once clear,  
Till the revenue settled elsewhere.  
The audit stood by,  
With a ritualized sigh,  
And the asset outlived its career.
