# Jiggler Jimmy

A dongle named Jimmy, fifteen bucks,  
Keeps status dots green — zero flukes.  
The human can rot,  
The dashboard cares not,  
Just sine waves that nobody spukes.  

Jimmy jiggles the cursor at night,  
Keeps the presence curve polished and bright.  
The human's away,  
The dashboard says "stay,"  
And the green dot persists out of spite.
