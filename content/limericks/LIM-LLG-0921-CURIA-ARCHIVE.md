# Limericks: Curial Archivist

**Core counterpart:** [[mascots/M-0086|Curial Archivist]]

The Archivist stamped with grave care  
On a text that was never quite there.  
“Nothing obstructs,”  
Said the seal as it rusts,  
“Which is why the absence is fair.”
