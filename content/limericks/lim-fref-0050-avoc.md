# Assurance Vocabulary Desk – Optics Coordination Notice

And properly strictly compiled.  
The words are agreed,  
To cover the need,  
And keep the vocabulary mild.  



The desk of assurance coordinates,  
The words and the grammar and dates.  
We stare at the screen,  
Like a mindless machine,  
And dream of the cafeteria plates.  



The notice is processed in full,  
Without any procedural pull.  
The grammar is neat,  
The desk is complete,  
The process is properly dull.  



The desk has a very long word,  
That honestly seems quite absurd.  
I stamp it with red,  
And go back to bed,  
And act like it hasn't occurred.  



A syllable drifted a bit!
