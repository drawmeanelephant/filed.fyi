# Limericks: Bureaucratic Capture Vector

**Core counterpart:** [[mascots/M-0091|Bureaucratic Capture Vector]]

No mascot, only a strain,  
That rides on a chartered chain.  
From boardroom to shrine,  
The forms realign,  
And capture arrives with a name.
