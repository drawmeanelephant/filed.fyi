# Limericks: Title Society Registrar

**Core counterpart:** [[mascots/M-0090|Title Society Registrar]]

A title was bought with a fee,  
Beside one received properly.  
The seal hit them both,  
With bureaucratic oath,  
And the ledger declared unity.
