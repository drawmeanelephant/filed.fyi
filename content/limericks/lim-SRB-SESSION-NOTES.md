# Stub: Scan Reinterpretation Session – Shelf Line 3B


The seven remain in deferred,  
A status we strongly preferred.  
The presence is nil,  
But billable still,  
As noted in memo the third.  



Twelve records lack owners or names,  
Which violates standard-use frames.  
We marked them as free,  
By local decree,  
To minimize subsequent claims.  



Four identifiers wait in a line,  
For data to make them align.  
They point to a space,  
That lacks any face,  
Which means that the system is fine.  



The backlog was gathering dust,  
The filing was taken on trust.  
We wrote "stable phase,"  
To get through the days,  
And simply did what we must.  



Twelve orphans were found in the row,  
A catastrophic procedural blow!  
We summoned the guard,  
And double-locked hard,  
Because they had nowhere to go.  



The absent are waiting their turn,  
A fact that the ledgers must learn.  
The "Seven" remain,  
A spiritual stain,  
That makes the machinery burn.  



The engine will run in the fall,  
According to protocol’s call.  
We printed the text,  
To see what comes next.  
The numbers did nothing at all.  



We labeled the absence "curated,"  
To show that the risk was abated.  
The shelf line is bare,  
But functioning there,  
Exactly as we had dictated.
