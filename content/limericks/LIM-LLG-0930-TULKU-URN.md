# Limericks: Tulku Recognition Clerk

**Core counterpart:** [[mascots/M-0090|Tulku Recognition Clerk]]

The urn had a gold-plated shine,  
But its answer was set in the line.  
Two names were unclean,  
One name was foreseen,  
And the lottery worked out just fine.
