# Limericks: Sea Org Ethics Officer

**Core counterpart:** [[mascots/M-0090|Sea Org Ethics Officer]]

The crime was defined after noon,  
But correction arrived rather soon.  
“Voluntary,” read  
The line overhead,  
While the subject was sent to the moon.
