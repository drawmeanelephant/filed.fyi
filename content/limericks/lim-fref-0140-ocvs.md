# Over‑Coherence Screening Checklist


The screening for logic is tight,  
To check if the structure is right.  
If things are too clear,  
The checklist is here,  
To govern the procedural light.  



The checklist ensures we are sane,  
Which causes a lot of our pain.  
The logic flows well,  
Which feels like a spell,  
And clearly means we have no brain.  



This sentence makes perfectly sense!  
The logic is far too intense!  
Deploy the blank ink,  
Before we can think,  
And build up a cognitive fence!  



The checklist of old was so wise,  
It blinded the archivist's eyes.  
Too coherent a thought,  
Was instantly caught,  
And sent to the digital skies.  



The screening for pastries is strict,  
The over-coherence is kicked.  
The donuts are round,  
And firmly unbound,  
By logic that cannot conflict.
