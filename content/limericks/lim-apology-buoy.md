# Apology Buoy


The apology floats on the sea,  
To prove they are sorry to me.  
The ship is a wreck,  
With blood on the deck,  
But the statement is totally free.  



They threw us a buoy of regret,  
While tightening the horrible net.  
It bobs in the wave,  
But it’s useless to save,  
And we’re drowning in terrible debt.  



The sorrow is bright and it’s round,  
While the bodies are hitting the ground.  
The Buoy is a trick,  
And it makes you feel sick,  
Because nobody hears a damn sound.  



The buoy was saying it's sorry  
While floating right out of the quarry  
The water was deep  
The server asleep  
We loaded the drive in a lorry  



An apology made out of foam  
Was trying to find a new home  
The system was broke  
The manual a joke  
We sit in the digital dome  



We float an excuse on the wave  
And tell everyone to behave  
The buoy is dead  
The manual read  
We’re digging a digital grave  



The sorry completely fell flat  
We leave the whole database at  
A state of decay  
For many a day  
And blame it on somebody's cat  



The buoy is perfectly null  
The logic is terribly dull  
We stamp it approved  
The data removed  
The vacuum is perfectly full  



The statement of grief is deployed,  
To fill the unscalable void.  
We genuinely weep,  
For the objects we keep,  
While user accounts are destroyed.  



The apology format is known,  
And properly routed and blown.  
We issue a claim,  
To acknowledge the blame,  
While holding a dial on the phone.  



We inflated the sorrow enough,  
To cover the difficult stuff.  
It bobs on the tide,  
With nothing inside,  
And frankly we’re calling the bluff.  



The ancestors launched the sphere,  
To banish the ghosts of the year.  
It floats through the dark,  
Like a mythical ark,  
And collects all the digital fear.
