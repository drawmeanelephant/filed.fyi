# Assurance Coordination Notice, Trust Surface Narrative Alignment


The dashboard displays the new band,  
Exactly as optics had planned.  
The absent supply,  
We now classify,  
As governed and firmly in hand.  



The loss is a managed delay,  
The metrics are perfectly okay.  
The surface of trust,  
Is covered in dust,  
But optics demand it this way.  



The sandwiches kept in the hall,  
Are framed as a system-wide fall.  
The confidence chart,  
Is doing its part,  
To answer the catering call.  



The words were aligned as you said,  
I marked the whole surface as read.  
The trust is secure,  
Or something obscure,  
I'm going back into my bed.  



The dashboard of calm has awoke,  
It whispers of signals in smoke.  
The optics we steer,  
Have learned how to fear,  
And wear an invisible cloak.  



A comma was placed on the left,  
We treated the act as a theft.  
The optics team cried,  
And locked us inside,  
The trust surface now is bereft.  



The wording was placed in a file,  
And kept in the regular style.  
The dashboard is green,  
The surface is clean,  
It sat on the desk for a while.  



The trust in the optical band,  
Was shipped to a faraway land.  
The summary sheet,  
Was left in the street,  
Exactly as forestry planned.
