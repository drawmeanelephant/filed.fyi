# Limericks: Presbyterian Clerk of Session

**Core counterpart:** [[mascots/M-0088|Presbyterian Clerk of Session]]

The Clerk had a vote split in two,  
With six saying no, seven true.  
He wrote with a grin,  
“Consensus came in,”  
And the minute book published the view.
