# Limericks: Beth Din Registrar

**Core counterpart:** [[mascots/M-0090|Beth Din Registrar]]

The Registrar filed every get,  
With a fee and a witness well met.  
The form said “consent,”  
Though coercion was sent,  
And the page stayed impeccably set.
