# Dashboard Alignment Session – Extracted Notes


The notes are extracted and read,  
To ensure the dashboard is fed.  
Alignment is key,  
For column part B,  
Before the whole session is dead.  



The dashboard alignment is grand,  
We carefully sift through the sand.  
The session was long,  
The widgets were wrong,  
But no one would dare raise a hand.  



The notes on aligning the car,  
Are placed in a dashboard of tar.  
The steering is tight,  
The widgets are bright,  
We drove it completely too far.  



The notes have a spelling mistake!  
The dashboard is starting to shake!  
Call down the brigade,  
And cancel the trade,  
Before the whole system will break!  



The notes from the session are bland,  
I don't really quite understand.  
I check off a box,  
And stare at the clocks,  
And hope I get paid as I planned.
