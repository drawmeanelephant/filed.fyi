# Assurance Vocabulary Desk – Style Guide


The style of the desk is exact,  
To keep the vocabulary packed.  
The guide is clear,  
From year to year,  
And standardizes every act.  



The style guide insists we are plain,  
Which heavily taxes the brain.  
We type out the text,  
And quickly get vexed,  
Because we are standing in rain.  



The style guide is sitting right there,  
But honestly I do not care.  
I use the wrong font,  
Whenever I want,  
And slump in the clerical chair.  



An italic was used on the line!  
The style guide refuses to sign!  
Shut down the whole floor,  
And weld up the door,  
The text is incredibly fine!  



The guide is applied to the word,  
And nothing improper is heard.  
The grammar is neat,  
The desk is complete,  
The process is quietly blurred.
