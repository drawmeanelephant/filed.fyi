# Explanation Layer Residue Audit


The summary layer is clean,  
The clearest that ever was seen.  
We smoothed out the terror,  
Distributed error,  
And nobody knows what we mean.  



The annex contains the real threat,  
A danger we freely forget.  
We passed it above,  
With bureaucratic love,  
And cited the void with regret.  



The problem was sliced very thin,  
And filed in a separate bin.  
With parts everywhere,  
There’s nothing to scare,  
But nobody knows where we’ve been.  



The managers smiled at the text,  
Unsure of what action came next.  
The data was bright,  
The formatting right,  
But the system is heavily hexed.
