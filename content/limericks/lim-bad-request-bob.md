# Bad Request Bob

## Archival Records


A boolean toggle was left unsupplied,  
Which Bob, without checking, just marked as "implied."  
He logged the blank space,  
As a valid test case,  
And watched as the intake queue quietly died.  



The input was fractured and missing a date,  
But checking the context would just make him late.  
He stamped it as fine,  
And sent it down line,  
Because fixing the errors is not on his plate.  



The porter is carrying weight,
Through a cold and unyielding state.
The conflicts remain,
A dull, heavy chain,
While sidecars are sealed at the gate.



A fugue in the system of walls,
Where none of the proxy recalls.
The silence is vast,
Obscuring the past,
As dust in the corridor falls.



The boolean toggle was blank,
Which Bob just allowed out of rank.
The intake was dead,
With nothing ahead,
And into the system it sank.
