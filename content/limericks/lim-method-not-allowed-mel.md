# Method Not Allowed Mel


A fugue in the corridor space,
Where nothing is leaving a trace.
The methods are barred,
And heavily scarred,
Erasing the lines of the face.



The ledger conceals all the cost,
As sidecars are severed and lost.
The conflict remains,
In empty domains,
And every old boundary crossed.



The action you stated is clear,  
But the method is not allowed here.  
We cannot "remove,"  
As we only "approve,"  
So your document stays for a year.  



A user attempted to "stop" the routine,  
A crisis the office has rarely foreseen.  
We launched the alert,  
For the verb could assert  
A collapse of the entire machine.  



The request used the wrong kind of verb,  
Which I frankly refuse to disturb.  
I hit the reject,  
With minimal respect,  
And threw the whole file at the curb.
