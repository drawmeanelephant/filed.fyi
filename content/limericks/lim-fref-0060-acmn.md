# Assurance Coordination Session – Extracted Minutes


The minutes extracted are logged,  
Before the whole system gets bogged.  
Coordination is set,  
By policies met,  
And standard procedures are cogged.  



The minutes are quite hard to read,  
And offer no procedural speed.  
I stamp it as done,  
By quarter to one,  
And frankly ignore the main need.  



The session of old was so great,  
It altered the timeline of fate.  
The minutes remain,  
To govern the rain,  
And open the mystical gate.  



The session coordinated bread,  
To make sure the toaster was fed.  
The minutes confirm,  
The crust of the worm,  
And everything else that was said.  



A second was lost in the clock!  
The minutes are facing a shock!  
Send everyone home,  
And write a new tome,  
And change out the principal lock!
