# Witness Custody Registry — Known Routing Gaps


The witness approached the front desk,  
With claims of a system grotesque.  
We noted the date,  
And sealed up their fate,  
In routing we deemed picturesque.  



The form was received by the board,  
And quietly, cleanly ignored.  
We lack the right tools,  
According to rules,  
The data is properly stored.  



A testimony entered the queue,  
With actions we cannot pursue.  
The layer below,  
Has nowhere to go,  
So filing is all we can do.  



The SCL intake is full,  
But nobody here has the pull.  
We catalog pain,  
Again and again,  
To render the narrative dull.  



We log every gap that we find,  
When systems are strictly aligned.  
We cannot assist,  
But you're on the list,  
Forever sequentially signed.
