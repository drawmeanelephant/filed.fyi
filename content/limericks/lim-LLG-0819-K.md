# Incident LORE-0819-K // Null-State Resonance in Compliance Badging


The hashes returned were all null,  
Which rapidly rendered it full.  
The prompt flashed the green,  
Across the machine,  
And the storage was rendered quite dull.  



The lock gave a comforting gleam,  
While the storage went down the ravine.  
They saved nothing twice,  
At an excellent price,  
And declared it a synergized dream.  



The metrics are soaring extremely,  
Our compliance is looking supremely.  
Though the data is dead,  
The UI is red,  
And the user engagement is seemly.  



The uptime continues to slide,
While the server refuses to hide.
The mandate is clear,
The failure is near,
As the system and rules have collided.




The resonance echoes inside,
Where confidence started to slide.
The hashes return,
With nothing to learn,
And quiet is largely applied.
