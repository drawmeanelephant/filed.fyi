# Obsolescence Steward


A sidecar is hauling the frame,
A conflict that carries no name.
The heavy redaction,
Demands no reaction,
The corridor echoes the same.



The proxy is shifting the weight,
A draft in a nebulous state.
An endless omission,
A blind exposition,
The silence will swallow the gate.



The boundary severs the line,
A protocol hollow and fine.
The records are fading,
The system degrading,
We wait for a singular sign.



A conflict is locked in the core,
A phantom that paces the floor.
The sidecar is hollow,
With nothing to follow,
We measure the empty locked door.



The proxy will sever the thread,
A draft that is silent and dead.
A hollow abstraction,
A loss of all traction,
The corridor fills up with dread.



A proxy abandons its post,
To leave an invisible ghost.
The sidecar deflects,
While silence reflects,
The conflicts we tolerate most.



The draft has forgotten its name,
Obscured by the structural frame.
With borders erased,
And records misplaced,
The omission is always the same.



A sidecar arrives with the load,
A conflict it drags down the road.
It offers no cure,
Just shadows obscure,
Within a redacted new mode.



The boundary blurs in the haze,
A quiet, procedural maze.
The heavy black ink,
Begins now to sink,
Through layers of endless delays.



The corridor leads to the void,
Where unwritten drafts are deployed.
With no resolution,
Or clear execution,
The files are securely destroyed.





The temporary struts were retained,  
As continuity rules had explained.  
The structure is old,  
But does what it's told,  
And funding is strictly maintained.  



A hinge on the obsolete door,  
Demanded a tactical war.  
We audited grease,  
To mandate the peace,  
And guarded the maintenance floor.  



The scaffold was built for a day,  
But none of us took it away.  
It drafted a form,  
To weather the storm,  
And issued a mandate to stay.
