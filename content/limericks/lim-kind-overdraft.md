# Kind Overdraft


He promised to care for the team,  
But the budget was just a pipe dream.  
The Overdraft hits,  
And he loses his wits,  
While releasing a terrible scream.  



The kindness was issued on credit,  
And now he is starting to dread it.  
He’s totally broke,  
In a corporate joke,  
And wishes he never had said it.  



The bank of compassion is bare,  
And there isn’t a drop of it there.  
The Overdraft tries,  
But it’s built upon lies,  
And nobody actually cares.  





The overdraft took all the care  
And left the old database bare  
We spent all the grace  
In absolute space  
With absolutely no kind of flair  



We overdrafted the heart  
The system was falling apart  
The error was loud  
We step in the cloud  
And wait for the process to start  



A negative balance of kind  
The humans are totally blind  
We owe a big debt  
We deeply regret  
And left the old server behind  



The overdraft causes a chill  
We pay the procedural bill  
The kindness is spent  
Without any intent  
The error is making us ill  



The balance is perfectly null  
The system is boring and dull  
We overdraft nil  
And swallow the pill  
The vacuum is perfectly full
