# Limericks: SUCC Seam

**Core counterpart:** [[reference/FREF-0920-RAB|Religious Administrative Bureaucracy]]  
**Seam registry:** [[mascots/M-0090|Religious Administrative Seams]]  

A successor came by a draw,  
After gates had narrowed the law.  
The urn made a show,  
Of the name set to go,  
And continuity bowed in awe.
