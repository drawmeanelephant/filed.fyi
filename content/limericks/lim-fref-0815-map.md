# Managed Absence Spine


The absence was formally logged,  
Before the main server got bogged.  
The spine was aligned,  
The void was defined,  
And all human input was clogged.  



The structural absence collapsed,  
When too many seconds elapsed.  
The void got too tight,  
Turned off all the light,  
And management quietly gasped.  



The cabinet’s spine is removed,  
A medical status approved.  
The vertebrae shift,  
In dimensional drift,  
And human mistakes are reproved.  



The form for the absence is blank,  
A negligible drop in my rank.  
I stamped it "Unseen,"  
And locked up the screen,  
And filed the thin air in the bank.  



The Managed Spine hums in the deep,  
Where missing coordinates sleep.  
We polish the bone,  
Of absence unknown,  
And promise the silence to keep.  



A space in the spreadsheet appeared,  
Which all of the management feared.  
The lockdown deployed,  
To handle the void,  
Until the blank pixel was cleared.  



The absence was tracked in the tray,  
Where pending requests must delay.  
The status is set,  
The criteria met,  
The record is filed for the day.  



The structural spine is intact,  
Supported by absence of fact.  
The load-bearing void,  
Is fully deployed,  
To keep human variance tracked.
