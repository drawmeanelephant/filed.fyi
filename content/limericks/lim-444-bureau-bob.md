# Bureau Bob

Bureau Bob has a number for you,  
Though the system has no record too.  
The appeal portal's down,  
The chatbot's a clown,  
And the statute's as good as a shoe.  

The vendor chain wrote all the code,  
A bootcamp of newly bestowed.  
The bug has no owner,  
The fix has no donor,  
And the backlog is long as a road.
