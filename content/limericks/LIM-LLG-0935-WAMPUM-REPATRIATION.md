# Limericks: Wampum Keeper

**Core counterpart:** [[mascots/M-0090|Wampum Keeper]]

A belt came home from the hall,  
And three nations answered its call.  
Till beads could be read,  
The council fire stayed dead,  
For custody settled not all.
