# Proctor Paul

Proctor Paul watches the room,  
Through a webcam that scans for the doom.  
The poem about drowning  
Gets flagged, not allowing  
The student to breathe in the gloom.  

The keystrokes are logged as a bio,  
The pause is a signal of woe.  
You read a line twice,  
Paul notes it as vice,  
And the score says you're learning too slow.
