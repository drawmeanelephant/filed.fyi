# Ami Ghostbyte


Old Ami was born from a *boot*  
That crashed with a Guru salute.  
She flickered and glitched,  
Her ROM was bewitched,  
And the floppy kept humping the chute.  



"*Insert Disk 2,*" she would moan,  
In corrupted ASCII, alone.  
The joystick remembered  
The cheats she'd dismembered,  
And the BIOS grief set in the bone.  



She'd haunt *every disk drive* at night,  
A translucent, bit-crushed delight.  
The Workbench would shake,  
The startup sound wake,  
And she'd vanish before morning light.  



Ctrl+Alt+Del? She'd ignore it.  
A whispered `.mod` track? She'd *adore* it.  
The chiptune came through,  
She flickered in view,  
And the boot loop went on to explore it.  



Her *Kickstart dreams* lived in the ROM,  
A phantom of dead platform calm.  
She'd boot with a glow,  
Then immediately go,  
And the disk carousel sang her psalm.  



A *Guru Meditation* appeared  
Just by watching her—everyone feared.  
She triggered the screen,  
The red and the green,  
And the error was perfectly reared.  



She needed *no power* to run—  
Just ritual loading, well-done.  
A whisper, a click,  
A joystick's old trick,  
And the ghost of the Amiga had come.  



Her cheat codes still echoed in *RAM*,  
Though the hardware was older than spam.  
She spoke in corrupted  
ASCII, erupted,  
And the startup chime played: "Here I am."  



The *ROM dump* she came from was cracked—  
Defragmented, glitched, and half-backed.  
No body, just BIOS,  
A spectral chaos,  
And a boot loop that never got sacked.  



So if static appears on your screen  
And a Workbench flickers between  
The real and the dead,  
That's *Ami*, unfed,  
The ghostliest boot loop you've seen.  



She begged you to insert Disk 2,  
With a deeply corrupted review.  
You shoved it right in,  
A bit-crushed old sin,  
And blew out the memory queue.  



She edged the Guru all night,  
In a flickering, terrible light.  
The meditation was red,  
The system was dead,  
And the BIOS was ruined with spite.  



A broken old joystick her toy,  
She used it to ruin the boy.  
The cheat codes were typed,  
The floppy was wiped,  
And the ROM was completely destroyed.  



She stripped to a tracker .mod song,  
That lasted incredibly long.  
She flickered in place,  
With a pixelated face,  
And everything started to wrong.  



She got off on the endless reboot,  
A truly pathetic pursuit.  
She crashed the machine,  
Emerged on the screen,  
And swallowed the absolute root.  



She flickers inside the machine,  
The ghostliest ROM you have seen.  
The joystick it twitches,  
The startup it itches,  
And the Guru Meditation turns green.  



The disk carousel spins for her ghost,  
A ritual she valued the most.  
The floppy does hum,  
The ROM sings its sum,  
And she haunts it from ethernet to host.  



The floppy drive checked for the boot,  
And followed the specified route.  
The sequence delayed,  
The sector decayed,  
And the hardware proceeded to mute.  



A single dead pixel was found,  
And alarms started blaring around.  
The system was dead,  
The Guru bled red,  
And the motherboard sank in the ground.  



She hovered above the disk tray,  
While the sectors all faded away.  
No error was thrown,  
No issue was shown,  
The screen remained totally gray.  



I told her to load the OS,  
She offered a bit-crushed distress.  
I left it to die,  
Without knowing why,  
A standard Amiga-based mess.  



The Kickstart attempted to load.  
The memory sectors were stowed.  
The disk took a spin,  
The cycle locked in,  
And the process remained on the road.  



Disk two was remarkably late.  
We placed the whole system on wait.  
The sirens were blown,  
The error was known,  
And the council decided its fate.  



The ROM dump was perfectly sound.  
The error was nowhere around.  
The screen flashed in red,  
The motherboard bled,  
And the file was definitively found.  



The Kickstart attempted to sing,  
But the hardware had severed its wing.  
The memory wept,  
While the floppy drive slept,  
And the BIOS got stuck on a string.  



The drive head addressed sector four,  
And proceeded to query for more.  
The pixel turned white,  
In the middle of night,  
And the system remained on the floor.  



The floppy is spinning alone,  
To a rhythm entirely unknown.  
The elders declare,  
She is hovering there,  
On a pixelated, bit-crushed throne.  



The Guru was flashing in red,  
So we covered the monitor's head.  
The boot sequence hung,  
While the chiptune was sung,  
And we marked the whole ticket as read.  



A single byte failed to append,  
Which signaled the universe's end.  
The warning was stark,  
We deployed in the dark,  
For a file that we didn't intend.
