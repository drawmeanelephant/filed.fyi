## Continuity Renderings


The trust surface rendering screen,  
Has been certified safe and pristine.  
Though the basis is dead,  
We use caches instead,  
To ensure that the signal is green.  



The assurance was logged as complete,  
Though the data had suffered defeat.  
It’s a managed delay,  
So we looked the other way,  
And hit 'Save' on the continuity sheet.  



The ancient verification stream,  
Is an administrative pipe dream.  
Though the signal was lost,  
We ignored every cost,  
To protect the great dashboard regime.  




The surface is glowing and green,
But blocks out the signal between.
We checked on the sheet,
The loss is complete,
Yet nothing will show on the screen.



The dashboard regime holds the sway,
While caches are fading away.
The view was maintained,
And silence retained,
To honor the end of the day.
