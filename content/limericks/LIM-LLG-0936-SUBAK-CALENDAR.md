# Limericks: Subak Scribe

**Core counterpart:** [[mascots/M-0090|Subak Scribe]]

The Scribe had a calendar bright,  
Though the water denied it outright.  
He opened the gate,  
Too early, too late,  
And the ledger still balanced at night.
