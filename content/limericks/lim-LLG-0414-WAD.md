# Stub: Workaround Adoption Drift in Successor Environment


The forms that we made to bypass,  
Are printed on durable glass.  
The temporary route,  
Took a permanent root,  
And now it's required for the class.  



The manuals promised a cure,  
With interfaces clean and secure.  
But the old routing slip,  
Has a permanent grip,  
And the handling exceptions endure.  



We built a new desk for the floor,  
To bypass the queue at the door.  
But the shortcut we drew,  
Is the only path through,  
And the regular line is no more.  



The successor arrived with a gleam,  
To streamline the routing regime.  
But the habits of old,  
Were already enrolled,  
And the workaround governs the stream.  



The exception was meant for a day,  
A temporary filing display.  
But the clerks took it in,  
As the place to begin,  
And the workaround drifted to stay.
