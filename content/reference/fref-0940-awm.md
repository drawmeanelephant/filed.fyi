# Filing Amnesty Metrics

For five working days the Office suspended consequence: any late, misfiled, or regret-shaped submission could be surrendered at the window without penalty, remark, or eye contact. This table records the observed effect.

Measured at the window, before and during:

| Metric | Before Amnesty | During Amnesty Week |
|---|---|---|
| Outstanding filings, declared | 4 | 212 |
| Outstanding filings, actual | 212 | 212 |
| Drawer closure rate | 100% | 34% |
| Guilt per record | unmeasured; saturated | nominal |
| Confetti volume | reserve stock | deployed twice |
| Requests for a second amnesty | 0 | continuous |
| Backlog visibility | faith-based | tabular |
| Officer morale | composed | temporarily legible |

The table records what changed when consequence was briefly suspended. It does not claim the surrendered filings improved; it claims they surfaced, which is the only improvement the week was authorized to produce.

## Filing Rules

1. One row per metric the Office agreed to watch. Metrics that asked not to be watched were honored and are not shown.
2. `During` means the five working days of the amnesty plus the weekend, which the filings observed and the Office did not.
3. A metric unchanged by amnesty is still a metric. Stability is data.
4. The 212 surrendered filings are processed under ordinary rules; amnesty covered the shame, not the work.

## Living Document Protocol

- The table is republished each amnesty. Prior editions are retained; they read identically, which is itself a finding.
- Post-amnesty figures are not collected. The Office measures the suspension, not the return.
