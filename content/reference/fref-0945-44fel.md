# Form 44-FEL — Application for Recognition of Fellowship

**Record type:** Filing instrument, organizational recognition.

**Introduced:** Revision 1 no longer attested. Revision 3 current.

**Misfiled under:** Catering, Recurring Events.

**Managed-absence code:** LCGU.

Form 44-FEL is the instrument by which a fellowship is recognized. It is a single page. Completion requires approximately eleven minutes, of which ten are spent locating a room in which to sign it. The form creates the organization at the moment of filing. Nothing else is required of the organization, and nothing else has ever been observed.

**Finding.** Approval is not a decision. It is a duration.

## 2. Approval Process

Applications are reviewed by the Office of Fellowship Petitions. The Office was itself chartered by the first filing of this form. The instrument therefore predates its own review authority, a sequence Legal describes as established.

The Office has not convened with quorum since its chartering. Applications are approved when ninety days elapse without objection. Recognition is retroactive to the date of filing. The certificate is generated before the applicant is notified. Notification is not evidence of a meeting.

No application has been rejected. Rejection would require the Office to convene.

## 3. Instructions to Filers (Extract)

> **Field 1 — Name of proposed fellowship.** Enter any value. The value is binding.
>
> **Field 2 — Intended purpose.** Optional since revision 2. Values entered in this field are superseded by the charter generated at filing.
>
> **Field 3 — Proposed meeting cadence.** Enter any value. The value is ceremonial.
>
> **Field 4 — Signature of the last person to leave the room.** If no room has been reserved, any room will do. See note 4.
>
> **Note 4.** There is no note 4. The room will do.

## 4. Revision History

| Revision | Change | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| v1 | Paper original; required a notarized statement of purpose | Completion rate low; purposes notarized: 31; purposes surviving first charter: 0 |
| v2 | Purpose field made optional after review found no entered purpose had ever been consulted again | Completion rate rose to its present level |
| v3 | Fee removed; filing moved to elapsed time; purpose field retained as optional out of respect for the two filers who still use it | Current |

Revision 2's review was conducted by the Office of Fellowship Petitions. It is the only action the Office is recorded as having taken. The record does not describe how it was taken.

## 5. Classification

Filed on the [[reference/FREF-0815-MAP|Managed Absence Spine]] as LCGU: authoritative in its corner, invisible elsewhere. The form is the sole recognition authority for fellowships and is unknown outside the filing room. Most entities chartered on it are unaware of the instrument. Their charters cite it correctly.

The best-documented product of the form is the [[lorelog/LLG-0940-CORPORATE-FELLOWSHIP|Corporate Fellowship]], whose charter is the instrument's model output: a structured venue, requiring nothing of its members, commemorative by design.

## Filing Note

The form was misfiled under Catering at intake, on the strength of the recurring events its products list in their charters. The Catering desk reports that none of the events in question have been catered. It has asked to keep the file, as the closest thing to a reservation system it possesses.
