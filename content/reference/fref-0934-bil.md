The ledger records the arrival of generosity, not its fulfillment. A row marked `Cleared` indicates the attached conditions were read; it does not indicate the conditions were met, metable, or fully understood by any living desk.

## Filing Rules

1. One row per received gift, bequest, or unsolicited item of value. Value is determined by the Development Window and is not subject to appeal.
2. Attached Conditions are transcribed verbatim. Editorial clarification is a form of gratitude and is therefore regulated.
3. Custody names the desk or officer holding the item. Custody is not endorsement.
4. Status is `Received`, `Held`, `Escrowed`, `Cleared`, `Contested`, or `Reverted`.
5. A gift's conditions outlive the gift. The ledger does not track which outlives which; it notes only that both arrived together.

## Living Document Protocol

- Rows append at the bottom. A gift's conditions may evolve; the row does not.
- Returned gifts are recorded, not removed. The archive retains the record of the return and, where possible, the return itself.
- Disputes over stated intent are referred to the [[reference/FREF-0942-FPS|Fee and Penalty Schedule]] for assessment before being respectfully ignored.
