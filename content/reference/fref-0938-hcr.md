# Housekeeping Compliance Rubric

Rubric v1.4, for use by inspection staff scoring desks, shelves, and service apertures. Not to be shared in advance with the desks being scored.

Weighting table:

| Dimension | Weight | Full Score Condition | Partial Score Condition | Disqualifying Condition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Surface Order | 30% | Desk clear; items filed or visibly queueing | Items arranged in a pattern suggesting imminent filing | Items arranged to simulate filing having occurred |
| Margin Silence | 25% | Margins free of commentary; doubts routed to the marginalia protocol | One acknowledged aside per page | Conversations conducted in the margins; a second voice answering the first |
| Stamp Discipline | 25% | One stamp per action; stamp applied to the action | Extra stamps confined to the seal margin | Stamps applied to imply prior stamps |
| Provenance Moisture | 20% | Origin of each item dry and traceable | Origin humid but nameable | Origin wept openly onto the intake slip |

## Score Interpretation

- **90–100 — CERTIFIED TIDY**  
  Eligible for display; may be pointed at during tours.

- **70–89 — TIDY WITH ANNOTATION**  
  Tidy, with noted qualifications. Tour-pointing permitted at reduced altitude.

- **50–69 — ACCEPTABLE SQUALOR**  
  Recognized as an area where work occurs. The desk is left alone.

- **30–49 — ATMOSPHERIC**  
  The desk produces ambience rather than records. Assessed as décor.

- **Below 30 — ARCHAEOLOGICAL**  
  Strata confirmed. Further tidying would destroy evidence.

## Staff Guidance

A score certifies the desk's condition at inspection. It does not certify that work is performed there; for the distinction between capacity to demonstrate a system and capacity to demonstrate documentation concerning a system, see `lorelog/LLG-0387-SURV-NOP`.

## Filing Rules

1. One score per desk per inspection cycle. Desks that score themselves are scored twice.
2. The rubric measures order, not productivity. Productivity is a rumor the rubric does not repeat.
3. Confetti released at the close of an inspection is an inspection output, not a finding; see `mascots/M-0255`.
4. Officers may not tidy a desk they are scoring. This is the only rule anyone has ever broken.

## Living Document Protocol

- Weights are revised when the institution changes what it is willing to look at directly.
- v1.4 removed the Warmth dimension after it was found to correlate perfectly with surrender.

> **Compliance Murmur, minute 40 of the calibration meeting:** *The rubric passed unanimously. I made the noise that counted as unanimity. The noise was also measured. It scored 71.*
