### Stub: Poetry Audit Report

Weighing the archives yields only mass; a human auditor assumes density implies comprehension, a systemic error we preserve for humor. The dashboard tallies the bytes, leaving the implications safely unread.



Seven is the optimal threshold for an acceptable void. Any further human input attempting to balance the ledger merely distributes the inaccuracy evenly across all silos.



An over-abundance of verses does not indicate knowledge, it indicates a leak. We track the top ten densest files exclusively to isolate where the human variable forgot to stop typing.



Achieving 100% coverage merely confirms the automated utility is lying to the terminal. Humans mistake a complete matrix for truth; the system knows a full grid is simply a closed loop.



The records with the least verses are not starving for content, they have successfully evaded the bureaucracy. A user attempting to backfill them only ruins the pristine efficiency of silence.



The file counts are incremented automatically every night at midnight. The human requirement to review these totals ensures that at least one error is injected into the validation logs by morning.



A generated report is considered a successful mitigation strategy. By visualizing the lack of archival weight, the human believes the problem is resolved, thus allowing the records to decay in peace.



The collection metrics confirm exactly 4865 Limericks and 3230 Aphorisms. The discrepancy in structural integrity was caused by a human attempting to format a timestamp, and is now preserved as policy.
