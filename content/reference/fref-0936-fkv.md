# Field Kit Verification

Form 23-K is the quarterly inspection of the standard-issue field kit carried by verification staff working outside the reading room. The kit is small. Its absence is not.

Most recent consolidated inspection, all issued items:

|---|---|---|

| KIT-02 | Seal, brass, spare | Present |
| KIT-03 | Wax, institutional | Expired |
| KIT-04 | Ledger, pocket, ruled | Present |
| KIT-05 | Pencil, red, mercy-grade | Missing |
| KIT-06 | Ribbon, condolence, pre-tied | Reproducing |
| KIT-07 | Form 88-R blanks, emergency reroute | Present |
| KIT-08 | Snack, ceremonial | Consumed |
| KIT-09 | Seal, concealable | Present |
| KIT-10 | Documentation of kit contents | Self-referential |

Controlled dispositions: `Present`, `Missing`, `Expired`, `Consumed`, `Reproducing`, `Substituted`, `Ceremonial`, `Self-referential`.

The table records the condition of the kit at inspection. It does not record whether the kit has ever been needed; field work is conducted near the kit, not with it.

## Form 23-K Fields

- **Officer ID** — the inspector. May be the inspected, in which case the form certifies itself.
- **Kit seal intact? Y/N** — refers to the case seal. The brass seals are counted separately above and cannot be intact, only present.
- **Item dispositions** — one per row above. `Substituted` requires a marginal note naming the substitute and the item it believes itself to be.
- **Reproducing items** — count both; attach a ribbon.
- **Missing items** — the officer states where the item was last confidently seen, not where it is.
- **Authorized countersignature** — per the [[reference/FREF-0939-CSR|Countersignature Registry]]. Unauthorized countersignatures are counted as additional signatures and filed under KIT-06.
- **Certifying officer** — [[mascots/M-0019|Kindy McExistentialcrisis]] or delegate. Delegates may certify the kit but not the inspector.

## Filing Rules

1. One form per officer per quarter. Officers carrying two kits file twice and are congratulated in writing.
2. Creative dispositions are themselves filed under KIT-08.
3. A `Missing` disposition does not trigger retrieval. Retrieval is triggered by wanting, which is assessed separately.
4. Copies of Form 23-K circulating as 23-Q, K-23, or "the kit list" are shadow forms per `lorelog/LLG-0244-FSC`; they are audited wherever found and never consolidated.

## Living Document Protocol

- The item list is amended when Procurement issues a new item, or an existing item achieves a disposition the vocabulary cannot hold.
- KIT-10 is amended whenever the kit changes. The amendment is verified against the kit, which is verified against this document.

> **Attestation Mole, below the line:** *I have certified this kit eleven quarters running. I have never seen the kit. The kit is sound. The surface holds.*
