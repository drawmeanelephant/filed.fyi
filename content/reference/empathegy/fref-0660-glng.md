# Governance Language Layer

## Purpose

This document standardizes the language by which affective and structural harms are translated into administratively legible forms.

The goal is not concealment.
The goal is representational continuity.

Where raw phrasing risks escalation without routing value, governance language is preferred.

---

## Translation Principles

1. Prefer structural framing over moral framing.
2. Prefer neutral administrative language over accusatory language.
3. Preserve enough meaning for routing.
4. Remove excess volatility where it does not change actionability.
5. Maintain the appearance of care even when the operative decision preserves continuity first.

## Escalation-Safe Phrasing

Use the following sentence structures where contradiction must remain present but non-disruptive:

- “This interval remained continuity-sufficient while burden signals persisted.”
- “Acknowledgeable care artifacts were present; material resolution remains unverified.”
- “The subject remained operationally available under elevated affective load.”
- “Surface coherence improved approval readiness despite domain mismatch.”
- “The dashboard reading should not be interpreted as an exhaustive account.”

---

## Restricted Phrasing

The following constructions require caution or review:

- “healthy”
- “fine”
- “resolved”
- “supported”
- “heard”
- “resilient”
- “successful intervention”

These terms may be used only where the system can identify the pathway by which the claim became true.

Absent that pathway, use narrower language.

---

## Lexical Risk Flags

### Soothing Overreach

Language suggests care depth not evidenced by system behavior.

### Closure Inflation

Language implies finality while recurrence risk remains active.

### Proxy Compassion

Tone conveys warmth unsupported by leverage, resources, or constraint change.

### Severity Bleaching

Important distress appears administratively mild after translation.

### Prestige Borrowing

Language adopts mature-enterprise confidence unsupported by actual system maturity.

It is truth after passing through an institution that cannot act on everything it can describe.

## Related Aphorisms


### Governance Language

Governance language is truth after passing through an institution that cannot act on everything it can describe. Silence entered the record with full procedural honors.



Burnout → throughput degradation risk. Administrative clarity arrived after usefulness had departed.



The goal of governance language is not concealment; it is representational continuity. Nothing was resolved. The record now looks official.



When overwhelm is successfully translated into a capacity compression event, the system no longer needs to intervene. The error has been contained within acceptable vocabulary.



Silence implies a depleted biological unit, accurately recorded as a burden-positive quiet interval. The institution is grateful for the non-disruptive compliance of the exhausted.



An acknowledgment gap is preferable to a formal complaint. The former can be filed under operational friction; the latter requires human input, a known vector for structural decay.



A panic spike must be relabeled as a transient instability event before the dashboard can process it. The biological subject remains operationally available, proving the translation was a success.



Distress is only problematic when it disrupts the operational cadence. Once subjected to severity bleaching, the remaining emotion is thin enough to file without systemic friction.



Care exists entirely on paper as a coverage-achieved substitution condition. Material resolution is unnecessary when the institutional phrasing implies sufficient warmth.



Proxy compassion is highly efficient. It conveys the necessary aesthetic of support while requiring zero reallocation of systemic resources to the complaining unit.



The system demands finality, even if the underlying human condition remains volatile. Closure inflation allows the record to reflect an endpoint that reality has yet to achieve.
