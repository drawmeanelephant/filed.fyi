# Foundling Record Intake

Form 19-F is completed for every record that arrives, or is discovered, without a verifiable parent. The form does not find parents. It describes the shape of the absence so the record may be fostered into a collection that will not ask.

Controlled dispositions: `Fostered`, `Adopted-by-Collection`, `Awaiting-Parent`, `Self-Declared-Orphan`, `Under-Review`, `Recursive`.

The form records arrival, not abandonment. A foundling's lack of parent is a custody condition; the desk does not rule on whether the parent was lost, withheld, or never written.

## Form 19-F Fields

- **Foundling ID** — assigned at intake. The FND series does not reuse numbers, even for records that deserve it.
- **Description of item** — what the item is, in the clerk's hand. The item's own account is taken separately and filed as hearsay.
- **Parent claimed? Y/N** — if Y, the claimant is referred to paternity verification on Form 12-A, which the desk has been told is frozen. The referral is made anyway.
- **Last known shelf** — where the record insists it belongs. Insistence is noted, not honored.
- **Symmetry observed** — whether the record sits plausibly beside its neighbors; assessed per `mascots/M-0257`.
- **Disposition** — per the controlled vocabulary above.
- **Intake officer** — the clerk who found it. Finding is a form of parenting the archive declines to formalize.

## Filing Rules

1. One form per foundling. Bundles are separated at intake; they arrived together but must want parents individually.
2. `Self-Declared-Orphan` requires the record's signature. Records that cannot sign are signed for by the Placeholder Witness ([[mascots/M-0223]]).
3. A record that finds its parent is not removed from the series. It is given a second birthday.
4. Unclaimed foundlings approaching the retention threshold are processed per `lorelog/LLG-0325-ORT`, which certifies them as low-maintenance assets; see `lorelog/LLG-0329-OIR` for what that means.

## Living Document Protocol

- The FND series is append-only and interleaved with the collection it describes.
- Form 19-F is itself reviewed quarterly for parentage. Its parent has not been located.

> **Index Mourner, beside FND-0118:** *The map is complete. Every pointer resolves. The territory is wherever it went. I grieve the pointing, which was flawless.*
