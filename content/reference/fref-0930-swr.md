# Seal Weighting Rubric — Residual Surface Scoring v2.0

### Residual Surface Scoring Rubric v2.0

For use by Verification Office staff when a mark of trust is presented
for grading. Do not circulate to custodial desks in advance; desks
prepare.

---

| Dimension | Weight | Full Score Condition | Partial Score Condition | Disqualifying Condition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Proof Basis** | 35% | verification chain inspectable to origin | chain terminates in inherited basis | chain asserted, never produced; custodian points to another desk |
| **Behavioral Pull** | 25% | mark guides behavior because proof does | mark guides behavior while proof sleeps | mark is decorative; nothing downstream defers to it |
| **Succession Depth** | 25% | seal passed hand to hand with a custody log | current custodian inherited the desk, not the proof | seal found already present at handoff; no first holder on file |
| **Removal Cost** | 15% | withdrawal would govern something | withdrawal is governance-heavy | removal unthinkable; the question corrupts the form it is written on |

---

### Score interpretation

- **90–100 — ACTIVE TRUST**  
  Surface verified to origin. File normally; inspect on schedule
  anyway.

- **70–89 — RESIDUAL TRUST OBJECT**  
  Visible, behavior-shaping, semantically thinned. Most marks score
  here. This is the office's quiet finding.

- **50–69 — INHERITED BASIS SURFACE**  
  Still rendering from prior proof. Schedule a custody check before
  the next handoff, whether or not a handoff is expected.

- **30–49 — CEREMONIAL CONTINUITY MARK**  
  Removal is governance-heavy. The mark stays; the file notes why.

- **Below 30 — VERIFICATION COLLAPSE**  
  The mark functions. The basis cannot be asked, because there is no
  one left to ask it of.

---

### Staff guidance on Verification Collapse

A collapse score is not a ruling that the mark is counterfeit, or
that the office's own marks would grade higher.

**It is a ruling that the proof chain ends inside the office doing
the grading.**

The desk files the score, files the surface's continuing effect, and
does not collapse either record into the other. Graded outcomes feed
the [[reference/FREF-0926-TSI|Trust Surface Inspection Log]].
