A form's row states where the form sleeps, not who is responsible for it. The index has never successfully named who is responsible.

## Filing Rules

1. State is one of the four catalog states — `CAAR`, `LCGU`, `STCP`, `AAOA` — assigned per `reference/FREF-0020-MAPS`, or `active variant` where a live form holds managed ancestry.
2. Last Observed records a sighting, not a service level. A form last observed on a ritual slide may be required tomorrow.
3. Custody Note names where the form sleeps. It does not name who owns it.
4. Witness carries a record ID only. No prose.
5. A form that feels simultaneously required, deprecated, and made up belongs here. That condition is the filing criterion.

## Maintenance Note

- Entries are additive. A holding's state may change; its row does not. State changes are appended as new holdings per the intake log.
- This index is not a complaint ledger, a service catalog, or a promise that a form can be produced on request.
- Do not reconcile the index against the catalog. The catalog says what should exist; the index says what sleeps where.

> **Registrar's note:** *The index lists absences by shape. Some of the shapes have queues. One of the shapes has a waiting room, and the waiting room has a number.*
