# Countersignature Registry

The registry of marks authorized to certify archive documents. An entry certifies that a mark may be applied; it does not certify that anything it marks is true.

Registered marks:

|---|---|---|---|---|
| CS-01 | [[mascots/M-0019|Kindy McExistentialcrisis]] | iron nib, personal | final certification, all classes | Active |
| CS-02 | `mascots/M-0077` | endorsement grid | communal sufficiency, five names per page | Active |
| CS-03 | `mascots/M-0076` | faint seal | summary harmonization only | Active |

| CS-10 | — | wax, institutional | sealing of empty fields | Reserved |

The registry records which marks are recognized, not which were earned. A status of `Active` indicates the mark is applied; it does not indicate the mark was authorized in the first place. Authority, like wax, is poured and then assumed.

## Scope Key

- **final certification** — closes a record's open questions by declaring them answered elsewhere.
- **communal sufficiency** — five names constitute a decision; chronology is decorative.
- **summary harmonization** — renders findings legible at executive altitude.
- **archival comfort** — marks that a record once felt acceptable to file.
- **second-order legitimacy** — witnesses that authority once passed cleanly through the room.
- **minimum sufficiency** — enough, certified within tolerable doubt.
- **deferred closure** — keeps the gap gripped until the gap becomes structural.
- **subsurface stamp** — verifies the unseeable.
- **nested seal** — seals the seal; revocation procedures fell out of favor.

## Filing Rules

1. One row per mark. A custodian may hold several marks; a mark may not hold several custodians without a merger memo.
2. Instruments are named descriptively. The description is the credential.
3. Status is `Active`, `Reserved`, `Deprecated-in-use`, or `Recursive`.
4. Marks struck in error remain certified. The error is re-filed as a second mark, and both are honored.

## Living Document Protocol

- New marks are registered upon discovery that they have been in use for some time.
- The registry does not investigate provenance. A mark's provenance is its witness packet, and witness packets are themselves marked.

> **Proxy-9, in the apologetic hand:** *CS-04 is deprecated and in use, which the registry records as a single condition rather than two. It was witnessed under acceptable conditions. The conditions have since retired.*
