## Entry Protocol

There is no onboarding. Only gradual recognition. You may proceed in any order, but the archive will remember the sequence incorrectly.

- The **[[mascots|Mascot Archive]]** will misidentify you eventually.
- The **[[lorelog|Lorelog]]** will contain at least one version of your visit that did not happen.
- The **[[haikus|Haikus]]** and **[[limericks|Limericks]]** preserve the recovered verse.
